# Act III: The Frostmaiden

Scene scripts for Chapters 11–13, the endings, and the postgame outline.

## Chapter 11: The Frost Saint Walks

**Chapter goal:** none at first; it's a celebration. Then Auril moves, and the goal is to get Frostina back.

### SC 11.01 — The wheel and the ones who bind it

- **Map:** 2053 Rooted Hearth, night of the Vote. **Trigger:** rest. **Present:** party, Hellen, Tamsin, the new councillors. **Music:** *Hearth*, then *Frostmaiden*.

Pine-mead. Someone's playing the bard's song about the Heralds, badly.

Hellen has added a verse about the Dagger Reborn. She keeps having to stop.

**ALERION** *(to Hellen)* The verse is good. Keep going.

**MEAT MEAT** Meat Meat has had four coffees. Meat Meat can hear colours.

**REPTIN** *(to the room)* She means she's happy. Mostly.

Alberica sets down her cup. The rune on her chest is glowing through her shirt.

The room goes white. Only Alberica can move.

A woman in frost-furs is sitting across the table. Nobody else can see her.

**AURIL** Hear the wheel, and the ones who bind it.

**AURIL** The dark half guards silence. The light half births flame.

**AURIL** And flame consumes as easily as it warms. The spark is spent.

Her breath frosts the table between you.

**AURIL** Life and death march only as long as they move.

**AURIL** And memory keeps the key. Keys open, Alberica. They also lock.

She leans in.

**AURIL** You are my vessel, little songbird. You always were.

**AURIL** Come and be beautiful forever.

The room comes back.

Alberica is on her knees. Meat Meat is holding her hands.

**ALBERICA** *(hoarse)* She's coming. No. She's already here.

`[herald: Alberica, quiet]` **ALBERICA** *(hoarse)* ...Something's pulling me north. Don't make a thing of it.

**MEAT MEAT** Meat Meat is making a thing of it.

Far above, at the crown of the tree, something screams.

A dragon.

- **Flow:** sets `auril_whisper`, then runs straight on to the Shrine (11.02). `[herald: Alberica]` the narration says "you"; Meat Meat holds your hands.

### SC 11.02 — The Frost Saint walks

- **Map:** 2061 Canopy, the Shrine of the Twinned Dragons, night. **Trigger:** follows 11.01; run to the Shrine. **Present:** party (Alerion, Frostina's Dragoon; Meat Meat, Razzilith's); Frostina; Razzilith; Matron Eline; Halo; Sephek; Iskra (Auril's roc). **Music:** *Frostmaiden*, then boss.

The Shrine is full of Halo robes.

On the dragons' altar lies Sephek's frozen body. The Halo carried it up while the city voted.

Eline holds a blue light in her hands. It's beating: the frost-light heart.

**ELINE** When your engine died, the heart fell free. We were waiting.

**ELINE** We are very patient.

She presses it into the hollow in Sephek's chest.

The ice cracks. Sephek breathes in, four hundred years late.

His eyes open, blue as a frozen lake.

**SEPHEK** *(very quietly)* ...It's cold. Why is it still so cold?

**ELINE** *(kneeling)* The Frost Saint walks again.

Alerion got here first. He always hears her first.

He's standing between them and Frostina, his ring white with frost.

**ALERION** You don't get her twice.

**MEAT MEAT** *(both arms around Razzilith's neck, holding him back)* Not yet, Razzy. Not yet.

`[herald: Meat Meat]` (Both arms around Razzilith's neck. He wants to go through them. So do you.)

**ELINE and SEPHEK** *(together, as if one were the other's echo)* Stand back from the Saint.

- **Battle:** Brother Ansel of the Halo, then Eline and Sephek together (a double battle if you have two Pokémon standing). Win or lose, the scene goes on, and your Pokémon are healed after.

`[won]` Eline goes down on one knee. Sephek doesn't move at all. He doesn't need to.

`[lost]` The cold comes off Sephek in a wave and puts every Pokémon on the Shrine on the ground.

The sky above the Canopy tears open.

Through the thinned Wall, a vast white roc drops out of the night, aurora trailing from her wingtips.

Iskra.

Sephek raises one hand. Ice blooms around Frostina: a crystal, not a cage.

She goes still inside it, her eyes still open.

**SEPHEK** *(to Alerion, and he means it)* I'm sorry. I'm taking her home.

**SEPHEK** She was always going home.

**ALERION** *(very quietly)* She's not hers.

Iskra takes the crystal in her talons. Sephek and Eline climb onto her back.

The roc turns north, toward the Sea of Moving Ice, and is gone.

Alerion is lying in the snow on the Shrine steps, a hand pressed to his chest.

Where Frostina's second heartbeat was.

Razzilith is roaring at the sky, and Meat Meat is roaring with him.

`[herald: Alerion]` You're lying in the snow on the Shrine steps, a hand pressed to your chest. Where her second heartbeat was. It's so faint now.

**ALERION** *(not getting up yet)* ...She's still beating. Far. North.

**ALERION** A Dragoon can follow that.

**ALBERICA** *(kneeling, a hand on his back)* Then up you get, pretty boy. Slowly.

- **Flow:** sets `frostina_taken`, `sephek_risen`. Alerion is hurt but stays in the party; the Dragoon bond still beats, faintly, and points north.

## Chapter 13: Grimskalle

**Chapter goal:** free Frostina and face Auril. The Four Tests are this game's Elite Four: four chambers in any order, each with a scenario and a keeper battle, no healing between them except items. Each Herald breaks one test by refusing its tenet. Every broken test weakens one stage of the final battle.

### SC 13.01 — The Hall of the Four Winds

- **Map:** new Hall of the Four Winds. **Trigger:** enter. **Present:** party; four tablets of ice; Sephek's voice. **Music:** *Frostmaiden*.

A round hall with four doors, and four tablets of ice.

Each is carved with one word. CRUELTY. ENDURANCE. ISOLATION. PRESERVATION.

**SEPHEK** *(his voice from everywhere at once)* Her tenets. Pass her tests, and she'll hear you.

**SEPHEK** Fail, and you'll stay here. And be beautiful.

**ALBERICA** And if we don't want to pass them her way?

`[herald: Alberica, quiet]` **ALBERICA** ...What if we pass them ours?

**SEPHEK** *(after a long pause, almost curious)* ...No one has ever asked that.

**MEAT MEAT** Meat Meat will pass the tests. Then Meat Meat will bite the tests.

**ALERION** That's not how tests work.

**MEAT MEAT** It is now.

- **Flow:** sets `hall_entered`. The four doors open; each Herald goes with you into one test. If that Herald isn't with you, their lines become your own voice: *(Your own voice, where theirs should be: ...)*. When you play that Herald, their key line in the test is your choice of a loud or a quiet version.

## Postgame outline: The White Vault

**Hook:** After the credits, Mira's daughter brings you a shard of mirror that won't stop whispering. Avarice's last fragment escaped into the hag's glass in SC 8.08. It has been travelling through every mirror in the Dale toward "the White Womb": the buried Netherese city of Ythryn under the Reghed Glacier.

**Beats (outline only; not scripted yet):**

1. **Vellynne Harpell** arrives in Rootspire with the *Codicil of White*. She needs the Heralds to open the glacier, and she has her own reasons.
2. **The Caves of Hunger:** read the Codicil's poem at the glacier wall and it splits open. Tekeli-li, the gnoll vampire, stalks you through ice caves that replay the past.
3. **Ythryn:** a tilted city in a cavern of ice, lit green and purple. Eight towers, one per school of magic: a battle-tower challenge.
4. **The night hags:** the Aunties charge one memory each to pass. Alberica pays hers.
5. **Avarice's last shard,** in a crystal body above the mythallar: the true final boss.
6. **The obelisk:** a secret super-boss that rewinds time to Ythryn's last spring.

**Payoffs:** Garagai's axe-head goes back to Norsu; the silver egg from Iskra's nest hatches into a postgame dragon partner (a sibling for Frostina). Glimmer does not come back; his death at the Forge is final.
