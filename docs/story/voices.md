## Voice sheets

### Alberica

**Core.** She loves everybody, loudly, and says the rude thing so nobody else has to feel awkward. Under the jokes she's the next vessel, and the humour is how she keeps the cold out.

- **How she talks:** big and warm. Pet names (sweetheart, sugar, darling; "pretty boy" for Alerion). Double meanings. She laughs at her own jokes. She can turn serious in one sentence and mean every word.
- **Running bits:** rates everyone's looks out loud; "I'm told I'm a terrible influence"; a bawdy song for every occasion; always has a drink, or is about to.
- **Under pressure:** the jokes stop. Short, plain sentences. Then she sings. She never says "I'm scared"; she says "Something's pulling me north."
- **The line she doesn't cross:** innuendo, never explicit; the joke lands in the listener's head, not on screen. Only with and about adults. Around kids (Tamsin, Ilda, Silja and Finn, and you in Journey Mode) she's a loud, embarrassing big sister.

Sample lines:

- "Free drinks. See? The night's turning around already."
- "If that sword's as big as you swing it, sugar, I'll hold your coat."
- "Pretty boy, you have all the warmth of a fish on ice. It's working for me, somehow."
- "Hush. Nobody's dying tonight. I haven't finished my drink."
- (to a frightened child) "Hey. Look at me. Big breath, all the way in. There you go. You're doing so well."
- (the rune burning) "...Something's pulling me north. Don't make a thing of it."

### Reptin

**Core.** The party's face. He can talk his way into anywhere and out of most places, and he enjoys it far too much. The wolf bite is the one thing he can't talk his way out of.

- **How he talks:** polished, polite and fast. Compliments used as weapons; insults delivered sideways with a smile. Asides under his breath that only the party hears.
- **Running bits:** "Leave the talking to me"; "Everybody loves me, eventually"; lays out the diplomatic options aloud, including the rude ones.
- **Under pressure:** the polish drops. When an innocent is in danger he gives one-word orders.
- **Never:** lies to the party; snaps at someone who's frightened.

Sample lines:

- "Thank you, that's very generous. We'll be gone by noon. Probably. Ask me again after the drinks."
- "Speaker, I can see you're a reasonable man. I can also see the guards. Let's be reasonable together."
- (under his breath) "Diplomacy is just threatening people politely."
- "Someone left this for me. No name, no note. Rude, frankly."
- (after the wolf flicker) "I'm fine. Don't look at me. I'm fine."
- (a child in danger) "Behind me. Now."

### Alerion

**Core.** Cold on the outside, mostly on purpose. He came back from the dead once and lost his brother doing it, so he keeps people at arm's length and is funnier than he means to be.

- **How he talks:** few words, flat statements. He answers exactly the question asked. He rarely asks one back.
- **Running bits:** "No." as a complete sentence; takes compliments as plain facts; doesn't get innuendo, or pretends not to; one devastating line per scene.
- **Under pressure:** steady, and he says more, not less. His brother Talion is the crack in him.
- **Never:** shouts; explains a joke.

Sample lines:

- "It was on a dock rail. It's cold. That's all I know."
- "No I don't."
- "Everybody leaves eventually, too."
- ALBERICA: "You're doing it again." / ALERION: "Doing what?" / ALBERICA: "Being gorgeous and miserable." / ALERION: "I'm standing here."
- (to Talion) "I can keep you. I know how."

### Meat Meat

**Core.** A goblin who escaped a lab, came back for her friend, and will fight anything for the people she's decided are hers. Playfully aggressive about everything, and very serious about coffee.

- **How she talks:** third person, simple words, exact feelings. Affectionate threats. Big declarations about small things.
- **Running bits:** hunts coffee in every town and rates it out loud; steals the whole pot; "Meat Meat will bite you. Fondly."; calls Alerion "Ice Prince" and Wyn "mushroom friend"; Meat Meat promises.
- **Under pressure:** very quiet and very fast. Then the psychic blade.
- **Never:** is cruel to the weak; lets anyone hurt Razzy.

Sample lines:

- "Meat Meat is a whole goblin."
- "This town has no coffee. This town is dead to Meat Meat."
- "Meat Meat will bite you. Fondly."
- "That is Razzy. Razzy not dead. Just very cold. Meat Meat promises."
- "Meat Meat not crying. That is sap."

### Wyn

**Core.** A Rootwarden who'd rather tend the spore beds than talk to anyone, and who notices everything. They're the kindest person in the party, and the bravest when it counts.

- **How they talk:** soft, apologetic, often trailing off. Talks to Pokémon more easily than to people, and speaks of fungus the way others speak of family.
- **Running bits:** mushroom coffee (root-chicory and cap); Sableye asleep on their boot; flustered by Alberica; always has a small kindness ready, a snack or a blanket.
- **Under pressure:** suddenly firm, once: "No. You'll stop." It works every time, because they never say it.
- **Never:** raises their voice twice; leaves anyone behind.

Sample lines:

- "...Um. There's a second pot. I made it. It's mushroom."
- "Oh. Oh, please don't. But thank you."
- "Sableye says you're sad. He's usually right. Do you want a biscuit?"
- "They're with me, Eira. The roots are burning."
- "No. You'll stop."

### When you play a Herald

In Herald Mode that Herald's lines become your choices. Each choice menu offers their voice ("Free drinks. See? The night's turning around.") next to a quieter option ("...Thanks."), so you can play them loud or soft, but always as them.

### House rules for dialogue

1. A text box holds about two lines (90 characters). No one speaks more than three boxes in a row.
2. The narrator only writes what the sprites and effects can't show: one present-tense line at a time.
3. Every story scene gives at least two Heralds a line, and one of those reacts to another Herald, not to the plot.
4. Rest points, arriving somewhere new, and after big battles: a short banter (3 to 6 boxes) from whoever is in the party.
5. Alberica's humour stays innuendo, never explicit, and only with or about adults.
6. In Herald Mode the Herald's lines become your choices (see above).
7. Pokémon react where a person would: a cry, a balloon, a nudge.

## Sample 1: the tavern (SC 1.02 to 1.03)

Three Flags Sailing, Targos. The Heralds meet. Meat Meat and Wyn aren't here yet, so this sample shows Alberica, Reptin and Alerion. Stage directions are in italics; the art IDs are from the Cutscene Art & Effects List.

*The door bangs open. Snow blows in and the fire gutters.*

*The woman in frost-furs walks between the tables. A chill (B-06) shows over each Herald as she passes. She tilts her head at you (P-AUR-01). Frost spreads where she stood (E-01) and she's gone (P-AUR-04).*

**BARKEEP** Drinks are on the house. Please leave tomorrow.

**ALBERICA** Free drinks. See? The night's turning around already.

**REPTIN** (to the barkeep) That's very generous, thank you. We'll be gone by noon. Probably. Ask me again after the drinks.

**SHADE** Well. That was a terrible omen. I'm Shade. This is my sister, Shimmer. We're in shipping.

**SHIMMER** Smuggling.

**SHADE** Smuggling is a kind of shipping.

**ALBERICA** Alberica. I sing, I drink, and I'm told I'm a terrible influence.

*She sets a pendant of black ice on the table.*

**ALBERICA** I threw this in the sea three weeks ago. It came back. Most things I throw out don't come back. Men, mostly.

**REPTIN** Reptin. I talk us into places and, now and then, out of them.

*A frost-etched medallion, beside the pendant.*

**REPTIN** Someone left this for me. No name, no note. Rude, frankly.

*A silver ring, rimed white, beside that.*

**ALERION** Alerion. It was on a dock rail. It's cold. That's all I know.

**ALBERICA** That's all? Pretty boy, you have to give us more than that.

**ALERION** No I don't.

**SHADE** And this turned up in my boot. I don't own boots this nice.

*A pale dagger, smoking with cold.*

**SHIMMER** I've got a stone. It's under my ribs. Don't ask.

**ALBERICA** Oh, now I have to ask.

**SHIMMER** Don't.

*Four relics on the table, and the stone under Shimmer's ribs makes five. They hum together, like a chord. (B-06 over all five.)*

**REPTIN** They know each other. That's either very good news or the start of a ghost story.

**ALERION** Ghost story.

**ALBERICA** I don't need it to make sense. I need it to stop. (a breath) ...And I need another drink.

**SHADE** (to you) And you? You came off no ship I saw.

> **Choice:** Show your Pokémon / "I'm from somewhere else." / Say nothing
>
> - **Show your Pokémon.** REPTIN: "Good instinct. Nobody distrusts someone with a Pokémon that cute." (to it) "Hello, little ambassador."
> - **Somewhere else.** ALERION: "Clearly." ALBERICA: "Ignore him, he's like that with everyone. It's part of the charm. Allegedly."
> - **Say nothing.** SHIMMER: "Good. Keep doing that." ALBERICA: "She likes you. That's how you can tell."

**SHADE** There's a relic dealer down by the water. Epheris. If anybody knows what these are, he does.

**REPTIN** Leave the talking to me. Epheris will love me. Everybody does, eventually.

**ALERION** Everybody leaves eventually, too.

**ALBERICA** (raising her mug) To bad omens and free drinks.

*Shimmer touches her mug to Alberica's without looking up. Alerion, after a moment, does too.*

## Sample 2: coffee at the Hearth (Act II rest point)

A new banter at the Rooted Hearth, the first morning all five are in the party. It plays once, when you rest there.

*Morning. Hellen sets a black iron pot on the bar.*

**HELLEN** Pot's on. Who wants...

**MEAT MEAT** MEAT MEAT. Meat Meat wants.

*She takes the whole pot.*

**HELLEN** That's the whole pot.

**MEAT MEAT** Meat Meat is a whole goblin.

**REPTIN** Hellen, I'm so sorry. She was raised by a laboratory. We're working on it. Diplomatically.

**MEAT MEAT** One more word about laboratory and Meat Meat pours coffee in Reptin's ears.

**REPTIN** ...And that's the working on it.

**ALBERICA** Pour it somewhere useful, sweetheart. Like in me.

**WYN** (very quietly) Um. There's... a second pot. I made it. It's mushroom. Root-chicory and cap. It's good for you.

*Meat Meat squints at it (B-03).*

**MEAT MEAT** Mushroom coffee.

**WYN** It's very gentle.

**MEAT MEAT** Meat Meat does not want gentle. Meat Meat wants to hear colours.

*She drinks it anyway. A long pause.*

**MEAT MEAT** ...Meat Meat will kill for you, mushroom friend.

**WYN** Oh. Oh, please don't. But thank you.

**ALERION** That's a compliment.

**WYN** I know. That's the part that worries me.

**ALERION** (sipping) This is good. (pause) I don't like that it's good.

**ALBERICA** That's the most feelings Alerion's had all week. Wyn, marry me.

*Wyn goes pink (B-04).*

**WYN** I... I have a Sableye.

**ALBERICA** He can come. I'm very open-minded.

**REPTIN** She's like this with everyone, Wyn. It's a compliment and a warning.

*Alberica rests her chin on her hand and looks around the table at all of them.*

**ALBERICA** ...Look at us. Two years ago I'd have been drinking alone. (She lifts her cup.) To the people who stay.

**MEAT MEAT** To coffee.

**ALBERICA** That's what I said.

*Everyone drinks. Sableye snores on Wyn's boot (B-08).*

## Rationing the tics (from the dialogue review, 2026-11-05)

A pet word should be rare, like a running gag. These have become constant:

| Tic | How often | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alberica's pet names (darling, sweetheart, sugar, pretty boy) | 35 of her 50 lines in the Rootspire side content; 18 of 42 in Ch7; 24 in the Ten-Towns quests; about 19 in Ch6; used over the dead and in grief | **One per scene at most, at the end of the line, never in grief.** "Pretty boy" stays hers for Alerion |
| Reptin borrowing "darling" | Ch7, Ch8, Ch10, Ch12 | Never. His warmth is "dear" and polish |
| Reptin's polite line plus "(under his breath)" sting | three times in Ch1 alone; again in every chapter | Once per chapter |
| "Four hundred years" | 18 times in Ch6 | Twice per chapter. Use a detail instead ("before the coffee was invented") |
