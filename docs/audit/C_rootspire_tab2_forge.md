**Overall judgement.** The slice splits into two very different halves, and the adaptation treats them very differently.

- **The festival and the Forge's main line (≈5388–6760) are adapted faithfully but heavily compressed.** Almost every *headline* beat is in the game, often with the source's own words. These include the festival's "Tonight, Rootspire remembers", the scorched Summer Throne with warm sap under it, the single tremor, and the aurora pulsing "like something built". Then come Kelyn's "something is waking below" summons and the passphrase "Rimebound. Rise unbroken." The enthralled duergar say "the failures always return". The flight-path illusion is there, and so is the hag's echo at a tea table ("the children come back to mother's kitchen… burn her forge and I'll whisper you a secret"). The Raz-MKIII journal and "the goblin survived" are there, as are the barbed-devil priest, Avarice's "my little failures", her escape through a mirror with "We'll speak again in the White Womb, little failures", and the dragon smashing up through the roots. The structure is the shortcut. The source's 3 levels and 37 rooms become 5 maps (`tools/build_ch8.rb`) with about 10 interactive beats. About 20 rooms are gone entirely. Most are combat rooms (sap golems, umber hulk, rust monsters, crossbow galleries, mech guards). Several cut rooms carry story, though: the traitor duergar, Avarice's own quarters and mural, the tortured myconid, the mind-flayer corpse, the mirror hall, the broken barracks, the tortured captain and the treasure vault with its personal relics. The game also brings in large changes that are *not* on the approved list. The Summer Marshal becomes Var (he), whose body Avarice has worn for 30 years as "a coat". Avarice is disguised, not bare-faced. The engine's heart is Sephek's rather than a stolen dragon heart. Avarice's goal becomes "endless summer as a god's worth of power" rather than a soul-harvest to supplant Auril and merge the Dale with the Nine Hells. These may come from other source tabs. Against this slice, they are rewrites.
- **The downtime half (3809–5386, nearly half the slice) is mostly missing.** Reptin's whole Rootwarden training arc with Arvik, ending in the Loop-Band, is absent: Arvik does not exist in the game. So are Alerion's six days of library research (only the "Dragon Warriors / wyrms need a guide" finding survives), Alberica's Memory Codex fragments, and most of Meat Meat's undercity walk and summer-imbalance study. Glimmer's seven-day Brightmane conversion with Lumin is replaced by the approved mirror-at-the-Rift scene. That replacement is fine, but the Brightmane Q&A went with it, including the *chardalyn-dragon vision*, which is the slice's main foreshadowing of the Forge. Some rumours were salvaged, scattered as NPC lines: the mirrors that watch back, the possessed child, Lirielle's burning-root dreams, the forged decree, missing Rootwardens and wyrmling shapes under the Glasswater.

Honest bottom line: for this slice the game is **plot-complete but texture-poor**. A player gets the Forge's spine and its best lines. A player who knew the campaign would notice Reptin's training, Alerion's research, Alberica's codex and Meat Meat's Razzilith flight are gone, and that most of the dungeon's horror set-pieces were cut. The owner's worry that shortcuts were taken is justified for the downtime and the dungeon's room count, and not justified for the festival and the Forge's climax.

## 2. Biggest gaps (ranked)

1. **Reptin's Rootwarden training with Arvik (Days 2–7) and the Rootwarden's Loop-Band** (4005–4199): Missing. This is Reptin's entire downtime arc. It covers the beatings and black eye, Arvik's returning faith, the Quiet Step, "The tree has begun to trust your weight", Reptin teaching Arvik in return, and the Loop-Band gift with the legacy speech. *Suggestion:* make Arvik a Rootwarden at Rootwarden Hall or the Split Dock, with a short trial chain (balance gauntlet, silent circuit) that ends in a Loop-Band key item. It could hang off Rheven's lines in `026_Rootspire.rb:190`.
2. **Glimmer's questions to Brightmane and the chardalyn-dragon vision** (3963–4001): Missing. The answers ("Power and Stasis… a spark of hope leads to growth", "Rely on the strength of those who give you hope… give it back") and the vision of a "Chard…alyn" dragon erupting from the World Tree are lost. Only "Brightmane said I'd know the fire when I saw it" (`034_Chapter8.rb:309`) hints at it. *Suggestion:* add three Q&A lines and the vision to `glimmer_arrives` in `029_Glimmer.rb:121`, as what Brightmane told him over his "seven years".
3. **Avarice's motive and identity as written** (5707–5711, 6449–6457, 6773): Changed, not approved. In the source she rules openly with no disguise, uses the duergar alias "Xardorok", and is a tiefling. She wants a soul-harvest from Ten-Towns to supplant Auril and merge the Dale with the Nine Hells. In the game she wears Var as a coat and wants endless summer (`034_Chapter8.rb:333-340`). The "Xardorok" alias, the Nine Hells and the soul-harvest are all gone. *Suggestion:* have the owner confirm the Var/coat rewrite. Either way, add a line of the soul-harvest / Nine Hells ambition to her monologue at `034_Chapter8.rb:338`.
4. **Avarice's root-vault and the PC relics** (6681–6707): Missing. The journal line "Tomorrow, the dragon flies. Tonight, I will burn a path to godhood." is gone, along with the broken frost-mirror, a charred note about Meat Meat, Razzy's first green scale and a frozen rose (a Shimmer echo). *Suggestion:* add a vault event behind the engine in Heart Chamber 2076 (`tools/build_ch8.rb:130`), reachable after the engine dies. The frozen rose would land hard right after Glimmer's death.
5. **Alerion's library research, Days 2, 3, 6 and 7** (4211–4251, 4477–4537): Missing or barely touched. This covers Dougan's Hole consumed by Year 57 VR, hag mirrors fracturing the timeline, and Rootspire remembering him before his birth and after his death. It also covers the Dale before Auril. Then come the Day-7 visions: himself cloaked in Winter's power, a golden key and a frost-sealed door, a Heralds' council and frost creeping up the tree, plus "Winter returns through those who do not fear the cold." Only the Dragon Warriors finding survives (`026_Rootspire.rb:362`). *Suggestion:* add frost-glass leaf readables in the Wind-Glass Library (2062), triggered when Alerion is in the party.
6. **Alberica's Memory Codex** (5160–5322): Missing. Her four fragments are gone. In them she *anchored* the Reversal, a pale-flame figure is "hunting a timeline", the Council's missing pages show three names crossed out and a fourth left blank, and her older self stands under the Celestial Rift. (Her Ch5 Reversal vision, `023_Chapter5.rb:710`, is a faint echo of fragment one.) *Suggestion:* add a Hearth morning scene in `024_Chapter6.rb` near `hearth_coffee`, or a codex item that fills in as you rest.
7. **Meat Meat and Razzilith: what Summer is, and the first flight** (4543–4681): Missing. Razzilith's warning is cut ("No one sees Summer… Summer is female… no one ever sees Summer's face… Autumn hides it… Summer missing, wrong things grow"). So is her first ride, as the first rider he has ever allowed. In the game she first rides him in Ch9 (`035_Chapter9.rb:127`). *Suggestion:* add a Shrine of the Twinned Dragons scene after `twin_orb` in `026_Rootspire.rb:849`. It would also foreshadow the Summer reveal far better than the attendant alone does (`033_Chapter7B.rb:347`).
8. **The traitor duergar and the tortured duergar captain** (5966–5982, 6632–6647): Missing. The two duergar who beg "Kill her… before she burns the Dale" / "or she will kill the seasons" are cut, along with Avarice's "Great Unchaining" and the final tactical hints. *Suggestion:* add one sympathetic duergar NPC on the Barricade (2072) who opens the gate as an alternative to the passphrase.
9. **Area 19: a tortured myconid begging "Please… let me die"** (6295–6319): Missing. Its spores animate the root-constructs. This fits Wyn and the myconid road in the game almost perfectly. *Suggestion:* put it in the Dragonforge (2075) before the devil-priest, with a Wyn reaction.
10. **The Forge's horror set-pieces: mirror hall, Echo Hall, the future-self mirror, the broken duergar barracks** (5873–5891, 6086–6109, 6593–6606, 6611–6627): Missing. The cut lines include "She will take your heart", "Don't let her take the heart…" and the chant "Fire replaces frost…". The nearest echo is the Heartroot overlook's "SHE WILL TAKE THE HEART IF WE DON'T" (`025_Chapter7.rb:63`). *Suggestion:* add one corridor map between the Pens and the Dragonforge with 2–3 mirror readables and the chanting duergar as non-combat NPCs.
11. **Prisoners in root-cocoons and the Rootwarden's plea** (6535–6544): Compressed. The pens hold RAZ cocoons, Selrae and a child. The source's many weeping prisoners and the Rootwarden's line "She's burning the seasons away… please… stop her…" are gone. *Suggestion:* add one Rootwarden cocoon in Cocoon Pens 2074 (`tools/build_ch8.rb:75`), ideally one of Rheven's missing Bough circle.
12. **Avarice's quarters: the mural, the effigy, the "Alberica" journal line** (5940–5961): Compressed. The journal moved to the pens. Lost: the mural of Auril and a horned, winged figure locked in a storm; the mirror-root Auril effigy trap; and "The frost-born subject screamed the name 'Alberica' before expiring." *Suggestion:* add the mural and that one journal line to the Hag's Domain (2073) or the pens journal at `034_Chapter8.rb:93`.
13. **Razzy's part in the Forge** (5669–5673, 5808, 6413–6415, 6527–6530, 7075–7082): Changed, not approved. In the source Razzy refuses to sleep and claws the floor because he senses "the woman who hurt him". He growls at the roots during the ceremony, reacts to the dragon, and is the subject of the failed-prototype beasts. In the game the twins stay at the Shrine, and Razzy appears only through the RAZ-MK plates and Meat Meat's scream. The whole-party reactions to the dragon (Alerion's Winter's Revenant echoes, Alberica's memory threads, Reptin's seasonal spike) are also gone. *Suggestion:* add a Razzilith beat at festival night (`024_Chapter6.rb:420`) and party reactions in `scene_dragonforge` (`034_Chapter8.rb:303`).
14. **Glimmer's seven-day Brightmane rite with Lumin** (3815–3959): Changed (approved mechanism), but nothing of its content survives. Lost: the horn falling off and regrowing fixed, the mane-braid pendant, the four virtues (Compassion, Courage, Persistence, Dawn-Love), "Hope has found you worthy. Step from frost into dawn." and the warlock tether shattering. *Suggestion:* Glimmer could quote "Step from frost into dawn" or name the four virtues in his arrival scene (`029_Glimmer.rb:106`) or his death (`034_Chapter8.rb:381`).
15. **The GM outline's investigation chain** (5647–5667): Compressed. In the source, Rootwardens come to the door, warm scorch marks are found at the Echo Gate, a *chardalyn fragment fused with infernal runes* turns up, and Eirwen gives a private audience with early maps, the 20 Stones and a humming sealed passage. The game folds all this into one Eirwen line at the festival (`024_Chapter6.rb:430`). No chardalyn appears before the Forge. *Suggestion:* add a post-festival Library visit with Eirwen (`026_Rootspire.rb:340`) that hands over a chardalyn shard key item.
