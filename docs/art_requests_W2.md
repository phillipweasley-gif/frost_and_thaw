borrowed and what each thing should really look like. Creature designs start from the
real animal's anatomy (owner's rule). Sizes are the game's usual: overworld characters
are 4x4 walking sheets (32x48 or 64x64 frames, matching the existing BW sprites);
item icons are 48x48.
