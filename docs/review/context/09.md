# Context pass 09 · Chapter 9: Destruction's Light

What a newcomer can and can't follow in Chapter 9, judged only from what plays in 0.7.3: `traces_073/journey.txt` 8329–8700 (the Echo Vault stair to the first trial goal), the Herald traces (`herald_alberica.txt` 7760–8100, `herald_wyn.txt` 12380–12800, `herald_reptin.txt` 7950–8290), and the code (`035_Chapter9.rb`; the Tribunal in `036_Chapter10.rb` `scene_tribunal`; the omens in `048_Rootspire_E_Nights.rb`). It builds on `notes/09.md`, `story/09.md` and `dialogue/09.md` and assumes their staging fixes (the party on screen, notes E2; the flight shown with fades and panels, E3). It also assumes part 08's fixes, which several beats here pay off (the sending-stone, the Rootwardens who carry the child up, "first light").

**In one paragraph.** Chapter 9 is the best-shaped chapter in Act II, and its context gaps are mostly about *time and consequence*. A whole night passes between "two dragons are already diving" and the leaves falling, and nobody says why it isn't too late (the Wall's own rule answers it, if someone says it). The Dragoon binding explains the word but not why these two, or what the tree foretold about them (the prophecy is in an optional loot room in Chapter 8). The villain's face smiles out of a shard minutes after everyone pronounced her dead, and nobody reacts. Bryn Shander leaves its walls with no reason given. And the arrest charges the Heralds with Var's death in front of a Speaker who heard Avarice say Var had been "gone thirty years", for a death up top nobody could have seen. The Tribunal then charges Wyn with something no mode has shown.

## 1. What the player knows coming in

**New Journey** (from the log, end of Chapter 8):

- **Summer was Avarice**, wearing Var for thirty years ("Thirty years I've worn this man. Thirty years of summer."). She built an engine on Sephek's heart and Hyphora's sap, and a dragon "of black crystal and mirror glass".
- **Glimmer is gone**: he drove the Rimefang into the engine; "Tell Frostina it was a better fire. She'll know what I mean." The dagger came back gold: Dawnfang.
- **Avarice seems dead**: "a body in a ring of melted mirror-glass: white hair"; ALERION: "...Dead. Nothing in her." But she said "We will speak again in the White Womb, little failures", and if the player looked, the body's arms were bare ("Avarice's arms were covered in burned sigils. These have none."). Only that player knows.
- **The dragon is loose**: "It blasts through the cavern roof... and rises into the night: toward Ten-Towns." Its route was shown at the Barricade: "Good Mead first. Then Bryn Shander."
- **Darrus Fell, the Autumn Warden, fell into the sap** when Meat Meat chose the child over him. The child was carried; where to, the game doesn't say.
- **The time**: the forge was "tonight" only if the player opened Avarice's vault [skip]; the climb out "for a long time"; then "The gold dawn flickers." The summer has ended and "the shimmering Auroral Wall goes thin as breath."
- **The Wall's rule** (Ch6 Council, on the spine): "Inside the Wall, four hundred years. Outside it, weeks." Nobody has applied it to tonight.
- **Optional, only if played:** the tree's prophecy in the Echo Vault ("Each needs a rider who is not afraid of falling. Each will choose one." / "a third season... She must not.") and the personal visions; Avarice's vault ("Tomorrow, the dragon flies."); Vessel IX.
- **Not known on the golden path:** that Meat Meat has a mother; why Wyn left their post (the Tribunal's charge); what "carrying the myconids' spores to the surface" refers to.

**Herald Mode** (the traces): the same, plus what each Herald brings.
- **Alberica** sees the omens as her own ("(Something only you can see.)"); she has told nobody ("She hasn't told anyone any of it."). Her bargain has not been named in any mode.
- **Reptin** has heard Anduin once, in the Rift ("(Wolves, singing. And under them Anduin, your husband, calling you home.)"), and nothing else of his life before the ship.
- **Wyn** has not been told, in their own mode, that they left the Underdark door; the trace first says it at line 13335 (Kethan, *after* the Tribunal: "You left your post, Rootwarden. The night the Underdark door broke.").

### M9-1 · Why it isn't too late, and one line for Glimmer: (b) inside the leaves falling

**What's missing.** Chapter 8 ends with the dragon rising "into the night: toward Ten-Towns" and "Far above, two dragons are already diving." Then the party climbs "for a long time", dawn comes, the leaves fall, and the goal says "The dragons are waiting." A newcomer can't say what happened overnight, why the Dale isn't already burned, or why the twin dragons stopped. The game's own rule answers all three (world rule 3: inside the Wall four hundred years, outside weeks), and it's on the spine since Chapter 6, but nobody applies it (Toolbox p. 9: "what happened" includes how much time passed; Seger pp. 79–80: each scene carries the seed of the next). The banter also plays a few minutes after Glimmer died with nobody naming him (notes E23; WRITING_GUIDE §3, no joke right after grief except Alberica's).

**Where:** the market, Rootspire [2050] · **Code:** `035_Chapter9.rb` `scene_leaves_fall` (72), after "Beyond it, snowflakes that have hung in the air for four hundred years begin, slowly, to fall." · **Staging:** Alerion faces the Wall (east edge of the map, `Stage.turn_to`); Rheven walks up from the Council stair (`Stage.approach`). **Music:** the market theme, a step down.

```
[after the owner's leaves and Rheven's two lines]
 1  ALBERICA: Put it in your hair, Tamsin. Autumn's the best-dressed season. Trust me.
 2  MEAT MEAT: Meat Meat has never seen autumn.
 3  CHANGED  ALERION: (quietly) He'd have hummed at it. Badly.     (replaces "It's leaves." / "Ice Prince...")
 4  Past the valley's edge, the shimmering Auroral Wall goes thin as breath.

 6  NEW  ALERION: (watching the Wall) A night in here. Out there, a few breaths.
 7  NEW  REPTIN: Then it's barely past the Wall. Good Mead hasn't even seen it yet.

GOAL: "The Shrine of the Twinned Dragons, on the Canopy-Tier. Catch the dragon before Good Mead."
```
