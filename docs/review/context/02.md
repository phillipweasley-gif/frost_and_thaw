# Context pass 02 · Chapter 2: Lottery Night

What a newcomer can and can't follow in Chapter 2, judged only from what plays (`script/02.md`, `journey_log.txt` 549–885, the Herald trace). It builds on `notes/02.md` (whose §2 already lists sixteen first-time questions), `story/02.md` and `dialogue/02.md`, and assumes their staging fixes: the party on screen (notes E1), Razzy staged (E2), the placeholder girl gone (E3), Mishann out of the gym (E4), a fade before morning (E5).

The core problem: the chapter uses the Frostmaiden as the engine of every scene (the chasm's light, the lottery, the mark, Sephek) and never once says what she is. The player meets a goddess as a pronoun ("Her light", "owes her one"), then hears her name from someone who isn't there. Second, the chapter sends the player north, away from the one goal of their own the game has given them, and nobody notices.

## 1. What the player knows coming in

**New Journey** (log lines 1–548):

- They fell through a cracked lantern of "real Reversal glass" in Rootspire (year 400) onto a snowbound dock. The title card says "Four hundred years ago, a winter that would not end gripped Icewind Dale." Nobody in Targos has said whose winter it is.
- Five strangers, five cold relics: Alberica's pendant ("It came back"), Reptin's medallion, Alerion's ring, Shade's dagger, and Shimmer's stone "under my ribs. Don't ask." A woman in frost-furs looked at each, smiled at the player, left frost.
- Epheris: the relics are bait, pointing south-east to Dougan's Hole. The player's shard is "hag-glass... one old woman in the Dale. South-east. Dougan's Hole. Same as theirs." and "Glass that brought you can take you back. If you find the mirror it broke from." **The player's own goal.**
- Naerth: each new moon Targos, Bryn Shander and Easthaven draw a name; the drawn person "walks out onto the tundra". Name-buyers turn up frozen "by a man who doesn't feel the cold". Shimmer: "Sephek." A trapper: "The Frostmaiden's chosen... If you meet a man who doesn't feel the cold... run."
- Naerth: "A lady in a tower pays very well for cold trinkets... Pale. Books and bones."
- The goal leaving Targos: "Bryn Shander is south-east along the road, and Dougan's Hole beyond it."
- **Not on the golden path:** the Targos rest with the carved cat and "Saltmarsh was one of them" (1.07), Kip's list.

**Herald Mode** (Alberica trace; Reptin, Alerion and Meat Meat prologues in `005_NewGame.rb`): the same, minus the lantern glass and Epheris's "home" lines, plus "The Everlasting Rime has held it for two years." ## 3. Missing scenes

Format as in 03: each numbered line is one box; `[...]` is staging; **NEW** / **CHANGED** mark changes; invented material is *(suggestion)*.

### M2-1 · Razzy's scar, asked: (b) one box in 2.02

Razzy's origin is told in 3.11 ("She took Razzy's voice."), which is right, but only works if the question was asked first (Seger, p. 43: raise the question so the answer lands). With notes E2's staging (Razzy bursts out, runs to the Reptin stand-in, `pan_to(28,26)` for the glow):

```
 5  He opens his mouth. No sound comes out.
 6  NEW  REPTIN: (very quiet) Someone cut that scar. On purpose.
[pan_to the glow]
 7  He points east, at a crack in the snow glowing blue-white. Then he runs.
```

### M2-2 · "Her light": (b) two boxes in 2.03

The first scene the Frostmaiden drives. **Code:** `scene_frostlight_enter` (014:407); trigger it where Siv is in view, or `pan_to` her (notes E10).

```
 1  Blue light seeps out of the ice like breath.
 2  Two figures in white robes chip at it with bone picks.
 3  CHANGED  SIV: The Frostmaiden's light. Every crumb belongs to her. You don't.
 4  CHANGED  REPTIN: (under his breath) Aurilites. They pray to the winter, and it listens.
 5  NEW  REPTIN: Zealots with picks. My favourite.
 6  ALBERICA: Sugar, nobody belongs to anybody. Step aside.
```

Box 3 is notes Q5. Box 4 is the first of three scattered pieces that tell the player the Frostmaiden *is* the winter (Seger, p. 136: scatter it): here, the guard (M2-4), the priestess (M2-4).

### M2-3 · The horn, flagged: (b) one box in 2.04

The horn's origin is a disguised setup that 03 M3-1 pays off ("in the morning there was a horn in the snow"). The player needs only to see that it *matters* to Shimmer. **Code:** `scene_razzy_falls` (014:416).

```

[Shade lays the horn against the shell]
GOAL CHANGED: "Keep the egg warm, and find someone who knows his name. North to Bryn Shander."
```

Box 3 goes unanswered on purpose. The goal carries the egg's need into the next chapter (notes Q8). Dialogue review's alternative ("It was in the snow at Saltmarsh, the night I dug Shimmer out.") gives the answer away a chapter early; with M3-1, keep the owner's two lines.

### M2-4 · What the lottery is for, and who she is: (b) in 2.05 and 2.07
