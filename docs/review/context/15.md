# Context pass 15 · Side: Rootspire's quests and townsfolk (Act II)

What a newcomer can and can't follow in Rootspire's optional content (the downtime week, the Hearth's nights, the omens and vignettes, the Verdant Halls, the Promenade, the Root-Tier, and the 048 quests), judged from what plays in 0.7.3 (`traces_073/journey.txt` 3167-8460 and the three Herald runs) and, for the eighty per cent no run reached, from the code (`048_Rootspire_A`-`H`, `026_Rootspire.rb`). It builds on `notes/15.md` (19 editor findings, 14 first-time-player questions), `story/15.md` and `dialogue/15.md`. Some of their findings are now fixed in the code (the Act II side quests write `side_goal` lines, not `state[:goal] ||=`, except Darrus's heist; Alerion's Long Gold line no longer names Avarice; Kelyn points at the Verdant Halls); this pass checks against the code where they disagree.

The skip test runs both ways here as in part 14. **Out:** the spine's own scenes stand without this part, with one exception that matters: Chapter 10's Trial counts finished side quests as witnesses, and nobody tells the player during the week that it will. **In:** most quests make sense alone, but Act II turns the outsider rule inside out, and the side content hasn't noticed. In Act I the player was the stranger. In Rootspire the Journey player and Wyn are *home*, and the Heralds are the strangers: so the right people to have things explained to are Alberica, Reptin, Alerion and Meat Meat, and the right person to explain them is often Tamsin, Hellen, or Wyn. Instead, Rootspire's townsfolk call the Journey kid "Herald", introduce Rootwarden captains to a Rootwarden, and explain nothing to the four people from four hundred years ago.

## 1. What the player knows coming in

**New Journey** (from `journey.txt`, the fall to the end of Chapter 6's week):

- You tumbled out of the cracked lantern into Rootspire's market "an hour after you vanished" (Tamsin, log 3186), with the Heralds, Meat Meat and "a great deal of snow".
- Hellen (log 3211-3237): "You broke the hag's mirrors, and a seed took root. The tree grew in one night that lasted four hundred years. Inside the Auroral Wall, anyway." Outside, "the flakes hang in the air". Reptin: "Anduin is out there."
- The Council (log 3348-3421): Kelyn of Spring, Selrae of Winter, Darrus of Autumn, an empty, scorched Summer throne; "Our Summer Marshal, Var Rime-Breaker, went down to check the heat in the roots. Seven days ago." Kelyn: "Rootspire has been stuck in this summer for thirty years." "We'll vote on it after the festival." Selrae courts Glimmer and names "the White Womb", Avarice's vault.
- The Rite at the Still Sky Shrine is stopped, and Eline says "Summer has the heart" (log 4436). The goal: "Summer has Sephek's heart. The festival runs seven days: rest at the Hearth for its last night when you're ready." (4446)
- Then seven nights. The goal lists "Arvik at the Rootwarden Hall, Alerion's desk in the Wind-Glass Library, Lumin at the twin orb, Razzilith, the Lorn Scribe" (4559). Hellen counts down. The run did the Lorn Scribe's riddles, the Memory Codex, Meat Meat's days, the Still Ones and the frozen hunter, and **never entered the Verdant Halls or the Lightfall Promenade** (no `[map] [218x]`/`[219x]` but the Smuggler's Run, in the trial). Neither did any Herald run.
- The festival's last night; the investigation night (log 4915-4984); the Council's summons: "Two ways down" (4997). From here (Chapter 7) about a third of the side content opens (Felka, Halden, Ivran, Kelyn's orb, the watchers' gates).
- **Never on the golden path:** why the party waits a week while "Summer has Sephek's heart"; that Rootspire will judge them by who they helped (the Trial's witnesses, first said at log 8664); what the Rootwardens' circles (Bough, Claw), the Corsairs, the Halo's Silencers, the Guild of Roots and Stone and the "Wardens Emeritus" are, beyond their names; that the player, in Journey, *grew up here*.

**Herald Mode.** The same beats. What differs is who is the stranger:

- **Herald Alberica, Reptin, Alerion, Meat Meat** are four hundred years out of their time. Every Rootspire name is new to them, and the side content explains little of it to them; but the Journey kid's knowledge is no help either, since in Herald Mode there is no Journey kid. Hellen and Tamsin are the only locals who talk *to* them.
- **Herald Wyn** is home. Wyn is a Rootwarden of 400 VR (prologue, `herald_wyn.txt` 18: "Spores druid and Rootwarden of Rootspire, 400 years after the Heralds"), pulled back to the Dale off festival watch. In Rootspire, Kelyn introduces Wyn's own captain to Wyn ("Captain Kethan of the Rootwardens says it was no accident. He keeps the Rootwarden Hall, in the Verdant Halls off the Bazaar.", log 4480-4481), and Rootwarden Ash, a colleague, challenges Wyn as a stranger ("Everyone claims to be a Herald at festival time. Prove it.", log 5238). `grep "herald?(:wyn)"` over `048_A`-`H` finds **no** Wyn branch anywhere in Rootspire's side content.

## 3. Missing scenes

Each numbered line is one box (about 90 characters or less, name included); `[...]` is staging. **NEW** / **CHANGED** as in part 14. Invented material is *(suggestion)*. The staging fixes in notes 15 (E1, E10: givers walk up, speakers on the map) are assumed.

### M15-1 · Why we wait a week: (b) four boxes at the Shrine, on the spine

**What's missing.** The week is the part's container: every quest below happens inside it. It starts on the line "Summer has Sephek's heart", and the next thing the game says is "Nobody rushes the seventh night." The Root-Gate *is* sealed (the sign: *THE ROOT-GATE IS CLOSED. BY ORDER OF THE VERDANT COUNCIL.*, `024:77-79`; Ash: "Nobody's said why", script 15.20), but no one ties the seal to the heart, so the wait reads as the story forgetting its own stakes (Bell pp. 87-89; Mills p. 192: a clock threatens something). One messenger fixes it, and turns the week from waiting into asking, which is what the side content actually is: people who each know something about Summer (Toolbox p. 132: tie the side quests to the main stakes).

**Where:** `024_Chapter6.rb`, after Eline leaves and the Full Restores (`:695`), before the goal (`:696`). **New:** Ivo, the junior Rootwarden who is already a Chapter 10 witness (`036:126`), walks up the Shrine stair (`Stage.actor`, `Stage.approach`). **Music:** the Shrine's, quiet.

```
 1  NEW  A junior Rootwarden comes up the stair two at a time, and stops dead at the frozen man.
 2  NEW  IVO: Heralds. The High Speaker's word: nobody goes below. The Root-Gate stays sealed.
 3  NEW  IVO: Till the festival's done. The whole city's praying at the roots this week.
 4  NEW  REPTIN: Summer has a saint's heart, and we're to wait seven days?          [cl; Herald Reptin: speak]
 5  NEW  IVO: (very young) She said you'd say that. She said: find out where Summer keeps things.
 6  NEW  ALBERICA: Then we ask, sugar. A city this size, somebody's seen something.   [cl]
GOAL (CHANGED): "Summer has Sephek's heart. The Root-Gate's sealed till the festival ends.
                 Ask round the city; rest at the Hearth for the seventh night."
```

- Herald Wyn: box 2 becomes **IVO:** Wyn! You're back. The Speaker's word: nobody goes below. (Ivo knows Wyn: §6.)
- Ash's repeat line (script 15.20) changes to fit: **ASH:** The Root-Gate's sealed. Council orders, till the festival's done.
- The countdown goal (`048`, "The festival's last night is in N nights...") keeps its list and gains the purpose: "...in N nights. Ask round: Arvik at the Rootwarden Hall, Alerion's desk in the Library, ..."

### M15-3 · The asks nobody writes down: (c) side goals, and what the Canticle is for

The Lorn Scribe's four riddles are the biggest side chain the Journey run played, and the game never writes one of them anywhere (Toolbox p. 182; Nicklin pp. 95-101: no scroll-back, many sessions). The lines, in the register the Goal menu needs (plain, no joke, nothing unexplained):

```
side_goal(:scribe_key,    "The Lorn Scribe's riddle for Alberica: a key 'where the year sleeps', in the Library.")
side_goal(:scribe_blade,  "His riddle for Meat Meat: a blade in the silt where the boats froze. The Split Dock.")
side_goal(:scribe_staff,  "His riddle for Reptin: a staff with the first Wardens, in clear sap. The Rootwarden Crypt.")
side_goal(:scribe_mantle, "His riddle for Alerion: a mantle where the world stops at the wall of light. The Wall's edge.")

side_goal(:kelyn_orb,     "The High Speaker goes up to the twin orb at night, alone. Hellen's seen her.")
```

- The riddles' place names are the answers: that's deliberate. The riddles are flavour; the spine-of-the-side-chain (where to go) should be plain (Bateman p. 89, in WRITING_GUIDE §7).
- **The Canticle** (`048_H:288`, notes §3's rewrite). After Eirwen's verses offer, one box that says what it's *for*, since the bible makes it the key to the Third Way's full epilogue:

- **Ivran** (15.27), after "keep the dead off her": **IVRAN:** Get her across whole and the Corsairs pay. Coin, and a favour. We're good for both. *(suggestion; check against the payout)*

### M15-4 · The city keeps count: (b) set up the witnesses during the week

**What's missing.** Chapter 10's verdict is decided by witnesses and evidence, about twenty of them earned in Act II's optional content. The player first hears the word at the trial itself: *KELYN: You have seven days to bring witnesses and evidence.* (log 8664). That's a spine beat resting on content the player was never told mattered; a player who skipped the week learns at the trial that they had a week of chances and spent it sleeping (Toolbox p. 128: choices count once the player sees them; Seger pp. 148-150: set up the payoff). The setup must not spoil the arrest. The Council Hall's four crystals already exist, and already "hear" the verdict: plant them as the city's memory.

**A. At the Council (spine, Ch6).** `024_Chapter6.rb`, Kelyn's first session, after the Wardens' oath (log 3376). Kelyn is on the map.

```
 1  NEW  KELYN: (a hand on the green crystal) The Hall's crystals hear every oath sworn in here.
 2  NEW  KELYN: And everything said for or against the ones who swore them. They forget nothing.
```

**B. The first time any Act II side quest is finished** (a hook in `side_goal_done` for the 048 keys, once). Narration, then Hellen at the next rest:

```
 1  NEW  (Far up the trunk, in the Council Hall, a crystal brightens. Rootspire remembers.)
 2  NEW  HELLEN: Word's round the Bazaar what you did. This city keeps count, love. Good and bad.
```

**C.** Start the Heralds menu's witness list now, under another name: "Friends in Rootspire". It already exists from Chapter 10 ("The Heralds menu now keeps a list of your witnesses and evidence.", log 8669); showing it in Chapter 6 means the trial's list is one the player has watched grow.
