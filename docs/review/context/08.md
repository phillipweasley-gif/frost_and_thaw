# Context pass 08 · Chapter 8: The Rootsun Forge

What a newcomer can and can't follow in Chapter 8, judged only from what plays in 0.7.3: `traces_073/journey.txt` 6993–8359 (the Guild Dig door to the leaves falling), the Herald traces (`herald_alberica.txt` 6402–7782, `herald_wyn.txt` 9863–12394, `herald_reptin.txt` 6581–7970), and the code (`034_Chapter8.rb`, `046_ForgeRooms.rb`, `046_DeepLabyrinth.rb`). It builds on `notes/08.md`, `story/08.md` and `dialogue/08.md`, and assumes their staging fixes (the party on screen, notes E1; Avarice visible through the reveal, E2). Where the pre-0.7.3 script disagrees with the trace, the trace wins: 0.7.3 has already pulled the statements that spent the twist (Razzilith's "a tiefling", the attendant's "wearing a man for thirty years", the Scriptory's "the city trusts his face", Selrae's "I didn't know it was her").

**In one paragraph.** The twist is now fair but hard to find. Since 0.7.3 nothing on the golden path says Summer is Avarice; what's left are good, ambiguous facts (Summer took Sephek's heart, Summer holds a key to Avarice's door, Summer's people paid for the compass that speaks in her voice), but four things stop a newcomer from adding them up. **Nobody has ever said who Avarice is.** The name arrives in Chapter 6 with no introduction and is never tied to "the lady with books and bones" who made Meat Meat. **Var has no face and no history**: the one scene that shows him, and the one fact that makes "thirty years" a clue, are in an optional side room. **The chapter's question, "Who is Summer?", is never asked**, so the player isn't looking. And **the reveal never looks back**: the only recall box plays for players who took the optional liquid mirror. Around the twist, the chapter leans on a sending-stone nobody was given, an Autumn Warden the player has met once, a child who vanishes after she's saved, a clock that lives in an optional vault, and a heart choice whose rules live in an optional ghost.

## 1. What the player knows coming in

**New Journey** (from the log, up to the door of iron and glass):

- **Where and why.** They're home in Rootspire, Year 400, with five Heralds four hundred years out of their time. Selrae told them the rule at the Council: "Inside the Wall, four hundred years. Outside it, weeks." / "Unstick it, and the Wall comes down." The player promised "I'll get you home." That is the act's adhesive, and the Goal menu carries it.
- **The mission.** Kelyn (festival night): "Find our Summer Marshal. And stop whatever is burning the roots." The Marshal, **Var Rime-Breaker**, "went down to check the heat in the roots. Seven days ago." A decree arrived the next morning "signed with Summer's seal", with "a tiny mark: a cracked mirror" in the wax; Alerion: "That's the hag's sign." The city has been "stuck in this summer for thirty years." Var has never been seen on the golden path. Nobody has said how long he has been Marshal.
- **Summer's dealings** (all on the golden path):
  - Eline: "We gave it to Summer. Summer has promised us a winter." / "Summer has the heart. Summer keeps promises." The goal: "Summer has Sephek's heart."
  - Odile at the sap cache: "Summer said a little fire here and the tree would sleep. And winter would come." / ALERION: "Summer's promises don't add up."
  - Hew: "Ten years I've sold sap to Summer's people." / "Iron and glass. Summer's people again." His root-compass is "Built from Prototype 3's spare parts, the batch Summer's people paid for."
  - Selrae in the Sap Wells: "They gave the Frost Saint's heart to Summer." / "The Halo had a friend down here too. White hair. Lovely manners." / ALBERICA: "Avarice." / "The Root-Key. Summer gave Winter a copy."
- **Avarice, as a name only.** First heard from Selrae at the Council ("Avarice has a vault in the ice to the north. The White Womb."), then Eirwen ("Avarice was bleeding Targos white long before the Rime."), then Alberica's one word in the Sap Wells. **Nobody says what she is or that she made Meat Meat.** The Act I facts ("A lady in a tower... - **Her voice.** The boy's mirror ("Hello again, variables."), possessed Mira ("Our little failures."), and both times "MEAT MEAT: ...Meat Meat knows that voice. From the cold table."
- **Glimmer.** Shade stepped through Brightmane's mirror and came back as Glimmer: "And this is still cursed. ... Still killing me slowly, since Saltmarsh." / "Brightmane says I'll find it a better fire."
- **The place.** "Dougan's Hole. Right under our feet." (Reptin, the Root-Tier); "The library's oldest maps show the hag's lair. Under Dougan's Hole. / Which is under us." (Eirwen).
- **Optional, and only if played** (skip test, Toolbox p. 229): Razzilith's "Summer is... a shape. (Heat-shimmer, a long coat. No face.)" (a Rootspire downtime morning); the attendant's "Summer is a coat." Keep it warm."); the Dissectory (Darrus's sigil on the crates; "Autumn paid for the cold table."); the ice mask ("Ithryn Athis").

**Herald Mode** (the Alberica, Wyn and Reptin traces): the same facts, with three differences.
- **Alberica** hears Selrae and thinks "(Avarice.)". The player-as-Alberica is given a name they were never told, as if they'd always known it.
- **Wyn** has been on the Heartroot road as a native, and Hyphora is their god, so the sap in the engine ("That's her sap. That's Hyphora's blood.") lands harder; but Wyn's own want ("why Hyphora called them away from their post at the Underdark door") has still never been said in any mode before the Tribunal charges them with it (part 09).
- **Reptin** heard Anduin in the Rift ("(Wolves, singing. And under them Anduin, your husband, calling you home.)"). No mode has told Reptin what the Rootwardens think he is, which Chapter 9 and the Tribunal lean on.

### M8-1 · Kelyn's sending-stone, and the chapter's question said aloud: (b) two short beats, Ch6 and 8.01

The stone is also the cleanest way to put the chapter's question and its wrong paths on screen at the chapter's first scene (Seger p. 43; WRITING_GUIDE §3a "every chapter asks one question and the game says it aloud").
