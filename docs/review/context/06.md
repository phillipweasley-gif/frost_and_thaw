It builds on `notes/06.md` (F1–F15), `story/06.md` and `dialogue/06.md` (§3 "lines that don't follow", §4b the Avarice ruling), and doesn't repeat their staging and line fixes. Boxes are one per line, ≤ 90 characters with `NAME: `. Invented material is *(suggestion)*.

The 0.7.3 build fixed the worst of it: the Heralds now walk in when they speak (`Stage.summon`), and Selrae's new beat after the oath (`their_time_beat`, 024:741) gives the Journey player the Wall rule and their second goal. What's left is a chapter that **names things before it shows them** (the Halo, the Rimefang, Avarice, the White Womb, chardalyn, Arvik), **waits a week without saying why**, and **tells the Herald player nothing about why they can't go home**: the Wall rule plays only under `journey?`.

## 1. What the player knows coming in

**New Journey** (prologue and Act I, `journey.txt` 1–3167):

- They're a kid from Rootspire who runs errands for Professor Wynsnow; Tamsin Brightrun (Hellen's kid, a would-be Rootwarden) is their rival; Hellen keeps the Rooted Hearth. They've seen the market, the root-lift, the College, and the mural: five Heralds, two dragons, and "a small sixth figure. No face." It was the Festival of Returning Light, "Four hundred years ago today."
- The legend as Hellen told it: the Heralds "broke her mirrors... A seed took root, and the tree grew in one night." The word **Verdant Reversal** ("Year nought") is Tamsin's.
- A cracked lantern of "Reversal glass" pulled them into the Heralds' winter. The ground shuddered first: "The ground shudders. Once."
- Act I: the five Heralds, Shimmer's death and the Requiem, Frostina (Shimmer's heart in Auril's roc's egg), Razzy and Meat Meat ("ran away together. From a cold table. From the lady with books and bones. She took Razzy's voice." Alerion: "Naerth's 'pale lady'."), Sephek frozen and carried south on the Wennen's sledge, Thredra's mirrors, the last mirror, the golden acorn in the root, the wyrmlings hatching and being pulled back ("WAIT"), the fall through four hundred years (panels S-08a–e: the sapling, the Wennen dragging Sephek's ice to it, the dragons, Hellen's ancestor carving the wall).
- **Their own goal is met.** This run picked "Step through." (`tried_to_leave`): Alberica caught their wrist, the glass broke. "Far to the north, something felt you choose... The Frostmaiden has marked you." They hold the **Rimed Owl Feather** and a **Wolf-and-Bloom Carving**.
- **Never shown:** what the Auroral Wall is; who rules Rootspire; what "Summer" is; the Fractured Halo; the Rimefang or any curse on Shade's knife; the name **Avarice**; the word **chardalyn**.

**Herald Mode.** The Herald player saw Act I from inside the party, but **not** the prologue: they've never seen Rootspire, the mural or the lantern, and "the same shudder as the lantern" (festival night) points at nothing for them. Alberica's prologue adds "The family's bargain with Auril. This thing's part of it. Nobody ever said the price." Reptin knows Anduin is his husband (Ch5's chasm voices: "And Anduin, calling me home. My husband."). **Herald Wyn** is the special case: a Rootwarden of 400 VR who fell through the Sap Gate's mirror shard on festival night, so Chapter 6 is *their own home*, and they come back to it with their own goal met (`my_glass` = "gate shard"; the last mirror showed "the Sap Gate... Your post. Your lamp, still lit.").

## 3. Missing scenes

Each numbered line is one box; `[...]` is staging; **NEW** is a box that doesn't exist, **CHANGED** replaces the box it follows, unmarked boxes are the owner's. All assume 0.7.3's `Stage.summon` (Heralds walk in when they speak).

**The one idea under most of these.** In Act I the player was the outsider and people explained things to them. In Act II the outsiders flip: the *Heralds* fell four hundred years into the player's city. So Rootspire's people explain things to the Heralds, because they need telling, and the player, who knows the market but not the Council, hears it at the same time (Toolbox p. 101). That's the licence for every gloss below: Hellen, Kelyn, Rheven and Lumin say what things are because the legends in front of them have never seen them.

## 6. Herald Mode

What a Herald player is never told, beyond the Journey gaps above.

**All Heralds.** The Wall rule and the second goal (M6-3, **High**); "the same shudder as the lantern" (M6-8 box 3); Rootspire itself: a Herald has never seen the market, the mural or the College, so the arrival needs one line that says this city grew from the acorn they planted. **Alberica.** Her prologue gives her the bargain ("Nobody ever said the price"), and the Dreaming shows her a key that locks the Dale in winter; nothing in Ch6 lets her connect the two, and that's right for a postgame secret. But she should *notice*: after her vision, `(A key. A vault. Grandmother's bargain had a vault in it. Nobody ever said.)` *(suggestion; keeps the price unsaid)*. **Alerion.** M6-3's thought ties the Wall to Talion, which sets up the Echo Gate.

**Meat Meat.** M6-7 is hers to speak: as Meat Meat, she names her own maker. That's the right weight for her: Avarice is "what she was made for" (bible arc). Keep the driftwood chest optional; the scorch carries the spine fact.
