# Context pass 14 · Side: the Ten-Towns quests and Act I's optional townsfolk

What a newcomer can and can't follow in Act I's optional content, judged from what plays in 0.7.3 (`traces_073/journey.txt` and the three Herald runs) and, since no run finished a single quest, from the code (`045_*.rb`, `045z_TownFavours.rb`, `045_TenTowns.rb`). It builds on `notes/14.md` (33 editor findings, most still open), `story/14.md` and `dialogue/14.md`, and doesn't repeat their staging fixes (givers walking up, Boy's sprite, the yetis' sprites): it assumes them.

Side content is optional, so the skip test runs both ways. **Out:** the main story must not need anything only found here. **In:** each quest must make sense on its own, met in any order, with its giver's problem, the stakes and the reward said up front. The quests themselves pass the second test better than most of Act I's spine does: Keegan, Hruna and Nimsy open mid-problem, and the yeti mother is a real choice. Where they fail is around the edges. The word "Ten-Towns" is explained only by Keegan. The favours system the owner asked for is never announced, so its first message ("Ten-Towns favours: 1 of 5. Still owed a debt: ...") is the first the player hears of it. Two quests hide the town's best reward until the payout. And when the player falls into Rootspire, every unfinished quest stays in the Goal menu for the rest of the game, pointing at a mountain four hundred years away.

## 1. What the player knows coming in

Side content in Act I is met at five points on the spine, so "coming in" depends on the town.

**New Journey** (from `journey.txt`):

- **Targos, the minute you land** (log 278-325). You fell through Tamsin's lantern into "Targos, in Icewind Dale. The winter from Hellen's stories". A pale elf, a woman with a lute and a girl with "Tavern's west" walked past. That is all. The goal says "Get out of the cold. Three Flags Sailing, up the hill." Nobody has said "Ten-Towns", "Speaker", "the Frostmaiden", or that you can't simply go home. **The run took the Mountain Climb here** (Boy at log 297, `climb_taken` at 325): the third thing it did in the Dale, before it met the Heralds. It never went up the mountain.
- **Bryn Shander, lottery day** (log 815ff). You know the relics are bait (Epheris); the glass that brought you broke from a hag's mirror at Dougan's Hole (your own goal, `017:484`); "Speaker" means the person who "runs the town, and most of its debts" (Epheris, log 214 in the Herald runs, same line in Journey); the lottery; Sephek's name. You don't yet know the Speakers signed a bargain with Auril: Duvessa says it the next morning (log 973). Foaming Mugs is offered before that.
- **Termalaine, on the road north after Sephek** (log 1012-1085). You know Duvessa's "Ten Speakers signed a bargain with Her. A life a month, or food, or warmth.", that she'll "owe you every name on that board", and that Sephek wants Shimmer back.
- **Lonelywood, the morning after Shimmer goes** (log 1150-1280). Grief, the Glade, Jet Sentry. Then the Wennen sledge carries you south to Bryn Shander (log 1505-1520). Nothing says you can walk back north.
- **Good Mead, on the Eastway to the Hole** (log 1548-1592). "Headed to the Hole? Eat first. Nobody comes back from there fat." Nobody says the town's Speaker was killed three days ago.
- **The fall.** Act I ends at the hag's Heart (log 3167). There is no warning, in any mode, that the Dale's side content closes there. The Ten-Towns maps come back only in Chapter 9's burning flyover (log 8457), where nothing can be done.

**Herald Mode** (Alberica, Reptin and Wyn runs). The same, plus the prologue's "The Everlasting Rime has held it for two years." A Herald starts on the ship and lands with the party, so Keegan's companions' lines play from the first visit. None of the three runs took a single side quest (`grep "[flag] climb|mugs|mine|white_moose|mead"` finds only Journey's `climb_taken`). Herald Alberica has one fact a Journey player lacks and the side content could use: *(The family's bargain with Auril. This thing's part of it. Nobody ever said the price.)* (`005:269`). Herald Wyn is an outsider like you (a Rootwarden from 400 VR), so everything Journey lacks, Wyn lacks.

**Never on the golden path before or during Act I:** what "Ten-Towns" is (Keegan only), that there are ten towns and you'll see five of them, the Council of Speakers (a hall nothing points to, which closes once you reach Termalaine), that the towns' troubles add up to anything, and what the Pokémon reward items do (Exp. Share, the Choice items, evolution stones, Amulet Coin, Ability Capsule) to someone who's never played Pokémon.

### M14-1 · The Ten-Towns favours, said before they pay: (b) four short beats, one on the spine

**What's missing.** The owner's favours system has a payoff and no setup (Seger, p. 148: every payoff set up, earlier, in another scene). The player first learns it exists from a parenthesis after their first reward; learns there is a bigger prize only when a runner hands it over; and is never told which five of the ten towns count. Duvessa is the right mouth for all of it: she's the head of the Council, she signs the thanks letter, and she has just said "I'll owe you every name on that board" on the spine (log 973). The outsider rule (Toolbox p. 101) gives her the reason: you're strangers, so she tells you what Ten-Towns is.

**A. At Duvessa's seal (spine).** `014_Chapter2.rb` `leader_duvessa`, after 842-843 ("Take the seal... every name on that board."), before the Palisade Seal. Duvessa is on screen (she just fought you). **Music:** gymleader fades to Opelucid City under box 1.

## 6. Herald Mode

What a Herald player is told differently here. The side content mostly passes: its Herald lines are `cl()` (dropped when absent) or `speak()` (spoken by you, with a quiet version), and the runs show Reptin's "We swear it" and the Council's lines working. The gaps are about who the Herald is.

**Alberica.** The one Herald with a stake in the charter: her own prologue says *(The family's bargain with Auril. This thing's part of it. Nobody ever said the price.)* (`005:269`). At the Council, ten Speakers read out *their* bargain with Auril, and Herald Alberica thinks nothing. And her three Council lines are `as()` thoughts that Naerth answers ("All of us, my dear."), which WRITING_GUIDE §5 forbids. *Fix:* make them `speak()` (loud: "Excuse me. Speakers. Hello..."; quiet: "...Who wrote 'necessary'?"), and add after the charter sign, Herald Alberica only *(suggestion)*: *(Another bargain with Her. Your family made one too. Nobody ever said what it cost.)* That repeats the prologue's fact, gives away nothing about the White Vault, and sets up Act II's pendant clues (§8).

**Reptin.** Fine: his oath to Garret is a `speak()`, the bears scene switches to "You walk up", and his Council question ("Speaker, what would 'some other way' look like?") is a line Oarus answers, so it should be `speak()` too, not a thought.

**Wyn.** An outsider, like the Journey player: every Journey fix here serves Wyn too, and Keegan's "You're not from Ten-Towns" is right for a Rootwarden from 400 VR. One Wyn-only line would help *(suggestion)*: at Ravisin, a frost druid, *(A druid. Like you. Gone the other way, the way the cold goes.)*, the first time Herald Wyn sees another of their kind turned.

**Meat Meat** (no trace; from the code). Herald Meat Meat travels with you from 2.01, so her goblin lines at Izobai's sled play as thoughts: *(Meat Meat knows goblins. Goblins are cold too.)* The "offer them something warm" branch then gives her line to nobody. *Fix:* make it her `speak()` choice, since Izobai answers it.

**Alerion** (no trace). His ring-goes-cold lines (the Cairn, the mine, Janth) are the only sense he has of the dead; as a Herald he's never told why the ring does it. Not this part's to fix (part 00/01); the side quests use it well once the spine has.
