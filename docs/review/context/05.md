# Context pass 05 · Chapter 5: The Cackling Chasm (the Act I climax, the Reversal, the fall into Rootspire)

Read as a newcomer, from the New Journey log (lines 1834-2801, including two losses and replays), the Alberica
trace (1543-2357) and the code as it stands (`023_Chapter5.rb`, `044_ChasmFaithful.rb`, `tools/build_ch5.rb`).
The 2026-11-05 build is in the log: Heralds as stand-ins, a Goal per step, and the last-mirror choice ("Step
through." / "Break it.", `last_mirror_choice`, 023:1185). Both Herald traces are Alberica runs (the "meat_meat"
trace picks Alberica at line 15); other Heralds are from the code. Boxes are one per line in backticks, ≤ 90
characters including `NAME: `. Invented lines are *(suggestion)*.

The question this part has to answer for a newcomer is the brief's: **does the player understand what the
Reversal is, and what just happened when they fall?** As built, mostly not. The word "Reversal" is never said in
Chapter 5 (the player last saw it on the prologue's first card, "the year 400 of the Verdant Reversal", and on the
lantern seller's "Real Reversal glass! Dug out of the deep roots, where the old hag's mirrors broke!"). The game has
planted the answer and never lets the player pick it up. §3 M5-9 is the fix.

## 1. What the player knows coming in

**New Journey** (log to 1833), on top of part 04 §1:

- Thredra, "Granny", a hag, sent the relics; her "watchers watched you aboard"; Auril "wants a new coat to wear...
  I'm only her seamstress". She offered to end the winter and "take the wolf out of" Reptin for "a little task":
  "There's a door down there only a wolf can open, and a thing I need fetched."
- She knows the player "fell through my glass", offered to "send you home", and said at the end: "My last glass is
  in the chasm. Yours too." Goal: "The Cackling Chasm... She's there, with the last of her mirrors."
- Breaking her three mirrors freed the frozen fishers. Nobody said why it worked (only the Journey dare links them).
- Reptin was bitten by a starving wolf on the tundra; it "burns like fire under snow". Thredra called him "cursed"
  and spoke of the moon. Nothing has happened to him yet.
- The golden acorn (Ch2: "Plant it somewhere the sun can't reach") and both eggs (Ch2 Razzy's green egg; Ch3 the
  Frost Egg with Shimmer's heart, named Frostina, which hums her four notes) have not been mentioned since Ch3.
  Luna Carver, Ch3: "It has a heart now. It needs time, and the world hasn't got enough."
- Shimmer, Ch3: "There's always a third way."
- Kanan, one of the two talking wolves, "bolts south, toward the chasm" after the ambush.
- Never said: that the player is in the past, or that Rootspire's World Tree hasn't grown yet.

**Herald Mode (Alberica):** the same, minus every lantern-glass line. She has the rune on her chest, not the
pendant. Rootspire means nothing to her: the Herald player has never seen it, and nothing in Act I names it.
