Boxes ≤ 90 characters with `NAME: `; *(suggestion)* marks invented material.

Two facts about the golden path shape everything here. **Road B's minimum is short:** `forge_door(:b)` sets `road_b_done` as soon as the Root-Key turns in Hew's door (032:553), so the boy's mirror, the Overflow, Mira, and the whole of the lower Heartroot (Mirror Garden to Scriptory) are off the spine. They hold most of the chapter's answers: the engine's purpose, the dragon, V-7, Sephek's heart as Auril's, Autumn's crates, Var's night. **Road A's spine is all talk with people who know things** (Eira, Yarro, Myr, Hyphora), and it's the better-built road. So the fixes put the few facts Chapter 8 needs on the two short spines, and leave the deep zones as the reward for looking.
