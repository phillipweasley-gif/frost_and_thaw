# 00 · Context pass: the Prologue (New Journey) and the Herald Mode openings

What a first-time player is assumed to know in part 00, and the fixes, written so they play. The player here has never played a Pokémon game, has never heard of D&D or *Rime of the Frostmaiden*, and has not read the bible.

**Sources.** The New Journey run (`journey_log.txt` lines 1–210, build 0.7.2), the Herald trace for Alberica (`trace_herald_alberica.txt` lines 1–36), the code (`005_NewGame.rb`, `030_Prologue.rb`, `001_Config.rb`, `007_Story.rb`), the as-played script `script/00.md`, and the earlier reviews (`notes/00.md`, `story/00.md`, `dialogue/00.md`). Where those reviews already wrote a fix, this file points to it (for example "notes E2") and adds only what the context problem needs.

**About the Herald traces.** The first `trace_herald_meat_meat.txt` picked Alberica (the driver took the first option); it was re-run during this pass and now plays Meat Meat (its lines 20–37 match the code quoted here). Reptin's, Alerion's and Wyn's openings are read from the code (`005_NewGame.rb:228-302`, `007_Story.rb:150-176`); no trace exists for them.

**Lines marked *(suggestion)* are new.** They keep to the bible's canon. Where the canon has no answer, the line has a `[bracket]` and an owner question.

## 1. What the player knows coming in

**New Journey, at the white screen (log 1–207).** What the screen has said:

- "Four hundred years ago, a winter that would not end gripped Icewind Dale..." (trails into a menu; never says it ended).
- Rootspire, "the city under the World Tree", year 400 "of the Verdant Reversal" (unexplained). The festival's lanterns are "for the Heralds of Dawn and Dusk", who "saved the Dale" on "pine-mead and spite". **No Herald is named; no deed is told.**
- You run Wynsnow's errands; Tamsin is Hellen's kid and wants to be a Rootwarden (never explained).
- You have a Pokémon and fought Tamsin. Five Poké Balls and two Potions arrived with no giver. Nobody says what a Pokémon is for, how to catch one, or what heals one.
- A carving of five heroes, two dragons and a faceless sixth, never called the Heralds.
- "Reversal glass... where the old hag's mirrors broke." Tamsin says it's fake. No hag, no mirrors, no rule.
- A dock in a blizzard inside the crack. Then white. **No line says you fell in.** The only line before Chapter 6 that says you go "back through time" is the optional mode help (`005_NewGame.rb:99`).
- No goal. The last one (your first Pokémon) is done.

**Herald Mode, Alberica, Reptin, Alerion (Alberica trace 1–36).** The select-screen blurb (the only place that says "dwarf bard", "elf druid, exile of the Sacred Flame", "eladrin ranger, once frozen outside of time"); "The Everlasting Rime has held it for two years" (no meaning given); a few lines about the relic arriving unasked; "the lanterns of Targos... on the shore of Maer Dualdon"; "your partner... as always", unseen. **Not told:** why they're on the ship, what they want, who they left (Anduin, Talion, Alberica's family), what a Lutling is.

**Herald Mode, Meat Meat (trace 20–29).** Three weeks out of "the lab", hiding in the hay with Razzy, who "can't talk any more"; Razzy gone tonight, footprints "going north"; five cold strangers on the dock. **Not told:** who Razzy is to her, or that the gate is shut to her too.

**Herald Mode, Wyn (code, `005_NewGame.rb:281-302`).** Rootspire's festival, "somebody else's tonight"; the Sap Gate, the spores, Sableye; the shudder, the shard, the dock, the cold. **Not told:** that Wyn is a Rootwarden, what the gate guards (Wyn is put on trial for leaving it in Ch10), or any of the Heralds legend: a Wyn player skips the whole New Journey prologue.

## 3. Missing scenes

Events the prologue refers to but never shows, in story order. Each says whether to (a) add a new scene, (b) add a short beat to an existing scene, or (c) leave it told but tell it better. Box lengths below were checked: none is over 90 characters, `NAME: ` included.

### M1 · The Heralds on the wall: "last night's stories" (b, inside 0.11)

**Referred to, never shown:** "somewhere in the middle of last night's stories" (0.03), "Heralds didn't save the Dale on an empty stomach" (0.03), "Every lantern in the city is lit for the Heralds" (0.02). The whole game rests on the player knowing, at 1.03, that the strangers in the tavern are the legends of Rootspire. As played, the prologue never names one Herald or tells one deed, so that recognition can't happen. Chapter 6's Hellen line ("Here's what my grandmother told me. You broke the hag's mirrors, and a seed took root") shows the legend exists in canon; the prologue simply never plays it.

**Why a beat and not a speech.** Despain's rule is to kill the backstory unless the present needs it (Walsh, pp. 107–108), and here it does: it's the setup for the game's first payoff (01.md B2). Seger's ways to make it go down are an interesting character, conflict, and back-and-forth instead of monologue (pp. 136–142): Tamsin, a fan, recites it and gets it wrong, and Hellen, who told it last night, corrects her. It ends on the one thing nobody knows, the sixth figure, so the scene's point stays last (Seger pp. 142–143).

**Where:** `scene_pro_mural` (`030_Prologue.rb:398-431`). Replaces the box list at lines 410–424. Trigger unchanged (autorun on entering the Hearth after the battle).

**On screen:** Tamsin at the mural (15,7), you at (15,8) facing the wall, Hellen behind the bar (3,6), the bard (7,10), the drinker (10,8). **Music:** when the camera reaches the wall, cut from Ecruteak City to a Heralds cue (new `Stage::THEMES` key `heralds:`; track to choose), as notes E9 asked. ### M2 · Your first Pokémon, and what it's for (b, inside 0.07 and 0.09)

For a player who has never played Pokémon, this is the tutorial. Despain's order is tell, show, do, feel (Walsh, pp. 94–97): Wynsnow *tells* (one line), Tamsin *shows* (her counter-pick, said as a gloat), the battle is the *do*, and the loss is the *feel*. Walsh's "text trap" warns against putting button names in dialogue (p. 98), so none of these lines names a button.

## 7. Priority list

The ten fixes that would most help a newcomer follow part 00, in order.

1. **M1, the Heralds on the wall.** Tamsin names Alberica and Reptin, gets the legend wrong, and Hellen corrects her: they broke the hag's mirrors and the tree grew in one night. Without this, nothing in Chapter 1 can be recognised.
2. **M3, the glass remembers.** The seller's "Mirror-glass keeps what it sees. That glass saw the Heralds.", the lute on the gangplank, and "You fall into the Heralds' winter", added to story/00 §8's rewrite with the player's input on the lantern.
3. **H5, Wyn's watch.** The legend as something everyone else is celebrating, the gate as a post Wyn is holding, and a shard in Sableye's claws so Wyn lands with the same goal as the Journey player.
4. **The title card and mode menu.** Finish the sentence ("came out the other side with spring"), mark New Journey "(start here)", and say in the help that the lantern throws you back 400 years.
5. **M2, what a Pokémon is for.** Wynsnow's one line before the table, Tamsin's type gloat, "Lost? Good.", and the Poké Balls and Potions handed over with the catching lesson.
6. **H1–H3, a want for each Herald on the ship.** The shared Rime box, one line of why-north each, the partner shown doing something, and a ship backdrop.
7. **H3 and H2, plant Talion and Anduin.** One line each. Anduin needs the owner's decision on where he is in Act I.
8. **H4, Meat Meat.** Razzy in the next cage, the footprints toward the south gate, the gate shut, and a side goal; end the hay prologue before the dock so 1.01 shows it.
9. **The version help and the character-menu frame.** Canon-safe roles for Auril, Avarice, Frostina and Razzilith (Frostina is not Auril's dragon), and "You're a kid from Rootspire, and today you get your first Pokémon."
10. **Small glosses and bridges.** Rootwarden in Tamsin's narration, the Rootspire card's "since the tree grew, all in one night", the eladrin gloss, and one box each for the lift up and down.
