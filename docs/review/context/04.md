## 1. What the player knows coming in

**New Journey** (from the log, lines 1-1255):

- They are a kid from Rootspire, a city under a World Tree, in "the year 400 of the Verdant Reversal". They grew up
  on Hellen's stories of the Heralds, five heroes carved on her tavern wall, with a small sixth figure with no face.
- A lantern of "Reversal glass", "dug out of the deep roots, where the old hag's mirrors broke", cracked and dropped
  them on the Targos docks in a blizzard. They still hold a shard: the Lantern Glass.
- Nobody has said aloud that this is four hundred years in the past. The title card ("Four hundred years ago, a
  winter that would not end...") and five figures off a gangplank are the only clues.
- Epheris: the five relics are bait ("Somebody wanted you here"); the shard is "hag-glass, mirror-ice. Nobody makes it
  but one old woman in the Dale"; "Glass that brought you can take you back. If you find the mirror it broke from."
  Both point south-east to Dougan's Hole.
- The party: Alberica (dwarf bard; her pendant flared and cracked at the Bryn Shander lottery and Auril marked her
  with a rune; "So she wants a coat again. And she's trying me on for size."), Reptin (the talker, carries the golden
  acorn and the Moon-Touched Egg), Alerion (says almost nothing), Shade (smuggler; their sister Shimmer died in
  Lonelywood choosing "a third way"; Shade carries the Frost Egg with Shimmer's heart in it, which hummed four notes
  and was named Frostina), Meat Meat (goblin; her friend Razzy turned into the green egg).
- Auril, the Frostmaiden, holds the Dale in a two-year winter and needs a body to walk in ("Bring a third [heart]...
  and the Frostmaiden gets a body to walk in", Luna Carver).
- Zenith and Jet Sentry: "a hag lives in a chasm south of Dougan's Hole... Your trinkets pull toward her... She keeps
  mirrors. Cold glass. Like the shard you carry." Luna to the player: "You I can't see at all. Like you're not on her
  wheel yet."
- Goal entering the chapter: "Bryn Shander's east gate, then south-east to Dougan's Hole: who's calling them, and the
  hag's mirrors."
- Last seen of the acorn: Ch2, Mishann, "Plant it somewhere the sun can't reach." Last seen of the eggs: Ch3.

**Herald Mode (Alberica; from the trace):**

- She knows herself from the menu blurb and her opening: the pendant she threw in the sea came back; "Something too
  tall stands at the edge of the lamplight" on the ship. She lost the pendant in the Bryn Shander attic ("The pendant
  is gone. The rune on your chest isn't.").
- She knows the others only as tavern strangers from Chapter 1. Nobody has told her what Reptin, Alerion or Shade
  were before Targos, and nothing in Act I does.
- Goal entering: "Bryn Shander's east gate..." with "Find who's calling" (no mirror half).
- Reptin, Alerion and Meat Meat Heralds get a three- or four-line opening (`005_NewGame.rb` 236-276): Reptin's
  "It knows" captain, Alerion "woke calling out in a voice that wasn't yours", Meat Meat's lab and Razzy. Reptin is
  never told he has a husband, or why he is an exile; Alerion is never told about Talion.
