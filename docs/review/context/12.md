# Context pass 12 · Chapter 12: The Sea of Moving Ice

What a newcomer can and can't follow in Chapter 12, judged only from what plays in 0.7.3: `traces_073/journey.txt` 10271–10478 (from `ch11_done` to the first step into the Statue Fields), and the same stretch of `herald_alberica.txt` (9639–9848), `herald_reptin.txt` (9843–10058) and `herald_wyn.txt` (14825–15212). It builds on `notes/12.md`, `story/12.md` and `dialogue/12.md` (§1a, what moved with the Vault).

**The chapter has changed since the as-played script.** `script/12.md` still has the White Vault, the Glasswater and Avarice's cradle in the middle of this chapter. In 0.7.3 they are postgame (Part 13, §3 M13-8); Chapter 12 is now: the Council's send-off, Auril's three relics in the Heartroot, the root-lift, Tamsin's last battle, the griffon flight, the camp and Sopo. The code still files the Statue Fields, the Garden and the Gate under "SC 12.04–12.06" (`038_Chapter12.rb`); this pass keeps the old split and reviews them in 13.md. Staging has moved on too: Heralds now appear on the map in most scenes (`[stage] alberica appears`), but Tamsin, Hellen, Rheven and the Corsair are still voices over an empty roost or dock.

The chapter's question in the bible is "Can we reach her?" Nobody asks it. The words are mostly fine; the context missing is **why we're going and what we'll do when we get there**. Chapter 11 ends with Reptin's "And on the way, we decide what happens to Auril", and then the way contains no deciding at all: the dilemma the ending turns on is never argued (story review 12, problem 3). The Goal says "The griffon roost" with no reason. The crossing, the chapter's title, is three narration boxes over a tree-city. And the two things the player carried in alone (Auril's owl-feather mark and Avarice's bare arms in the Forge) and the one thing Alberica carries alone (her family's bargain) get no beat here, the last quiet stretch before the climax.

## 1. What the player knows coming in

**New Journey** (from the log, up to `ch11_done`):

- They are a Rootspire kid who fell four hundred years back through a lantern of "real Reversal glass", travelled the Dale with five strangers who are now the Heralds, and broke the hag's last mirror. At that mirror they reached for home (`tried_to_leave` in this run) and the glass broke in their hands; "Far to the north, something felt you choose... The Frostmaiden has marked you." They carry a **Rimed Owl Feather** (key item). Nobody has mentioned it since (Act I, 3155–3160).
- They came home an hour after they left, with the Heralds. **Their second goal** was given at the Council in Chapter 6 (3387–3394): Selrae said the Wall stands because the wheel is stuck, "Unstick it, and the Wall comes down. Your time and ours, one morning", and the player chose **"I'll get you home."** (`promised_their_time`). The Goal menu still carries it as "Also: Your own: you broke the glass that brought them..."
- In the Forge (Chapter 8) Avarice "died" in Hell's Veil, and the whole party believed it: ALERION: "...Dead. Nothing in her." This run also saw the body's **bare arms** ("Avarice's arms were covered in burned sigils. These have none.") and *(Vessel IX: viable.)*; "You don't say anything. Nobody else noticed." (8180–8187). That clue is **optional**: it needs `decoy_clue` (the Stasis Gallery's Vessel IX, a side find) and the "Look more closely" pick (`034_Chapter8.rb` 813–818). On the bare golden path the player believes she's dead.
- Chapter 11, on the spine: the Halo raised Sephek; Iskra, "the roc. The bird that dropped her egg", took Frostina north in a crystal, with **Sephek and Eline on her back**. Lumin: "Three is a summons." Selrae: "Solstice. Her island in the Sea of Moving Ice. She'll pour herself into all three at once... the Rewritten Auril." - Reptin, 10200: "And on the way, we decide what happens to Auril."
- **Not on the golden path:** Alberica's family bargain (never, in Journey); the Sap Wells' vision of "a vault of white ice... wings spread over a frozen eye" (optional drink, 6059); the White Womb as a place (Selrae's 3421 and Avarice's 8176 are on the spine, but nobody says what it is or where); who Kethan is beyond Wyn's trial hearing (9140); the Corsairs' debt to Hellen (an optional night, 4566).
