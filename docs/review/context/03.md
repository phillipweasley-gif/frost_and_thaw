# Context pass 03 · Chapter 3: Shimmer's Requiem

What a newcomer can and can't follow in Chapter 3, judged only from what plays (`script/03.md`, `journey_log.txt` 886–1257, the Herald trace). It builds on `notes/03.md`, `story/03.md` and `dialogue/03.md` and assumes their staging fixes (Heralds on screen, Jet walking in, the Requiem's egg and hum).

The words are good; the context is missing. Nobody says what Auril *is*. "Vessel" exists only in the pause-menu goal, "Chosen" only in a battle title. The player never learns how Shimmer knows Sephek, or what he wants her *for*, until she is on the slab. The night she leaves happens inside a "..." box, and the road from the attic to the Glade is never shown or told. The frost egg and the Wennen arrive in the scene that uses them.

## 1. What the player knows coming in

**New Journey** (from the log, up to the Termalaine Path):

- They fell through a lantern into a snowbound Targos, "four hundred years ago" (the title card). The word "Rime" has not been used; nobody has said this winter is someone's doing.
- Five strangers met in a tavern, each with a cold relic; Shimmer's is "a stone. It's under my ribs. Don't ask." A woman in frost-furs looked at each of them, smiled at the player, and left frost on the floor.
- Epheris: the relics are bait, pointing south-east to Dougan's Hole. The player's lantern shard is "hag-glass, mirror-ice" from "one old woman in the Dale", and "Glass that brought you can take you back. If you find the mirror it broke from." **This is the player's own goal, and it has not been mentioned since.**
- The lottery: each new moon Targos, Bryn Shander and Easthaven draw a name, and the drawn person "walks out onto the tundra". People who pay to dodge it are found frozen "by a man who doesn't feel the cold". Shimmer named him: "Sephek." A trapper called him "The Frostmaiden's chosen." Nobody said who the Frostmaiden is.
- Chapter 2: a voiceless goblin with a throat scar was shelled in frost and saved as an egg that "wants a name, spoken as a promise" (Reptin's blood; Shade's unicorn horn, "since the Saltmarsh job"). At the draw, Alberica's pendant filled the square with memories, the woman in frost-furs marked her, and an unseen voice said "That's Her. The Frostmaiden. Auril." Sephek told "the girl with the stone" the Frostmaiden sends her regards; Shimmer: "Tell her I said no. Again." He: "Come before the next moon, or I choose someone from this square instead." Duvessa sent them north.
- **Not on the golden path:** the carved cat (the optional Targos rest, 1.07), Shimmer humming, Sephek at Torg's, Hlin, every Wennen line, the nursery egg.

**Herald Mode** (the Alberica trace; Reptin's and Alerion's prologues from `005_NewGame.rb`): all of the above except the lantern glass and Epheris's "home" line. They add one fact a Journey player lacks: "The Everlasting Rime has held it for two years." They do *not* add who the player-Herald is (Alberica's family, Reptin's druidry and husband, Alerion's dead brother) or what the relic does. *(The Meat Meat trace in the scratchpad picked Alberica by mistake, so Meat Meat's Chapter 3 is judged from the code.)*
