# Context pass 13 · Chapter 13: Grimskalle, the endings, the epilogue and the Vault hook

What a newcomer can and can't follow from the Statue Fields to the end, judged only from what plays in 0.7.3: `traces_073/journey.txt` 10478–10993, `herald_alberica.txt` 9848–10357, `herald_reptin.txt` 10058–10561 and `herald_wyn.txt` 15212–16299, all of which chose the Third Way. Shatter and the Still Keeper loop are read from `039_Chapter13.rb`. It builds on `notes/13.md`, `story/13.md` and `dialogue/13.md`, and on 12.md (the dock scene, M12-6, carries several of this part's setups).

The climax works: Shimmer's "There's always a third way" becomes the ending that lets winter be a season, and the Tests try what the player watched each Herald do (story review 13: "paid in full"). The context gaps are at the edges of it. **Four ending payoffs were set up off the spine, or not at all**: the player's own goal reaches Herald players not at all; Auril's mark on the player is never read; Alberica's family bargain is never said in Journey and never paid in any mode; and the postgame hook sends the player "beyond the Auroral Wall" one minute after telling them the Wall fell. Inside the chapter, two people arrive in the scene that uses them (Eline, who dies; Razzilith, who lands), and the Tests name kings, a boy and a form of Auril that nobody has introduced.
