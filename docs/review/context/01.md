# 01 · Context pass: Chapter 1, Targos

What a first-time player is assumed to know in Chapter 1, and the fixes, written so they play. Same player as 00.md: never played Pokémon, never heard of D&D or *Rime of the Frostmaiden*, hasn't read the bible.

**Sources.** The New Journey run (`journey_log.txt` lines 211–548, build 0.7.2, which already has the owner's 2026-11-05 lantern-glass goal at Epheris), the Alberica Herald trace (lines 37–307), the code (`007_Story.rb`, `017_TargosExtras.rb`, `006_Dialogue.rb`, `045_MountainClimb.rb`), `script/01.md` (written before the glass goal was built, so its 1.01 and 1.04 are out of date), and `notes/01.md`, `story/01.md`, `dialogue/01.md`. As in 00.md, earlier fixes are pointed to ("notes E1"), not repeated. The Meat Meat trace was re-run during this pass (17:12) and is now a real Meat Meat run; Reptin, Alerion and Wyn are read from the code. Lines marked *(suggestion)* are new.

**What changed since the script was written.** The log now has: the Lantern Glass key item on the pier; Epheris reading the glass, a two-way question ("Can it send me home?" / "Same as theirs?"), Reptin's "Then we're going the same way, dear" and the side goal "Your own: the hag's mirrors at Dougan's Hole"; the goal after Epheris naming the Luskan Arms; Keegan's quest kept as a side goal; and Heralds walking in (`Stage.summon`) for their lines at Epheris, Naerth and the gym, instead of speaking out of other people's bubbles. ## 1. What the player knows coming in

- **New Journey, from part 00 as built:** a winter four hundred years ago; the Heralds "saved the Dale" (none named); a carving of five heroes (snow, flame, lute, wolf, a knife in shadow) and a faceless sixth; Reversal glass from "where the old hag's mirrors broke"; a dock in a blizzard; white. One Pokémon, five Poké Balls, two Potions, no idea how to catch or heal. No goal. So on the pier they don't know where or when they are, or that the voices are the five from the glass. (With 00.md M1 and M3 built, they'd also know two Herald names, that the Heralds broke a hag's mirrors, that mirror-glass keeps what it saw, and that they fell "into the Heralds' winter".)
- **Herald Mode (Alberica, Reptin, Alerion):** their relic came unasked and they're on a ship to Targos; nothing about why, or about the other four (right: they're strangers until 1.03), or whose winter this is.
- **Meat Meat:** Razzy is gone, footprints "going north"; five cold strangers. She's lived in Targos three weeks, yet the game sends her "up the hill" to the tavern like a newcomer.
- **Wyn:** fell through a mirror shard from Rootspire's year 400. Wyn knows the Heralds' legend in-world; the player playing Wyn has never been told it (00.md H5).

### B1 · Where and when: the landing (b, inside 1.01)

**Told, never shown:** that you've arrived anywhere in particular. The chapter assumes the player knows they're in Targos, in the Dale, in the past. Seger's setup rule is who, where, when, in the opening image (pp. 37–39); this is the opening image of the whole Act. Despain: open on the moment of change, and the newcomer frame (Walsh, pp. 101–107) works only if the newcomer is told *where* they are new. The time is said in the kid's terms ("the winter from Hellen's stories"), not as a number, because the number is Chapter 6's line ("snow in your hair that fell four hundred years ago") and should stay Hellen's.

**Where:** `scene_targos_arrival`, Journey branch (`007_Story.rb:157-167`). **On screen:** you at (33,21); with notes E1, Alerion at (33,22) catching you, Shimmer and Alberica walking off west along the pier. **Camera:** fade in on you; hold. **Music:** silence for boxes 1–3 (wind only), then Mt. Silver. Show the map-name banner ("Targos") on the fade-in.

### B2 · They're the Heralds: the recognition (b, inside 1.03)

**Never shown:** the moment the Journey player realises who these strangers are. It's the payoff of the whole prologue, and as built it doesn't happen; the game's first line that calls them Heralds to their faces is Hellen's, in Chapter 6 (`024_Chapter6.rb:324`). That leaves five chapters in which the player walks with "five strangers" and the menu calls them Heralds without explanation. Despain's order is tell, show, do, *feel* (Walsh, pp. 94–97): this is the feel. Berger asks that a critical beat sit on the spine and be repeated through different delivery methods (p. 90): here it's the carving (00 M1), the glass (00 M3), the lute on the pier (B1), and now the name.

**Where:** `scene_tavern_convergence` (`007_Story.rb:197-209`), after Alberica sets down the pendant, and after Reptin's introduction. Journey only; Wyn's version is in §6. **On screen:** as built, the five at their tables. **Action:** a "!" over you (`Stage.emote($game_player, :exclaim)`) before box 2; your sprite turns to face Alberica. **Music:** as built; if a Heralds cue exists (00.md M1), bring it in under box 2 and out at the toast.

| 3 | (built) | *She sets a pendant of black ice on the table.* |
| 4–6 | (built: the pendant, "Men, mostly", Reptin's name) | ... |

| 8 | after "Ghost story." / "I need another drink.", before Shade turns to you *(suggestion)* | *...They're the Heralds. And nobody's carved them yet.* |

Three short narration lines, no input, and no character says it, because no character in Targos *can* know it. That's the point: only the player knows, which is dramatic irony the rest of Act I can use. Box 8 also plants the sixth figure ("nobody's carved them yet") without saying "you're the sixth". Then Shade's "And you? You came off no ship I saw." lands as a real question: the player is now hiding something.
