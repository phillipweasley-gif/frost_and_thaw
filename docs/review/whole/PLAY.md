# Whole-game read, reviewer P: playability (0.8.1)

Every mode and all three endings start, run and finish. Nothing stops a player in normal play. One soft-lock exists at the throne if a save loads without its running event, and one Journey choice does nothing although the game says it will count. The rest are balance spikes, loop and skip side effects, text that promises the wrong thing, and gaps in what the test driver covers. **Block 0, High 2, Medium 8, Low 7.**

Evidence: static passes over the scripts and map events (my scratch: `p_flags.py`, `p_mapscripts.rb`, `p_items.rb`, a switch scan), `tools/check_all.rb` (all 159 maps pass), and new 0.8.1 runs on `pifrun_g` / `pifrun_G1`:
- a full New Journey run, and a full Herald Meat Meat run with her own Noibat (not `HL_STARTER=1`). In both runs the autosave before the god choice writes a real save (`p_trace_save.rb`).
- that save, loaded the way the load screen loads it, then each of the three endings picked (`p_reload.rb`). After Shatter and after the Third Way the run continues postgame to the White Vault and finishes it.
- the Still Keeper loop, replaying the prologue and Act I without the skip (`p_loop.rb`, 44/44 beats), plus `try_loop.rb` (all ok).
- a full Herald Alerion run, which no 0.8.0 trace covers.
- Noibat against Act I's Ice fights, with and without the Blanket, compared with the Journey starters (`p_noibat.rb`).

## Checked and fine

**Endings, loop and Vault.**
- Loading the autosave (interpreter kept) asks the god choice exactly once, in Journey and in Herald Meat Meat. Each ending then plays:
  - Shatter and the Third Way reach "THE END", set `game_complete`, merge the sigils, add the White Vault side goal, and land you in Rootspire. The Vault can be finished from both, and the side goal clears.
  - The Still Keeper loops: Journey back to the Hearth, Herald to the Targos pier.
- The loop doesn't trap you. The Act I, Act II and Act III skips chain (Herald loop: Targos, then Rootspire, then the Hall). Declining the skip replays the prologue and Act I cleanly (44/44), with no second starter. The Act III skip needs a loop that reached the Hall, and it always has. The White Vault is unreachable inside the loop, by design ("Find the third way").
- Losing to Avarice at the throne doesn't white out. Auril's forms keep their progress across a loss (`state[:auril_stage]`).

**Modes and runs.**
- Herald Meat Meat with her own Noibat finished 236/237 with 8 retries, the same as with `HL_STARTER=1`. In the 2-Pokémon fights at the foe's ace level minus one, the Noibat (with or without the Blanket) did as well as or better than the Journey starters, and every team lost the Ice bosses. The Blanket gives no measurable edge or harm in those fights.
- Herald Alerion (0.8.1): 236/237 with 7 retries (Hellen ×2; Mira, Thredra and Eline once each). The only miss is the known Ch11 sigil check, and the autosave dumps cleanly.
- The trial week can't run out. Kelyn waits for Selrae's trial.
- No `log(...)` failures in today's runs: only staging notices, the Sephek re-arm and the player-move safety net. No GAME ERROR, no STUCK. Map 2072's gate misses are the driver.

**Maps and flags.**
- `check_all.rb` passes all 159 maps. Every scene trigger is an autorun page, and none is a touch tile you could walk round.
- The Glasswater crossing has a way back (`beyond_return`, 047b:763).
- Rootspire's retired flags are read nowhere. The Ten-Towns side goals have pointers in each town.
- Every blessing granted is read (except the text in #9).
- Every usable story medicine has a handler. The Mirror Lens works (046:1283).
- The bag-limit overflow goes to the PC, and key items are unlimited.
- Full-party gifts go to the PC.
