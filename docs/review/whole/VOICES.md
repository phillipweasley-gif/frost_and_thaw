# Whole-game read: voices and continuity (reviewer V, 0.8.1)

All seven traces in `traces_080/` read start to end, each Herald Mode against Journey line by line, plus the companion chatter tables. The voices hold up well. Meat Meat never slips out of third person, and no NPC copy speaks a player-Herald's line. What's left: 11 High, 11 Medium and 8 Low findings.
