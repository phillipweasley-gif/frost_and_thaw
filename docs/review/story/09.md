Sources: `docs/review/script/09.md` (9.01-9.11), `docs/review/notes/09.md`, and the bible's Chapter 9 (plus Ch10 beat 1, since the arrest plays here). The leaves-fall scene (SC 9.01) sits at the end of script 08, as 8.27. ## 1. The chapter in one line

**As the player would put it:** *Can we catch the dragon before it reaches Bryn Shander?* **The answer:** *Yes. It falls in the square, Frostina shields the crowd, and the city we saved arrests us for what it cost.*

**Does the game ever say the question?** Never as a question. The nearest are "RAZZILITH: (a painted thought: the black dragon, and teeth) ...CHASE." (9.03), and "MEAT MEAT: Then Meat Meat knows where it goes next. Meat Meat will be there first." (9.04). Bryn Shander then raises the stakes in words: "ALERION: It isn't here to burn Bryn Shander. It's here to collect it." (9.07).

**Does it answer the question the last chapter raised?** Yes, directly. Chapter 8 ends "Somewhere far above, something huge is flying." and the goal "The dragon is loose." The bible's question, "Can we stop the dragon?", is answered yes in 9.07. Then the chapter hands off better than any other in my parts. "KELYN: Half of them think you saved them. Half think you killed it." (9.10) *is* Ch10's question, "Were we right?", said out loud by the person who'll judge it.

## 7. Game fit

- **Story as reward, or as a wall?** The flight is told, not played. Berger's lean-forward rule (p. 45) is broken by the chapter's premise as built: a chase in which the player stands still on a sunny map (notes E3). The seams mechanic is the right instinct. Story responds to how well you fought (Berger pp. 37-38).
- **Scene/sequel (Mills pp. 108-109).**
  - The chase is a clean scene: goal (catch it), conflict (the dragon), disaster (it turns its head: the cold hand).
  - The cold hand is a model sequel: reaction ("Frostina falters"), dilemma (how to reach him), decision (your choice).
  - The climax lacks one. The dragon dies, the face says "Pick me up", "No.", and the next box is "The square is quiet." There's no reaction to the villain's survival, and the arrest has no sequel at all before the Tribunal (notes E5).
- **Conflict vs gameplay.** The story says a city-killing engine of war. The gameplay is one wild battle, single, against four standard moves, starting at half HP. The design's partner battle with Malevolent Presence (notes E10) is what would make the challenge match the stakes (Walsh in Despain).
- **Meaningful choice.** One real choice (the cold hand) and one false one ("Take off?", where No soft-locks: Berger's false choice, p. 36). Bryn Shander, the place where a choice between two rights belongs (Mills p. 129), has none.

## 8. Verdict

**What's working**
1. **The Shrine (9.03).** Glimmer's last words delivered, a hum that stops halfway, two bonds, and a carving that turns lore into a job title. "Congratulations on the new job titles. I assume there's no pay." / "I didn't apply."
2. **The cold hand (9.06).** The theme becomes the villain's weapon ("Stillness is easier."), and the answer is the player's: a name, or Shimmer's notes.
3. **The hand-off to Ch10.** "Var was a coat!" turns the game's best motif into a defence, and "Half of them think you saved them. Half think you killed it." states the next chapter's question.
4. **Tone rhythm.** It alternates warmth and cold scene by scene, as the bible asks.

**Top 3 story problems**

1. **The emergency waits for the player, so cause and effect breaks.**
   - *Why it hurts:* Ch8 ends with the dragons "already diving". Ch9 opens with a walk, two omens, a lift and a wild battle, and finds them "very still" on the stump. Momentum is each scene carrying the seed of the next (Seger pp. 79-80). Here the seed (the launch) is dropped for four scenes.
   - *Fix:* the twins land in the market as the leaves fall, and 9.03 plays there. Fold Alerion's omen into the bond (Alberica sees his shadow freeze as Frostina touches him, which is where "I'll watch her" answers "Sentinel of silence"). Move Meat Meat's omen to her trial.
   - *Cite:* Seger pp. 79-82; Mills p. 192 (the ticking clock).
2. **The player is a passenger in a chase.**
   - *Why it hurts:* a rider behind a Dragoon, two narrated flights, one choice. Seger p. 232; Berger p. 94.
   - *Fix* [suggestions]:
     - Make the Bryn Shander battle a double with your dragon as partner (the design's).
     - Before it, give the player Mills's choice between two rights (p. 129): "Draw it off over the lake" (the square is safe, and the dragon fights at full fury) or "Hold the square under the wings" (the crowd is shielded, but the drain goes on, and Frostina's breath is spent defending). Different starting conditions, the same fall.
     - In the air, let the player pick which seam the Dragoon dives at (a three-way choice whose reward is the seam's damage).
   - *Cite:* Berger pp. 92-94; Seger p. 251.
3. **The climax's emotions crowd each other, and the arrest has no breath.**
   - *Why it hurts:* Anduin's reunion is spliced into the seconds before the dragon lands. Avarice's survival gets one word ("No.") and no reaction. The arrest cuts straight into the Tribunal, which reads the same charges again. Mills asks that the sequel match the scene's intensity (p. 108). Here the biggest scene has three half-sequels and the reversal has none.
   - *Fix:* move Anduin to after the fall. Give the party two boxes for Avarice's face (notes E12). End 9.10 on the arrest, then a night in the root-cells [S: one box of Meat Meat and Alerion through the bars], and then the Tribunal.
   - *Cite:* Mills pp. 108-109; Seger p. 144.

### Rewrite sketch: Bryn Shander, 9.07 → 9.08

Keeps the owner's lines where marked. **[S]** marks a suggestion.

3. **The player's choice [S]:** draw it off over the lake / hold the square under the wings. Each choice gets one line from the Dragoon ("Single file." for the lake, from his sample lines; Meat Meat's "Meat Meat holds. Meat Meat is a wall."). *Changes:* the player decides how the town is saved.

5. **The battle: a double with your dragon.** The seams carry over; the lake choice skips Frostina's opening breath but stops the drain; the square choice keeps both. *The player acts.*
6. **The burst; the wings over the crowd** (an effect on the dragon actor). "The ring breaks on them like a wave on a sea-wall." *Changes:* awe.
7. **The shard.** "AVARICE: (from the shard) Pick me up." / Meat Meat stamps: "No." / Alerion: "That was her face." / Alberica, very quietly: "Mirrors keep things, pretty boy." *Changes:* a chill that the next chapter can use. The trial then charges them with killing someone they know is alive.
8. **Control returns. "The square is quiet. Snow falls on the black glass."** The player walks the square. *The player acts.*
9. **Anduin, found by walking.** He turns in the crowd at Reptin's voice. "...Anduin. My husband." / "Five weeks." / "What have you become?" The hooded woman pulls her hood lower as you pass. The refugees each have a line of their own (notes E17). *Changes:* the reunion gets its own scene, not the seconds before an impact.
10. **Duvessa at the south gate: "Bryn Shander! Pack what you can carry!" → "Lead them south?"** → 9.09. *Changes:* the next goal comes from a person.
