# Part 14 · Side: the Ten-Towns quests (notes)

The quests themselves are rich, branching and well written: a dog that fetches you to a frightened husband, a yeti mother you can talk down, a ghost riding a kobold, a moose that spies through a mirror, a murderer who names his goats. The trouble is getting to them and coming back from them. No quest-giver ever moves, turns, shows a "!" or walks up to you: the six quest files contain no staging at all, so every "runs at you", "stumble toward you", "one of them waves" and "crowd round" is text over a still map. The Goal menu has one slot, which side quests overwrite and later restore to a stale value (Foaming Mugs never sets it and never clears it). Four of the ten rumours point to towns that don't exist. And once a quest is done, nobody outside its own town ever mentions it again: no Herald, no speaker, no spine scene reads any quest's "done" flag. Severity counts: **High 7 · Medium 20 · Low 6** (33 editor findings), plus 18 first-time-player questions, 46 dialogue rewrites and 327 over-long boxes split (appendix).

Almost none of this part was played in the run (see the script's Sources), so every finding is from the code and the map builders, and staging claims are (code).

**E1 · High · every quest · Nobody comes to you.** The words describe the givers approaching ("A wolf-sized sled dog ... runs at you", "Three stocky figures ... stumble toward you", "Two bundled-up figures are talking outside the tavern. One of them waves.", "A figure ... comes floundering out of the woods", "The townsfolk crowd round"). On the map nobody moves: `045_FoamingMugs.rb`, `045_MountainClimb.rb`, `045_Termalaine.rb`, `045_WhiteMoose.rb`, `045_MeadMustFlow.rb` and `045_TenTowns.rb` contain no `Stage.*` call at all except the Council's `Stage.cleanup` (`045_TenTowns.rb:448`). The player has to walk up to a still sprite and press A, then read that it ran at them. A Pokémon game finds you: a trainer turns, "!", walks over. **Fix:** for each giver, one step-on row three or four tiles out (like a trainer's sight line) that plays once: `Stage.emote(giver, :exclaim)`, `Stage.approach(giver)`, then the giver's first lines. Per quest: Boy barks (SE) and runs up (E2); Hruna steps out from the other two and raises her gloved hand; Oarus turns and waves (`Stage.face` + a hop) as you pass the Blue Clam; Darmo's shout plays as you enter the square (E10); the Good Mead townsperson walks up (E11); Fef stumbles in from the west edge (E11).

**E2 · High · 14.04 · Boy, the hook for the longest quest, reads as a wild Pokémon running about.** He is `Followers/MIGHTYENA` with `move_type: 1, move_frequency: 5, move_speed: 4` (`build_act1.rb:138-141`): a black-and-grey hyena-wolf with red eyes, darting at random round the lower streets the moment you land, while the spine is pushing you to the tavern. The text says "light grey fur" and "a broken leather harness dragging behind it". Nothing marks him as someone to talk to, and the same sprite is a trainer's Pokémon on the tundra and the starving wolves in Part 04. **Fix:** a sled-dog sprite (a husky build: thick double coat, curled tail, a harness strap drawn on), slower (frequency 3, speed 3), and E1's trigger: when you come within three tiles, a bark SE, "!", he runs to you, then `Stage.walk_to` the Velryn door three steps and looks back (the tug). Put a sign on the house: *THE VELRYNS. Scrimshaw.*

**E3 · High · 14.04-2 · Walk into the Velryn house without Boy and the scene describes a door and a dog that aren't there.** `keegan` (`045_MountainClimb.rb:177`) always opens with "A handsome man in his thirties opens the door. The dog races past him inside." You came in through the door yourself, and Boy is still in the street. **Fix:** branch on `SW_MC_BOY_TAKEN`: without Boy, *A man sits by the fire, a cup of cider going cold in his hands.* / *He looks up as if you might be someone else.* And add one line so the quest can still start: **KEEGAN:** My husband's dog came home without him. He won't stop running the streets.

**E4 · High · Goal menu · One slot, overwritten and restored stale.** `state[:goal]` holds a single string. Mountain Climb, the mine, the moose, the mead and the election save the spine goal on "yes" and put it back on completion (`045_MountainClimb.rb:205,579`, `045_Termalaine.rb:238,604`, `045_WhiteMoose.rb:194,227`, `045_MeadMustFlow.rb:167,613`, `:652,779`). Any spine scene in between writes its own goal over the side goal, and finishing the quest then restores the old spine goal: take the climb in Targos ("Win Speaker Naerth's seal"), win the seal and reach Bryn Shander, finish the climb, and the menu says to win Naerth's seal again. Two quests break it outright: Foaming Mugs sets nothing on "yes" and sets "Bring the Battlehammer iron to Hruna, in Bryn Shander." at the sled (`045_FoamingMugs.rb:243`), which nothing ever clears; the White Moose sets nothing on "yes", and if you kill the moose before Nimsy asks, "Take the white moose's head to Speaker Huddle in Lonelywood." (`045_WhiteMoose.rb:602`) stays after you hand it in (no `moose_goal_prev` to restore). **Fix:** stop writing side quests into the spine's goal. Keep a list `state[:side_jobs]` (key → line), shown under the Goal on the pause menu as "Also:"; add on "yes", update at each step, remove on completion. If one slot must stay, restore with a function that recomputes the spine goal from flags rather than a saved string.

**E5 · High · 14.04-5 · Finding Garret without Keegan's ask leaves a goal you may never clear.** `fallen_climber` sets "Up the mountain: a cave above, where the yeti came from. Find Mokingo, Perilou and Astrix." (`045_MountainClimb.rb:378`) whether or not the quest was taken. If you then go home and answer Keegan "No" (`:229-231`), the goal stays for the rest of the act; if you say yes, `keegan_after_garret` saves that side goal as the one to restore (`:237`), so finishing the climb puts "Up the mountain..." back. **Fix:** with E4's list; without it, set the goal at `:378` only when `climb_taken`, and save the spine goal before Garret's scene, not at Keegan's door.

**E6 · Medium · 14.03 · The rumour table points at towns that aren't in the game, first.** Each teller gives the next rumour in turn, starting at the top, so the first thing any player hears is Bremen's lake monster (`045_TenTowns.rb:61`). Four of the ten (Bremen, Caer-Dineval, Caer-Konig, Easthaven, `:61-72`) are towns you can't reach (Easthaven's road is "closed", Part 04), and they never drop off. Rumour 9, the one that should send you to Keegan, names nobody: "In Targos, a band of adventurers set off up Kelvin's Cairn, hunting Oyaminartok." / "Hurry, you might catch them up!" (`:77-78`). The table never mentions the content that *is* there: Marta's ring, Copper's friend, Ol' Bitey, the Council, Ramshackle. The Lucky Liar's teller is tagged "LIAR" on an event named "Fisher (rumours)", so the bubble finds nobody (`build_tentowns.rb:1129`). **Fix:** replace the four with in-game leads (dialogue table), point rumour 9 at Keegan by name, order the list so the nearest open quest comes first (`ten_towns_rumor` can skip rumours for towns you haven't reached), and pass the event's own name as the tag.

**E7 · Medium · 14.05 · Foaming Mugs is lost forever on one "No", on a night you're told not to leave.** "No" plays "They stump away in a huff ... You don't see them again." and switch 4049 deletes them (`045_FoamingMugs.rb:101-104`). It's the only quest with no second chance, and it's offered on lottery day, when the guard has just said "On a draw night nobody leaves" (14.05 TIMING), so "No" is the answer a careful player gives. **Fix:** on "No", move them into the Northlook (an NPC page on switch 4049 at a table) with **HRUNA:** Hmph. We'll be at the Northlook, drinking to Oobok. Find yer spine, come find us. And have Hruna acknowledge the draw: **HRUNA:** Not tonight, aye. The sled'll keep till morning. The goblins won't.

**E8 · Medium · 14.07 · The Council is the best "what the towns think" scene and nothing leads to it.** Already logged in Part 02 (E23): no goal, NPC or Herald points at the Council Hall, and it expires once you reach Termalaine or Lonelywood (`045_TenTowns.rb:401-402`). From the side-quest angle it is also the only place you meet Oarus and Kendrick before their quests, and both quests then forget it (E26). **Fix:** Part 02's (Duvessa's last line points there; the clerk stands at the hall door in the square), plus E26's callbacks.

**E9 · Medium · 14.12 · The moose job is hidden behind the rest prompt, and comes at the worst moment.** `nimsy` only appends the ask once `rested_lonelywood` is set (`045_WhiteMoose.rb:153-156`): the first chance is the morning after the night Shimmer is taken, and it plays after "Rest in Nimsy's attic?", whatever you answer. Its first box is cheerful ("NIMSY: Before you go. (She offers the cookie plate again.) Our loggers are being terrorised by a white moose,", `:166`) straight after a companion was abducted from her attic, and nobody mentions Shimmer. Meanwhile the forest's logger and hunter talk about the moose to anyone walking the spine to the Glade (14.12-2 ORDER), so the player meets the problem before anyone offers the job, with no way to take it. **Fix:** Nimsy seeds it on the first visit (one line, no ask: "NIMSY: And mind the logging road. We've a moose problem. Ask me about it when you're settled."); after Shimmer, open with her grief first (dialogue table); the logger adds "Speaker Huddle's offering a bounty for it. Ask her."

**E10 · Medium · 14.09 · Termalaine's two hooks wait to be found.** Darmo stands on his box shouting the news "at people who don't stop", but only when you press A on him (`045_Termalaine.rb:207-211`); the arrival trigger that frames the town sits on the east side (Part 03), so a player from the south gate can cross the square without hearing anything. Oarus "waves" (`:223`) with no movement. **Fix:** a step-on row across the square's south entrance that plays Darmo's first shout with his "!" and a hop (no stop), so the news finds you; Oarus turns and waves as in E1.

**E11 · Medium · 14.13 · The Mead offer is told by nobody, and Fef arrives while standing still.** `mead_quest_offer` (`045_MeadMustFlow.rb:158-169`) is narration plus quotes with no speaker tag, so no bubble lands on anyone; "The townsfolk crowd round" over one wandering NPC. If you talk to Fef first, the same townsfolk offer plays from him, then "A figure in cold-weather clothing comes floundering out of the woods through the deep snow" (`:176`) while he stands in the street, with the fox already loose beside him (14.13 ACTION) and the offer saying "Fef the trapper went that way this morning" in front of him (`:165`). **Fix:** give the offer to a named speaker (a beekeeper or the gossip, `GOSSIP:`); stage two or three townsfolk walking up; play Fef's arrival as a scene on the west edge (he walks in from (0,27) with the fox in a trap, `Stage.approach`), or rewrite his lines for a man already home (dialogue table).

**E12 · Medium · 14.13-5 · Nothing sends you to Olivessa.** The election only starts if you go back into the cooperage and talk to her after the mead is home (`045_MeadMustFlow.rb:620-621`). The homecoming doesn't mention that Good Mead has no speaker, and nobody names her. When you do find her, she introduces herself again ("OLIVESSA: My name's Olivessa." `:626`) though she fed you on arrival (`022_Chapter4.rb:412`). The vote runs on rests you can't see counting, except the "(Good Mead votes in about N days...)" note. **Fix:** the brewmistress at the homecoming: "BREWMISTRESS: Now the town needs a speaker. Olivessa at the cooperage wants a word with you." (sets a side-job line); Olivessa opens with "OLIVESSA: The honey-cake strangers. Sit. I've a favour to ask." and the election side-job shows the days left.

**E13 · Medium · rewards · Rewards promise what the game doesn't do.** (Korux's snowshoes are fine: they are the running shoes.) Hilda's Boots of the Winterlands "never slip on ice" (`045_HoledUp.rb:32`): ice sliding only checks the Blessing of Still Sky (`027_Relics.rb:319-325`), never the boots. He cut the wood for my forge. I'll hang it over the door."); Astrix's book to the Velryns (Keegan sends it to Frozenfar); the Psi Crystal is a hook the mirror can name for a later act.

**E14 · High · every quest · Nobody outside the quest's town ever reacts.** The quests' "done" flags (`climb_done`, `mugs_done`, `mine_done`, `white_moose_done`, `mead_returned`, `council_session`) are read only by the quest's own people (Owenn, Garn and Elza, Darmo, the loggers) and by the rumour table: no file outside the seven `045_*.rb` reads any of them. **E15 · Medium · every quest · Finishing has no signal.** No quest ends with a jingle or a "done" line; the goal silently changes (sometimes to the wrong thing, E4). ### Staging and animation

**E18 · High · the quests' climaxes · The biggest moments happen to nobody on screen.** Gahg, the verbeeg whose four-way choice ("Stand aside." / "Stop her." / "Run." / "Hide back in the cave.") ends the mead quest, has no event at all (14.13-4); her torch, Duhg's torch and the reunion are text. The father yeti "blocks out the light" with no sprite (14.04-7); the crag cats are "suddenly much closer" and never appear (14.04-5); the white moose "emerges from under the hillside ... and walks straight toward your hiding place" on a map with no moose (14.12-3); the goblins, Izobai and both bears vanish in one frame while the wagon "goes up like a bonfire" with no fire (14.05-3); the chwinga "climbs onto the fox's back" and rides off when there's no fox on the map (14.13-2); the thugs, Shandar and five loggers fill the mead hall for nine boxes and none of them is there (14.13-5). **Fix:** `Stage.actor` for each, at minimum: Gahg walking in from the treeline (BW giant or the verbeeg sprite, E20) and `Stage.leave` into the cave; the father yeti in the cave mouth with `Stage.emote(yeti, :angry)`; two crag-cat actors walking in to two tiles from Garret; the moose actor walking out of the hole to the dial and toward the lookout; goblins `Stage.leave` in six directions (the text says so) and a fire effect on the wagon (`Stage.fx` with a new fire sheet; art request); the fox and chwinga leaving together; the thugs and loggers in the hall, then `Stage.leave` out the door.

**E19 · Medium · many scenes · Speakers with no body, and tags that miss.** Scorp and Thwip speak under the walkway with no sprites (14.11); Janth's "pale shape tears itself up out of him" with no actor; Sahnar speaks from a sarcophagus before he exists; Garret warns you on the third pitch while not on the map (`045_MountainClimb.rb:398`), and if he "comes with you" he is invisible for the rest of the quest; Perilou kneels beside Astrix on a map she isn't on; Duhg's bubble points nine tiles away into his tunnel during the livestock threat; Mishann answers "(from below)" from two tiles away in the same room. Tag mismatches: "SHRUB:" on an event named "Berry bush", "LIAR:" on "Fisher (rumours)", "FEF:" mid-box after narration, "SHANDAR:" and "THUG:" in a mead hall that has neither. (The Heralds themselves are absent everywhere: Part 02 E1.) **Fix:** stand-in actors for every speaker (`Stage.actor` with the right sprite, `cleanup` after); name the events to match their tags ("Berry bush (SHRUB)", "Fisher (LIAR)"); start a speaker's line on its own box.

**E20 · Medium · many maps · The sprites contradict the words.** The yeti mother and father are `Followers/URSARING`, a brown bear, and the tyke is `TEDDIURSA` (`build_tentowns.rb:40-41`), while staged `YETITAN` and `YETOT` follower sprites already exist in `build/yeti_stage/Graphics/Characters/Followers/`. The "white moose" is `STANTLER` (`:787`), a brown deer with round antlers: a moose has broad palmate antlers, a long face, a dewlap under the chin and a shoulder hump. The "white fox" is a red `VULPIX` (`build_ch4.rb:136`); an arctic fox is small, round-eared and white. The verbeeg Duhg is `MACHAMP` (`:987`), four-armed and muscled, for a giant the text calls "huge and lanky and lopsided". The grell, "a huge, pale brain with a beak, trailing long barbed tentacles", is `TANGELA`, a ball of vines. Goblins are `SNEASEL`; crag cats, "big cats, white as the snow", are `PERSIAN`, a cream house cat; Boy is `MIGHTYENA` (E2). People share sprites: Keegan, Oarus (a half-orc), Vernon and Danneth are all BW (54); Hruna, Korux, Storn, the dwarf sentry, Garagai and Kendrick ("a huge man with a beard like a hedge") are all BW (72). **Fix:** swap in the yeti sprites now; art requests for the moose, the arctic fox, the verbeeg and the grell drawn from the real anatomy (and the RotF descriptions); a white recolour of a big-cat sprite for the crag cats; distinct sprites for Oarus (half-orc) and Kendrick (a big human), and for at least one of the three dwarves (Hruna is the one who speaks).

**E21 · Medium · 14.07 · The Council plays 27 boxes with nobody moving, then all five vanish.** The speakers are seated when you step in, and nobody stands, turns or gestures through the scene; "She takes a halfling-shaped cookie" and "Kendrick claps you on the shoulder" are narration; "The speakers file out" is `Stage.cleanup` (`045_TenTowns.rb:448`), one frame. **Fix:** `Stage.face` each speaker to you on their first line; Naerth `Stage.hop` on "I'd say she had help"; Kendrick `Stage.walk_to` you for the shoulder clap; the five `Stage.leave` through the door in turn, behind a fade if needed.

### Structure and pacing

**E23 · Medium · several scenes · Long runs with no input.** Keegan speaks 17 boxes before the yes/no (14.04-2: 10 of his and the narration, 2 Herald, 5 more of his); the Council 27 (14.07); Marta's first visit 17 before "Ask Marta for the ring?" (14.10); Nimsy's moose ask arrives after her own lines and the rest, so a returning player reads the rest, Nimsy's greeting and four boxes of ask before choosing (14.12). WRITING_GUIDE §2.2-2.3: no one speaks more than three boxes in a row, about 25 without input is too long. **Fix:** Keegan: cut "I was born in Ten-Towns", "He lives to climb mountains" and the Frozenfar box (dialogue table), and move the ask before the payment ("Could I persuade you...", then the choice, then what he can pay); Council: E21's staging gives the eye something to do, and a CHOICE after Duvessa's "Or dealt with some other way." ("Say nothing." / "Answer her.") hands the Herald lines to the player; Marta: split the song from the story with a choice ("Ask about the song." / "Ask about the inn.").

**E24 · Medium · 14.12-2 · The moose hunt is a grind of yes/no prompts.** Each search is an hour, finds tracks 50% of the time (80% with Shade), and the white moose is one find in six (the sixth always), with the forest table rolling every three hours (`045_WhiteMoose.rb`, the "Tracks" section of 14.12-2). That is about twelve searches, each a prompt and a box, on four boulders. The fox shortcut needs Meat Meat. **Fix:** make the white tracks the third find (not the sixth), let the hunter or Halvard give a hint once beaten ("HALVARD: It drinks at the frozen stream at dusk. East. I never get there in time."), and offer the fox to everyone (Meat Meat's line is the flavour, not the key).

**E25 · Medium · 14.04-9 · Mountain Climb ends before its promise is kept, and Garret disappears into your pocket.** The homecoming plays once Garret is found and *either* Perilou is saved or Astrix is found (`045_MountainClimb.rb:554-557`), though Garret, Reptin and Keegan all swore "all three"; Mokingo's remains are never brought home or mentioned again after the trophies. If you say "Then come with us", Garret has no sprite and no further lines except the third pitch's (14.04-6). **Fix:** at the homecoming, a box for whoever wasn't found (**GARRET:** And Mokingo. We'll go up for him in the spring. All of us.); a follower or stand-in for Garret on the Cairn maps, or have him wait at base camp ("GARRET: I'll hold the camp. Go.").
