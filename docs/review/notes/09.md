# Part 09 · Chapter 9: Destruction's Light — notes

Script: `docs/review/script/09.md` (scenes 9.01-9.11). Line references are to `game/Data/Scripts/998_FrostThaw/` unless a path is given.

**In one paragraph.** Chapter 9 should be the game's big set piece: two bonded dragons, a chase through the sky, a burning town, a boss over a crowd, a reunion and an arrest. On screen it's a still picture. `035_Chapter9.rb` has no `Stage.*` call. 72 of the 85 boxes with a speaker are spoken by someone who isn't on the map. The flight is told over whichever map you're standing on (the sunny Canopy, then Good Mead). The dragon never appears. Bryn Shander never gives you control. One "No" at the Shrine soft-locks the game. The writing itself is mostly good and in voice. Fixing this is staging work: the lines can mostly stay.

---

## 1. Editor

### High

**E1 · 9.03 · Saying No to "Take off after the black dragon?" soft-locks the game.**
`dragon_shrine` sets `mounted_up` (035_Chapter9.rb:164) before the prompt (:168). If you answer No, the next talk to Frostina hits `return talk(...) if ... flag?(:mounted_up)` (:103) and only plays her idle hum. Nothing else in the codebase calls `sky_chase`, so the chapter can't go on.
*Fix:* re-offer the take-off. At the top of `dragon_shrine`, add `if flag?(:mounted_up) && !flag?(:air_seams) then return sky_chase if pbConfirmMessage(_INTL("Take off after the black dragon?")); return; end`. The other option is to set `mounted_up` only after the yes.

**E2 · Whole chapter · Nobody who talks is on screen.**
There are 46 Herald boxes and no Herald on any map: Alerion 12, Meat Meat 12, Alberica 10, Reptin 8, Wyn 4. Olivessa (4), Duvessa (5), Anduin (5), Kelyn (3 at the arrest), the widow, Avarice and the crowd voices are absent too. Rheven is 41 tiles off camera. In the Council Hall, the game's speaker search finds the Heralds' stone statues ("Statue statue_alberica" etc., `tools/build_ch6.rb:177-180`), so the bubbles would point at statues. This breaks WRITING_GUIDE §9a in every scene, and the biggest emotional beats are words over an empty square: the bond, Anduin, Avarice's face, the arrest.
*Fix:* add `Stage.herald` for every Herald in each scene: the Shrine, Good Mead, Bryn Shander, the walk, the arrest, the Tribunal (in the dock, not the statues). Add actors for the rest. Olivessa walks out of the mead hall door (15,10) behind a bee effect. Duvessa stands on the wall above the square. Anduin turns in the crowd (`Stage.emote :exclaim`) and walks to Reptin. Rheven and two Rootwardens walk in from the east, and Kelyn hurries in after them. In the Tribunal, put the child and her mother in the gallery. Give the statues names that don't match a speaker, e.g. "Carving: the goblin", so `Stage.speaker` can't land on them.

**E3 · 9.04-9.07 · The sky chase and the dragon are never shown.**
The design has a Sky Chase map (act2_script.md:1480). The code tells the take-off, the Wall, the dives and the scream as narration. Meanwhile the screen shows the Canopy-Tier in gold summer light, with your sprite standing in front of a dragon you're supposed to be riding. The second flight plays over Good Mead's square. In Bryn Shander, "The black dragon lands in the square, scattering the crowd" is a screen shake over five refugees standing still. There's no dragon sprite, panel or overlay anywhere in the chapter.
*Fix:* at the minimum, fade to black on "Take off" and run the flight over panels. You need about four: the Wall from the air, the dragon over Good Mead, the Dragoon's dive, the dragon over Bryn Shander. Add a snow overlay and a dark tone for the Rime. In Bryn Shander, a large actor or overlay for the dragon landing, with the refugees `Stage.walk`ing away from it, and Frostina as an actor beside you. Best: build the design's Sky Chase map with a scrolling cloud panorama.

**E4 · 9.01-9.03 · The emergency waits for you.**
Chapter 8 ends with the dragon "rising into the night: toward Ten-Towns" and "Far above, two dragons are already diving". Then a side vault, END OF CHAPTER 8, the leaves falling, a walk across the Trunk-Tier, two omens, the lift and a wild Natu. After all that, "Frostina waits at the Shrine, very still." The dragons said to be diving are standing still, and the town is burning somewhere you can't see. The Echo Gate opens at the same moment (`echo_gate`, 024_Chapter6.rb:241-253, after `wall_thinning`). So you can walk out to the Eastway beside Good Mead, where nothing is burning yet: its switch only turns on mid-flight.
*Fix:* after the leaves fall, have the twins come down into the market. Frostina lands on the cobbles; that's the first `Stage.actor`. Play 9.03 there, no lift. Or transfer straight to the Shrine. Hold the two omens back until after the arrest, turn off wild encounters on the Canopy while `summer_ended && !mounted_up`, and put a Rootwarden at the Echo Gate ("RHEVEN'S WARDEN: Not on foot. Not with that thing out there."). Change the Shrine's first line (see the Dialogue table).

**E5 · 9.07-9.11 · 99 boxes with almost no input, and Bryn Shander never gives you control.**
From landing in Bryn Shander to the Tribunal, the player gets one battle and one yes/no in 99 boxes. The runs are 30 boxes, then the battle, 26 boxes, the yes, then 43 boxes across three maps and the chapter break. WRITING_GUIDE §2.3 says about 25. The chapter header promises "you land in real places … and walk them". You never walk Bryn Shander: Anduin, the hooded woman and the refugees only become events after the scene, and if you say Yes (the default) you leave at once. The arrest cuts straight into the Tribunal, with no fade, no night and no title card for Chapter 10.
*Fix:* end `scene_green_light` on Duvessa's "Pack what you can carry!" and give control. Make the prompt a person: Duvessa at the south gate, "Lead them south?". Then the player can talk to Anduin, the hooded woman and the refugees, who should have five different lines. After "END OF CHAPTER 9", fade to black, show a "Chapter 10: Trials of the Roots" card, and open the Tribunal the next morning ("The next morning, the bells.").

**E6 · 9.11 · The dead Autumn Warden is standing in the Council Hall while he's charged for.**
`tools/build_ch6.rb:173` places "Darrus Fell" at (8,13) with no switch. He stands three tiles from you while "Four chairs wait. Two of them, gold and red, are draped in white." and Kelyn says "The Autumn Warden died by her hand." Talking to him plays his live lines ("DARRUS: Law and trade, Wardens…").
*Fix:* give the event a blank page on `autumn_fell`, or a page that says "Darrus's chair is draped in white.".

### Medium

**E7 · 9.05 · Good Mead burns only in the text.**
The map has its normal music (the Icirrus town theme), no fire, smoke or tint. The townsfolk say their Chapter 4 lines. The beekeeper: "The hives are in the mead hall, by the big fire. Bees hate the Rime as much as we do." Shandar: "Back from the Hole, are you? See anything interesting?" The wandering townsperson still offers *The Mead Must Flow* if you haven't taken it. Olivessa is trapped in the mead hall and also standing in the cooperage (2033, 13,7). After the rescue the mead hall interior is intact, with its rest, shop and "The hive room's the warmest place in town."
*Fix:* add a `SW_CH9_GOODMEAD` page for each townsperson with one panicked line each (beekeeper: "The hives! She went back in for the hives!"). Hide the cooperage Olivessa while the switch is on. Use fire `E-` effects on two roofs, a red-orange tone, crackling `se`, and the chase theme in place of the town theme. Keep the mead hall door shut ("Smoke pours out. Not yet.") until Chapter 10.

**E8 · 9.05 · You can walk away from the chase.**
Good Mead's exits work: Eastway, Redwaters, the woods. Bryn Shander isn't "packed" until after the second flight, so walking there on foot finds the town normal while the dragon is overhead.
*Fix:* put people on the exits while the switch is on. "MILITIA: Road's on fire. Your dragon's in the square." Then step back.

**E9 · 9.04, 9.06, 9.07 · The boss sounds like a wild Pidgey.**
All three dragon fights use HGSS Wild Pokémon Battle (Kanto), the same music as the Natu. "Battle! Kyurem" plays outside battle, during narration, then cuts out for the fight and doesn't come back for the second flight. The Shrine (design: *Heralds*), the green light (*Heralds*, slow) and the arrest (*Rime*) keep the map's music.
*Fix:* set the boss BGM for `boss_battle` here (`setBattleRule("battleBGM", "Battle! Kyurem")` or equivalent). Use a chase or wind theme for the narration, the party's theme at the Shrine and the green light, and cut the music for the arrest.

**E10 · 9.07 · The final battle isn't the designed battle.**
The design says "Your dragon fights beside you as a partner" and gives the dragon Malevolent Presence, which charms a Pokémon (act2_script.md:1579). In the game it's a single wild battle against four standard moves, starting at 51% HP. "Frostina breathes first" happens only in text.
*Fix:* make it a double battle with Frostina (or Razzilith) as an ally, using the partner-trainer rule. At the least, use the battle's opening message to say Frostina is with you, and add a once-per-battle confusion or attraction effect for Malevolent Presence.

**E11 · 9.07 · Losing the final battle sends you back inside the Wall.**
The Shrine moves your rest point to the Hearth (`set_rest_at(HEARTH_REST)`, :167). The final battle can't be lost cleanly (`boss_battle` without `canLose`), so a loss whites you out to Rootspire. You then walk out through the Echo Gate and across the Eastway to Bryn Shander, and the scene replays from the top, Anduin's reunion included.
*Fix:* `setBattleRule("canLose")` with a retry. "Frostina catches you before you hit the cobbles. The dragon is still there." Then heal and refight. Or set the rest point to Bryn Shander's Pokémon Center on landing.

**E12 · 9.07 · Avarice's face appears and nobody reacts.**
Three scenes earlier the party agreed she was dead ("ALBERICA: (very plainly) She doesn't get to come back from that. Not even her."). Now her face says "Pick me up." in a shard, Meat Meat says "No.", and the scene moves to the quiet square. The Council then tries them for killing her, and none of them mentions it.
*Fix:* add two boxes after Meat Meat's "No.". "ALERION: That was her face." / "ALBERICA: (very quietly) It was a reflection, pretty boy. Mirrors keep things." Pay it off in Chapter 10 (a Herald could raise it at the trial).

**E13 · 9.07-9.08 · Anduin arrives as a stranger.**
Before Chapter 9 the log names him once ("REPTIN: (very quietly) Anduin is out there.", Ch6). Nothing says he's Reptin's husband. Here he's "a tall elf in travelling clothes" with "an iron ring", and the reunion is the emotional peak of the scene. A first-time player doesn't know who he is.
*Fix:* make Reptin's line say it: "REPTIN: (not able to move) ...Anduin. My husband." (49 chars). Better, set it up in Chapter 6 or 7 with a line where Reptin turns the ring on his own finger.

**E14 · 9.10-9.11 · The charges are read twice in a row.**
Rheven reads the charges (Var Rime-Breaker and Darrus). Nine boxes later the Tribunal opens and Kelyn reads the same charges again, with no time passing between.
*Fix:* cut Rheven's charge to "RHEVEN: (not meeting your eyes) In the name of the Verdant Council. You're under arrest." Leave the full charges to the Tribunal, and put a night between the two scenes (E5).

**E15 · 9.01-9.02 · Two omens back to back, then a third at the start of part 10.**
Alerion's shadow fires on the walk to the lift and Meat Meat's laugh on the Canopy, a minute apart. Alberica's own omen comes right after the Tribunal. Each is good on its own, but stacked they flatten each other, and they stall the chase (E4).
*Fix:* allow one omen per map visit and per day (`state[:omen_day]`). Keep the Alerion omen for the night before the trial, and the Meat Meat omen for her hearing.

**E16 · 9.03 · The binding is a sound effect.**
"Frost-light runs up his arm under the skin" and "A green glow goes in over her heart" play over a still map with one `Saint7`. The design's showpiece is two dragons choosing their Dragoons.
*Fix:* show Alerion and Meat Meat as actors. Frostina turns to Alerion (`Stage.face`) and a frost effect plays on him. Razzilith hops (`Stage.hop`) into Meat Meat and a green sparkle `E-` plays on her. Then a panel of the carving.

**E17 · 9.08a · The after-scene content is hidden behind a No.**
Anduin's sleeve line, the hooded woman's look and five refugees all saying the same two lines are only reachable if the player refuses to lead the refugees. Most players will say Yes.
*Fix:* see E5. Give control first. Write five distinct refugee lines: a Good Mead beekeeper, a Caer-Konig fisher, an Easthaven child, a Targos dockhand, and someone asking where Rootspire is.

### Side-quest and NPC engagement

Olivessa never appears again in this part. *Fix:* she should be among the refugees in 9.08, with "OLIVESSA: The bees came too. They're in my hat."
- **Refugees:** five people with one shared two-line script, reachable only after a No (E17).
- **The hooded woman (Phildendrin):** a nice hook. She appears only in narration while you're in control of nothing; her event shows up as you leave. Give the player one look at her on the map.
- **The widow and her husband:** a lovely payoff for the Targos Spheal quest, but they aren't on the map and get one recycled line (see the Dialogue table).

## 2. First-time player

**"Who is talking? I can only see myself and two dragons."** In almost every scene the people speaking aren't on screen (E2). Someone new to Pokémon games learns who's who from the sprites, and here there are none. *Fix:* E2.

**"Am I flying? Why am I still standing on the leaves?"** The take-off, the Wall and the dives happen in text while the map doesn't change (E3). *Fix:* fade and panels for the flight.

**"What's a Dragoon, and why does Alerion get one and not me?"** The carving explains the word ("NOT RIDERS. DRAGOONS. GUIDES TO YOUNG DRAGONS."), but not why these two were chosen, or what "a second heartbeat" means for play. *Fix:* one line from Meat Meat after the carving: "MEAT MEAT: Ice Prince is Frostina's. Meat Meat is Razzy's. [Name] rides with us." It names the pairing and the player's place in it.

**"Who's Glimmer, and who is Frostina looking for?"** "for someone who isn't there" leans on Chapter 8 (Shade became Glimmer, and Glimmer is gone). That's fine for a player who's been following, but it reads as a mystery here. *Fix:* "Frostina looks past all of you, at the steps, for Glimmer. He isn't coming." (75 chars)

**"Wait, if this is urgent, why am I walking around the market?"** See E4.

**"What's the Rime, and how has it only been a month?"** "Two years of night, exactly as you left it" and "We've been gone a month!" contradict the 400 years inside the Wall, unless you remember Hellen's explanation from Chapter 6. *Fix:* in Alberica's line, keep "a month" and add Reptin's reply, "REPTIN: (shouting back) Four hundred years in there, a month out here! Lovely to be home!" (89 chars). That restates the rule in passing.

**"Who's 'she' in 'She's right. Let it burn'?"** It's the cold hand in Alerion's mind: Auril? Avarice? The text doesn't say. *Fix:* "ALERION: (flat, wrong) ...Turn back. Winter's right. Let it burn." (65 chars). That ties it to his frost bond and to Auril without naming her.

**"What do the three options in the cold-hand choice do?"** They all work. Nothing tells you the choice matters, though the Hum option quietly adds to a hidden Wheel counter. That's fine, but "Say their name" says "their" about a man you can see is Alerion (Dialogue table).

**"Who's Olivessa / the honey-cake people / Duvessa's seal?"** These are callbacks to Chapter 4 and Chapter 2, with no reminder. *Fix:* each callback carries its own reminder. "OLIVESSA: You! You ate all my honey cake a month ago! Where were you?" (69 chars). "DUVESSA: (on the wall) You. I gave you my seal a month ago. Where have you..." (77 chars).

**"Who is Anduin?"** See E13.

**"Why does the dragon want the crowd? What's the White Womb?"** "A soul harvest … for the White Womb" relies on Selrae's and Avarice's earlier mentions. It's acceptable, but the line is too long (Dialogue table), and "memory-threads" is jargon. *Fix:* the rewrite in the table.

**"That face in the glass was the villain. Isn't she dead?"** See E12.

**"Why am I being arrested? I just saved everyone."** Kelyn answers this ("Half of them think you saved them. Half think you killed it."). "For the deaths of Summer Marshal Var Rime-Breaker" is confusing, though: the player watched Var turn out to be Avarice in a borrowed body. Meat Meat's "Var was a coat!" helps only if you remember. *Fix:* Kelyn's line: "KELYN: (arriving, white-faced) The city saw its Marshal fall. Not what was inside him." (86 chars)

**"What's an Auroral Mote? Why did I get it?"** See E19.

**"Who are Kelyn and Selrae?"** They were introduced in Ch6, and the Tribunal names them only by speaker tag. *Fix:* the first Tribunal box says it: "KELYN OF SPRING:" as the tag for her first line, and "SELRAE, WINTER'S SEAT:" for Selrae's.

**"Where do I go after the arrest?"** The goal says it, but the scene cuts to the Tribunal at once (E5). A new player has no moment to find out what "under watch, but free to move" means. *Fix:* a night in the root-cells, then the morning.
