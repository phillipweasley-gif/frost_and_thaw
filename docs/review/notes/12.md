# Part 12 · Chapter 12: The Sea of Moving Ice — review notes

Read with `docs/review/script/12.md`. Scene numbers (12.01-12.17) are that script's. Line references are to
`game/Data/Scripts/998_FrostThaw/` unless a path is given.

**In one paragraph.** Part 12 carries three big beats: Avarice's return in the White Vault, Tamsin's goodbye, and
the crossing to Auril's island. The writing in each is mostly good and in voice. None of it is staged. No Herald is
ever on the map (65 of 71 named lines are logged absent), Tamsin and Hellen speak at an empty Pidgeot sprite, the
voyage that names the chapter is eleven boxes of text over the Rootspire canopy, and Avarice talks for nine boxes
in a row at a player who sees nothing change. Around those beats the chapter is a long backtrack with almost
nothing to do (Vault to Canopy-Tier: seven maps, one stale NPC), and two of its systems (Auril's relics and the
Vault's circuits) are invisible to anyone who doesn't already know they matter.

**Findings:** 26 editor findings (5 High, 10 Medium, 11 Low), 16 first-time-player questions, 42 dialogue rows.

## 1. Editor

### Staging and animation

**E1 · High · Every Herald scene in the part: the speakers are never on screen.** 65 of the 71 named lines are
`[offstage] … absent` (relics ×3, Vault gate, circuits, cradle, Tamsin, voyage, camp). None of 038_Chapter12.rb or
047c_WhiteVault.rb calls `Stage.*`, though `FT_alberica`, `FT_reptin`, `FT_alerion`, `FT_meat_meat`, `FT_wyn`,
`FT_tamsin`, `FT_hellen` and `FT_avarice` sprites all exist. The player sees themselves and a tile while five people
argue. WRITING_GUIDE §9a makes this a hard rule.
*Fix:* open every scene in this part with `Stage.herald(key, dx, dy, dir)` for each companion in `companions`
(two tiles behind and beside you), `Stage.face` them toward whoever speaks, and `Stage.cleanup` at the end. Give
the three set pieces more: at the cradle Reptin `walk`s one step in front of Meat Meat on "No. Not her."; at the
circuits each Herald steps to their ring before their relic line; at the camp they sit round the stove (a
`Stage.pose` row).

**E2 · High · 12.14 Fog and masts: the chapter's namesake is never shown.** The whole crossing (11 boxes:
griffons, fog, sunken masts, the snowflake island, the crowned skull) plays as narration while the screen shows
the Canopy-Tier roost in Rootspire, with Azalea Town still playing. There is no fade at take-off and no panel; the
first image of Solstice is the dock after a plain transfer. The design (act3_script.md SC 12.02) asks for "new
Sea of Moving Ice (travel sequence)", music *Rime*.
*Fix:* fade out on "Set out for Solstice?"; play the scene over full-screen panels (`Stage.panel`): S- griffons
over pack ice, S- masts in fog, S- the snowflake island with the skull, one per two or three boxes; change the music
on the fade. If panels aren't ready, transfer first to a small sea map (open water, ice floes, wreck masts as
events) with the party as actors on a griffon follower, then transfer to the dock.

**E3 · High · 12.13 Tamsin: the rival's last battle has no rival.** "TAMSIN: (blocking the way…) Hold it." is said
by nobody on the map; the player is facing a Pidgeot. No "!" bubble, no walk-up, no rival theme (the battle uses
`gymleader`, not Tamsin's theme). Hellen then speaks "(packing food into every bag you own)" from the Hearth, three
tiers below, and the Full Restores arrive after Wyn's line, so they read as Wyn's gift.
*Fix:* `Stage.actor(:tamsin, …)` beside the roost; on the first line `Stage.emote(t, :exclaim)` and `Stage.walk` two
steps to block you; `Stage.theme(:tamsin)` from "Hold it" to the battle. After the battle, Hellen comes up off the
lift (`Stage.actor(:hellen)` at the lift tile, walk to you) and gives the Full Restores right after her own line:
"HELLEN: Eat first. Then heroics. (She presses three Full Restores into your hands.)" (038_Chapter12.rb:111-116).

**E4 · High · 12.08 Avarice in every mirror: a nine-box monologue nobody answers, and nothing on screen.** After
Alberica's one line Avarice has boxes 155-171 to herself (WRITING_GUIDE §2.2: no one speaks more than three boxes
in a row). Each whisper is aimed at a Herald, and not one of them answers (Voices rule 3). "Every mirror shows her"
plays with no tint, flash, sprite or music change; Lavender Town has been playing since the Glasswater and keeps
playing through the reveal.
*Fix:* after each "(to X)" whisper give that Herald one short reply (rewrites in the Dialogue table: Alerion,
Reptin, Wyn, Meat Meat), so the run becomes Avarice/Herald pairs. Stage it: `$game_screen.start_tone_change` to a
cold silver, then `Stage.actor(:avarice, …, graphic: "FT_avarice")` at low opacity on each of the four
`maze_pane` tiles (14,9 / 18,9 / 14,15 / 18,15), all facing you; cut the music on "Hello again." and bring in her
theme.

**E10 · Medium · 12.10 The Seraph Cradle: Avarice is on the map but gets no bubble; the narration does what the
sprite doesn't.** The event is named "Avarice's cradle" (tools/build_w4.rb:401); `Stage.speaker` splits that into
"avarice's" and "cradle" and never matches the tag AVARICE, and `scene_vault_cradle` is called without
`@event_id`, so the fallback can't help (047c_WhiteVault.rb:350). Meanwhile she "sits up", "stretches", a needle
"slides out" toward Meat Meat, a case of ice holds the core, wards fall: none of that is on the map.
*Fix:* name the event "Avarice" (or pass `@event_id` into `scene_vault_cradle`); `Stage.hop` her on "sits up";
`Stage.fx` a glint for the needle at Meat Meat's stand-in; put the case of ice as a second event behind her that
switches to empty when the core is given; an `E-` shatter on "Her crystal body cracks".

**E11 · Medium · 12.07 and 12.02-12.04: enemies appear only in narration.** "Two things of mirror and iron stir:
Mirror Sentinels", "a woman of rime, faceless": the battles start out of a text box with nothing on the map. The
relic tile stays a mirror after "goes to black water".
*Fix:* `Stage.actor` the two sentinels behind the door at low opacity and walk them one step; for the
Relic-Warden, `Stage.fx` a frost burst on the tile and a cry; refresh the relic tile to the smashed graphic right
after the Ice8 sound.

**E12 · Medium · 12.15 The camp: the sprite contradicts the words.** On the griffon route the camp box says "The
griffons have dug a hollow in the snow and are asleep in a heap", but the camp event is a standing `BW_Sailor`
on every route (tools/build_act3.rb:77). No griffon is on Solstice.
*Fix:* give the camp event one page per route (switch or a script that sets the graphic): two `Followers/PIDGEOT`
(or griffon art) lying down for griffons, the sailor for the ship, Angajuk's whale tile at the dock's end for the
whale.

**E13 · Medium · 12.16 Sopo: the wrong speaker bubbles, and the Corsairs never come.** `say()` falls back to the
event you spoke to when it can't find a speaker (007_Story.rb:27-30), so Reptin's "Charming. We'll find our own
way" and Meat Meat's "Meat Meat will bite it" bubble out of Sopo. "A Corsair sloop slides out of the fog… Mira's
people pile off, shouting, and fall on Sopo's pack" while the two Glalie stand still and no Corsair appears; then
"CORSAIR:" speaks from nowhere.
*Fix:* only fall back to the event when its name matches the tag (or drop the fallback); stage the Heralds (E1);
`Stage.actor` two Corsairs walking up the dock from the water (19,58), `Stage.walk` them onto the Glalie tiles, and
remove the Glalie as they "fall on" them.

**E14 · Low · 12.16 Sopo vanishes the instant you win.** `SW_DOCK_CLEARED` blanks Sopo's page, so he blinks out
while the next box says he "scrambles away up the ice" and "follows you at a distance". His after-line (`sopo_after`,
"She's expecting EVERYONE!") can never be heard.
*Fix:* `Stage.walk` Sopo off north before setting the switch; add a page-2 Sopo on the climb (a few screens up)
who says `sopo_after` and then runs, so the "follows you" line is true.

### Structure, pacing and goals

**E6 · Medium · 12.01-12.04 Auril's relics are off the Goal.** Selrae's Ch 11 goal named them ("Auril's relics
wait in the Heartroot: smash them, and Sephek stays down", 037_Chapter11.rb:205); the Council goal that follows
replaces it with the griffon roost and the Vault (037_Chapter11.rb:245-250). Nothing in Part 12 says where the three
are (Root Gate, Sap Wells, Overflow Channel) or why it matters before Ch 13 (SC 13.07: "While one of her relics
stands, I stand").
*Fix:* append to the Council goal while `relics_left > 0`: "(Auril's relics: 3 left in the Heartroot. Break them
and Sephek stays down.)", and update it on each smash. Have Rheven or a Rootwarden at the Root-Gate say where the
Halo was seen carrying them.

**E7 · Medium · 12.02-12.04: the three relic scenes are the same scene three times.** 7-8 boxes each, word for
word apart from the location phrase: the same "frost-runes are fresh", the same Alerion and Wyn pair, the same
faceless warden, the same crack. By the third the player is skimming.
*Fix:* keep the full version for the first relic; for the second, one new narration line and a different pair
(e.g. Reptin and Meat Meat); for the third, a short version with Alerion's "That's all three." Rewrites in the
Dialogue table.

**E8 · Medium · 12.10 The Goal after the Vault doesn't say where to go.** "Back to Rootspire, and north to
Solstice. She went ahead of you." (047c_WhiteVault.rb:423) drops the departure point the Ch 11 goal had named.
The way back is seven maps (Circuits, Vault, Beyond, Glasswater, Fields, Trunk-Tier, lift to the Canopy).
*Fix:* build it from `voyage_route` as the Ch 11 goal does: "Back to Rootspire: the griffon roost, on the
Canopy-Tier. Then north to Solstice. She went ahead of you."

**E9 · Medium · 12.08-12.10 The circuits' reward is hidden.** Each circuit you stop (and the Verdant Blade's ward)
takes 15% off every Pokémon on Avarice's team and 20% off her Reflection. The player is only told so if they hold
all three vault-map fragments; otherwise "(Each one hums toward the far door.)" Nothing in the fight shows the
effect either.
*Fix:* always show the second line, softened without the map: "(Break them, and whatever's in the cradle will be
weaker for it.)"; at the cradle, one line per broken circuit before the battle ("Three rings dark. Her body
flickers where they fed it.").

**E25 · Medium · 12.11-12.12 A long backtrack with nothing in it.** From the cradle to Tamsin is seven map changes
with one story beat (the closing Wall) and one NPC (the lift-keeper, whose line is from Act II). It follows the
chapter's biggest reveal, so the momentum is spent walking.
*Fix:* give the return one beat on the way: Rheven at the Fields wall ("Griffons are saddled. The roost, when
you're ready."), which also tells the player where to go (E8); or let the griffons collect the party at the
Glasswater's far shore and fly them to the roost.

### Continuity

**E5 · High · 12.08: Avarice's whisper to Reptin contradicts canon.** "AVARICE: (to Reptin) Your wolf howled at
your birth. I heard it. I hear everything said in my laboratories." (047c_WhiteVault.rb:321). Reptin is an elf
druid born long before her labs; his wolf is a curse from a bite on the tundra in Act I (story_bible.md: "takes
the bite that makes him a werewolf"). Nobody answers, so it lands as fact. The Alerion whisper has the same
weakness on a smaller scale: "I taught a ghost to teach you" is a new claim about his necromancy that no one
picks up.
*Fix:* rewrite both so they are true and answered (Dialogue table rows 15-16).

**E15 · Medium · The White Vault is in Chapter 12; the design puts it in the postgame.** story_bible.md (places
table and Act summaries) and act3_script.md ("Postgame outline: The White Vault") make the Vault (Ythryn) the
postgame, with Avarice's last shard as its final boss; act3_script.md's Chapter 12 has no Vault at all. The game
puts the Vault, Avarice's crystal body and her end before Solstice, and finishes her at the Throne (SC 13). The
postgame hook ("Avarice's last shard waits in the White Vault") is now spent.
*Fix:* a design decision for the owner: either update story_bible.md and act3_script.md (Chapter 12 gains SC
12.00 "The White Vault"; the postgame needs a new hook), or move the Vault back to the postgame and keep Avarice to
mirrors until the Throne.

**E16 · Low · 12.10 and 12.13: item boxes before the moment that gives them.** "You obtained a Wyrmling Scale

runs before the say at :411). The Full Restores come after Wyn's blankets line, not Hellen's food (038:116).
*Fix:* move `give_key` after the case line; move `give(:FULLRESTORE, 3)` to right after Hellen's line (E3).

**E17 · Low · 12.10 RAZ-MKIII is one scale, then a cluster.** "a crystal grown round one green scale: RAZ-MKIII"
(047c:363), then "a crystal core grown round a cluster of green wyrmling scales" (047c:411).
*Fix:* say "green scales" both times (rewrite in the table).

**E18 · Low · 12.06 Alberica's door: dreamed, or seen in the well?** "I dreamed this door. Before the Chasm. Before
any of it." (047c:281) against her Ch 11 line at the sigils: "That's the vault I saw in the well."
*Fix:* "ALBERICA: (her rune prickling) Wings over an eye. I saw this door in the well."

**E19 · Low · Ch 11 says the door has opened; in 12.07 it's shut.** The sigils' merge says "Somewhere beyond the
Auroral Wall, a door has opened." (047c:111) but the door of wings is closed until you press the sigil in.

**E20 · Low · 12.12 The lift-keeper's line is from Act II.** "Canopy? The dragons have circled low since you

*Fix:* a `ch11_done` variant: "LIFT-KEEPER: Canopy again? Griffons have been pacing the roost since dawn." /

**E23 · Low · act3_script.md SC 12.01 gives Tamsin "her".** "her Rootwarden cloak", "Look at her", "She means" in

**E24 · Low · 12.08 "We watched you die" ignores the Ch 8 doubt.** If the player looked at the body's bare arms

## 2. First-time player

Read as someone who has never played a Pokémon game and doesn't know the campaign.

1. **"My Goal says the griffon roost. Why am I climbing down a well?"** (12.01) Nothing on screen sends you to the
   Heartroot. *Fix:* E6: the relics stay on the Goal, with a count.
2. **"Who is 'him'? It's beating 'in time with him'."** (12.02) Sephek is never named in the relic scenes, and
   "the Halo" isn't explained here. *Fix:* "In time with Sephek." and, on the first relic, "The Halo, Sephek's
   frost-cult, carried it in."
3. **"What happens if I don't smash them?"** (12.02-12.04) The stakes ("Sephek stays down") were only in an
   overwritten Goal. *Fix:* E6, plus Wyn on the first relic: "...While these stand, he gets up. Every time."
4. **"Why does a boat take me through a wall of light, and what's a 'time-freeze'?"** (12.05) The Wall's rule
   (time is frozen outside it) was taught chapters ago. *Fix:* one box before "Go through the Wall?": "On the far
   side, time stands still. Without a blessing, it bites."
5. **"Which blessing? Who are the Still Ones?"** (12.05) *Fix:* name what it does: "The Still Ones' blessing (Still
   Sky) holds you steady."
6. **"Why am I at a vault? What's in it?"** (12.06) The Goal says the sigils point there, but not what for. *Fix:*
   Wyn's Ch 11 line ("Something in there is awake. I can hear it counting.") could be repeated at the door, or
   Reptin: "Whatever's counting in there, I'd like it to stop."
7. **"Who is this woman in the mirrors, and wasn't she dead? What 'coat'?"** (12.08) "You watched a coat burn"
   relies on the Ch 8 metaphor of a borrowed body. *Fix:* "AVARICE: You watched a borrowed body burn, Alberica.
   Did you think I only had one?" (fits in 90).
8. **"What are V-7, a 'scale core', a 'Seraph-Draco'? Isn't Razzy a dragon now?"** (12.08) Razzy hatched as
   Razzilith; a "scale core" sounds like something cut out of him. *Fix:* Meat Meat answers the core line:
   "MEAT MEAT: Those are Razzy's old scales. From the lab. Razzy is a DRAGON now."
9. **"Who is the 'Mirror Mother'?"** (12.10) Named only in side quests. *Fix:* cut it (rewrite in the table).
10. **"What do the rings do? Do I have to stop them?"** (12.09) *Fix:* E9.
11. **"Why do I have a staff, a mantle and a key, and why do they fit?"** (12.09) The relics come from the Herald
    quests; the scene assumes you know which is whose. *Fix:* name the owner in the "stirs" line: "Reptin's
    Heartwarden's Staff stirs. It knows this ring."
12. **"She fled north. Where is that, and how do I follow?"** (12.10) *Fix:* E8.
13. **"Who's talking? I only see a bird."** (12.13) *Fix:* E3.
14. **"What's 'the sixth figure'? What does nodding mean?"** (12.13) It pays off a Prologue moment from many hours
    earlier. *Fix:* one reminder in Tamsin's line: "TAMSIN: That's you. On Hellen's wall. The sixth Herald, the
    one nobody could name."
15. **"Am I flying? I'm standing in a tree."** (12.14) *Fix:* E2.
16. **"Who is 'She', and who are 'Mira's people'? Why is a sailor in my camp?"** (12.15-12.16) Sopo's "She" and
    Avarice's "the Frostmaiden" both mean Auril; the Corsairs arrive without a face. *Fix:* "SOPO: The Frostmaiden's
    expecting you!"; E12 and E13 for the sailor and the Corsairs.
