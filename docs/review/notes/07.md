# Part 07 · Chapter 7: Two Roads Down — notes

Scene numbers refer to `docs/review/script/07.md`. File references are to `game/Data/Scripts/998_FrostThaw/` unless a path is given. Counts come from the log (`parts/07.txt`): 866 message boxes, 171 of them over ~90 characters, 7 sentences split across two boxes, 96 battles (20 story or trainer fights), 4 losses, 55 map changes. The Heralds speak 170 boxes; 145 are logged `absent` and the other 25 land their bubble on the wrong sprite.

## 1. Editor

### Staging and animation

**E1 · High · The party is never on the map (every scene).** None of Alberica, Reptin, Alerion, Meat Meat, Glimmer or Wyn has an event on any of the chapter's 20-odd maps (`tools/build_ch7.rb`, `build_ch7a.rb`, `build_ch7b.rb`, `build_ch7_deep.rb`). There are no followers. A Herald appears only when a scene calls `stage_act`: a stand-in pops up next to you, plays one pose, and `Stage.cleanup` removes it a few boxes later. That happens nine times in the chapter (7A.01 Wyn; 7A.09 Reptin; 7A.10 all five, one at a time; 7A.11 Wyn; 7B.14 Meat Meat). So "Reptin kneels at the edge", "Alberica's hand goes to her chest", "Wyn holding out a biscuit" and "Reptin stepping between the girl and her mother" all happen to nobody on screen. This breaks WRITING_GUIDE §9a in every scene. **Fix:** a `stage_party(ex, ey)` helper that calls `Stage.herald(k, dx, dy)` for each companion in a fan behind you at the start of every `scene_*` and every examinable with party lines, and `Stage.cleanup` at the end. Do these first: the Undercanopy gate (7A.02, 13 boxes), the Mycolith (7A.11), Mira's ship (7B.16), the Overflow mirror (7B.14) and Var's vision (7B.32).

**E2 · High · Bubbles land on the wrong character.** `say()` falls back to the event you're talking to when a tag has no event on the map (`007_Story.rb:21-40`). So the monster speaks the party's lines:
- 7A.09: the lake-wyrm says "Meat Meat can be gentle." and "Since when, sweetheart?"
- 7A.03: the Blight says Reptin's "Her? Forgive me. Who's 'her'?" and Alberica's "Then buy us a drink first, sugar."
- 7A.11: the Shadow Mycolith says Wyn's "It's the old tree." and Alerion's "I know how that goes."
- 7B.11: the attendant says "Don't do my voice. Nobody does my voice." while doing Alberica's voice.
- 7A.07, 7B.01, 7B.05, 7B.12: Myr, Hew and Selrae speak the party's lines.

Other tags find nothing: CHILD (the event is "Boy with a mirror"), CATALOGUER ("Cataloguing construct"), and DIGGER (it finds Gant 13 tiles away while Hobb is talking). **Fix:** E1 first. Then, in `say()`, never fall back to the current event for a tag that is a companion's name. Rename the events so their tags match: "Child with a mirror", "Cataloguer construct", "Digger Gant" and "Digger Hobb" (and tag Hobb's lines HOBB).

**E3 · High · Mira's gym plays on an empty dock (7B.16).** The design puts this aboard the *Boreal Requiem* (act2_script.md SC 7B.04). In the game there is no ship map and no Mira. The scene has the cold voice, the mirror-core in the hold, Mira collapsing, her daughter climbing into her lap and two battles. All of it plays with you standing at a gangplank tile. "Her daughter runs to her" refers to a girl who has never been drawn (7B.14). Mira and her daughter only pop onto the pier on the next refresh. **Fix:** build a deck map (a ship interior from the tileset, 2 rooms: deck and hold). Board with a fade. Put Mira at the wheel with FT_mira and the girl beside you. Walk the girl to her (`Stage.walk`). Put the mirror-core battle in the hold, then come back up for the gym fight. If there's no time for a map, stage Mira and her daughter on the gangplank at least, and have Mira collapse with a pose and E-06 on "The core cracks across".

### Structure and pacing

**E8 · High · Road B is three times the length of Road A, and most of it is lore read off walls.** Road A is 12 scenes on 5 maps. Road B is 36 scenes on 12 maps. The design's Road B had 8 scenes and ended when the Root-Key opened the door (act2_script.md SC 7B.08). The code adds six lower zones (7B.18-7B.35) and a second lock, the Glass Sigil, six maps down. These are mostly reading:
- the lab notes (6 boxes plus 4 of banter);
- the cataloguer (9);
- the binding notes (4 plus 4);
- Autumn's crates (4 plus 3);
- the script pool (6 plus 4);
- the chardalyn pages (3);
- the Var vision (19).

They tell you Avarice's whole plan (dragon, flight path, patron, White Womb, Xardorok, Autumn) a chapter before you confront her, and several of the same facts twice (the White Womb is named by Selrae, the script pool, the pages and the mask). **Fix:** cut each lore read to three boxes and one reaction. Keep one telling of each fact:
- the White Womb: Selrae;
- Xardorok: the pages;
- the flight path: the pool;
- Autumn: the crates.

Make the lab notes and the cataloguer optional rooms off the main path. Consider moving the Glass Sigil to the Mirror-Root Vault (two maps shallower), so the lift run is shorter.

**E9 · High · Losses send you a long way back (7B.08, 7B.16, 7B.25, 7B.35).** The test lost four times:
1. To a wild Sudowoodo between two runes.
2. To Mira.
3. To Gelt.
4. To the lift constructs.

The first sent it to the Rooted Hearth and down the well again (three boxes). The second sent it to the Hearth and back across the city to the dock. The last two sent it to the grotto in the Mirror Garden, two and four maps up. The deep zones have no other rest. Level jumps make the losses likely:
- The Mirror Maze's wrong-answer wraith is L54 (`047b_TrialWeek.rb:220`) when the Echo Gate wraiths are L39.
- Var's echo is L44-46 with a Hyper Potion.
- The lift constructs are L46 doubles.

**Fix:** add a rest in the Vault of Amber Dreams, e.g. the freed recruit (7B.30) sets up a camp. Make the root-lift (7B.35) work both ways as soon as you reach the Scriptory, and say so. Bring the maze wraith to L42.

**E10 · Medium · Hew's gym is a 22-box monologue, and he gives you a thing built from something you haven't met (7B.05).** After the battle: the seal, the TM, the confession, the door, the compass "built from Prototype 3's spare parts", the money and the motes. This run hadn't met Prototype 3: the trainer sight used up the walk-up (7B.04). Reptin's "That can't have been easy" is the only reaction to a ten-year confession. **Fix:** split it in two. (+$5000)"), with the items added silently (`give(..., silent: true)` or `$PokemonBag.pbStoreItem`). Put a heal between battles 2 and 3, e.g. Wyn's spark flares once.
