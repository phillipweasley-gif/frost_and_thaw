# Part 02 · Chapter 2: Lottery Night (notes)

The chapter's story is strong on paper: a voiceless goblin saved as an egg, a lottery broken by a song, a goddess marking Alberica, a killer naming his price. On screen most of it is told over an empty map. 39 of the 70 spoken boxes in the played run come from people who aren't on the map or aren't in view, Razzy never appears on the road, the chasm shows a placeholder girl, and the two biggest scenes end by making everyone vanish in one frame. The town around the lottery doesn't notice it happened. Severity counts: **High 7 · Medium 21 · Low 14** (42 editor findings), plus 16 first-time-player questions, 34 dialogue rewrites and 58 over-long boxes split.

## 1. Editor

### Staging and animation

**E1 · High · every scene but 2.07-2.08 · The Heralds aren't there.** Outside the lottery the party has no sprites. The log flags 38 of 70 spoken boxes as absent or off camera (Alberica 6, Reptin 7, Shade 4, Shimmer 4, Alerion 3, Mishann 8, Sephek 4, Siv 1, guard 1), plus Reptin's gym aside, which is mis-bubbled (E9). 2.01, 2.02, 2.03, 2.05 and 2.10 are entirely voices over an empty map; in 2.04 seven Herald boxes play before anyone appears. This breaks WRITING_GUIDE §9a ("a line from someone the player can't see is a bug") and is the biggest single reason the chapter feels disjointed. **Fix:** a helper (e.g. `Stage.party(formation)`) called at the start of every spine scene that places each travelling Herald with `Stage.herald(k, dx, dy)` round you, as `lottery_night` already does, and `cleanup` with a fade at the end. Or add Herald followers for the whole act.

**E2 · High · 2.02 · Razzy never appears.** Six boxes describe a goblin bursting out of the snow, grabbing Reptin's sleeve, opening his mouth and running east; the screen shows an empty field and nothing moves. The first sight of a character the whole act turns on is text only, and the "crack in the snow glowing blue-white" is 22 tiles off screen. **Fix:** `Stage.actor(:razzy, …)` two tiles ahead with E-01 snow burst and `Stage.hop`; `walk_to` the Reptin stand-in, `face` him; `emote(:question)` on Reptin; `pan_to(28, 26)` to show the glow; `Stage.leave(razzy, 28, 28)`; `pan_back`.

**E3 · High · 2.04 · The chasm shows a placeholder girl.** The "Frost-light" event (`build_ch2.rb:188-191`) wears BW (134), a girl in a green hat (the Story Bible's placeholder for Razzy). You press A on her, Razzy's real sprite appears on top of her, and after the scene she stays in the crack at 90 opacity, "a faint glow". **Fix:** give both pages an empty graphic (or a glow tile / E-03's last frame), and make the event a step-on trigger at the lip.

**E4 · High · 2.10 · The golden acorn is handed over by an invisible priestess in the wrong place.** "Outside the hall, a priestess ... is waiting on the steps" plays while you're still at Duvessa's table; no one appears; ten boxes of Mishann and Reptin play to an empty room. This is the item the whole act turns on (story bible: "the third way"). **Fix:** end the gym after the TM line. A step-on trigger on the hall's outside step (31,20) puts Mishann there (`Stage.actor`, `approach`), with the Reptin stand-in beside you. Or go back to the design: set the goal to "Mishann, at the House of the Morninglord, asked to see Reptin." and play SC 2.09 there.

**E5 · High · 2.08 · The lottery ends in a pop, and morning never comes.** `s.cleanup` removes about 20 actors in one frame, then the night tint lifts to daylight over 20 frames, with no fade (`014_Chapter2.rb:652-653`). Duvessa then says "Last night you made me break it", but the game never showed a night passing. **Fix:** `pbFadeOutIn { s.cleanup; tone reset }`, then one box: *Morning. The square is empty. The board is still up.*

**E6 · Medium · 2.07 · Auril is named by someone who isn't there and hasn't been met.** "MISHANN: That's Her. The Frostmaiden. Auril." Mishann isn't on the map and the player meets her two scenes later. **Fix:** stage her in the crowd with a lantern (BW (98)), name her on first sight: *A priestess with a lantern, at the crowd's edge, says it first:* and tag the line PRIESTESS until she gives her name in 2.10.

**E7 · Medium · 2.08 · Sephek doesn't leave, and his lines have no bubble.** He walks five tiles straight up to (26,15), not toward the gate (x 22-23), and stands there in view through Shade and Shimmer's exchange until the cleanup pop, while the text says "He walks out through the north gate." His four boxes get no bubble because `Stage.speaker` finds the gossip "NPC bryn_sephek_talk" (30,32) before the staged actor (log: off_camera). **Fix:** `Stage.leave(sephek, 22, 3)` with a `pan_to` that follows him; in `043_Stage.rb:758-775` check the scene's own actors (`@actors`) before map events.

**E8 · Medium · 2.07 · The town's NPCs stand in the scene.** The square's own people are never hidden: the wandering gossip stands on Ilda's mother's tile (24,23), Sheriff Southwell on the crowd's old woman (31,23), and the scrimshaw seller (BW (61)) beside a crowd member with the same sprite. **Fix:** give the square NPCs a page that hides them while switch 3955 is on, or use them as the crowd.

## 2. First-time player

*Questions someone with no Pokémon or D&D background would ask, in the order they'd hit them.*

1. **"Who's talking? I can only see myself."** (2.01-2.05, 2.10) → E1: put the party on screen.
2. **"Which way is Bryn Shander? The text says north, the goal says south-east."** → E19 goal rewrites.
3. **"What goblin? I didn't see anything."** (2.02) → E2: stage Razzy, pan to the glow.
4. **"Why can't he talk, and why is his skin glowing?"** Nobody wonders aloud. → one Herald line: "REPTIN: Someone cut that scar on purpose." (the design holds the answer for Ch3, but the question should be asked).
5. **"What are 'Aurilites' and 'frost-light'? Who is 'her'?"** (2.03) → Siv's line: "SIV: The Frostmaiden's light. Every crumb belongs to her. You don't."
6. **"Why is there a girl in a green hat in the crack?"** (2.04) → E3.
7. **"Why does a medallion want blood, and why would a horn help? What's Saltmarsh?"** → one line after the cut: "SHADE: I pulled my sister out of the snow in Saltmarsh. This was lying there." (sets up the horn).
8. **"A person turned into an egg? Will it hatch? What name?"** → Alerion's line could say who: "ALERION: Then we find someone who knows his name." The goal could add it: "Keep the egg warm. Find someone who knows his name."
9. **"What is the lottery, and what happens to the name?"** (2.05) → E40.
10. **"Who is Duvessa?"** She calls Ilda's name before you've met her if you skip the caravan guard. → narration: "Speaker Duvessa Shane lifts a token from the bowl." before her line.
11. **"What did the pendant do? Why 'remember the last summer'?"** → one box before her song: "Two years without a summer. Alberica sings them one." (the "two years" lives only in NPC lines).
12. **"Who is Mishann, and how does she know who Auril is?"** → E6.
13. **"Who's the man in shirtsleeves? Why does he want Shimmer? Why doesn't anyone stop him?"** → a Herald line as he arrives: "SHIMMER: (very still) Sephek. Don't. Nobody move." It names him, shows she knows him, and explains why nobody fights.
14. **"Why do I need a gym badge to chase him? Why is Duvessa fighting me if she's grateful?"** → keep "Show me it was worth it", and add to the goal: "Her seal opens the north gate."
15. **"What's a golden acorn for? What's the Morninglord? Who is my 'wolf-friend'?"** → E27; one line from Mishann: "MISHANN: The Morninglord is the dawn. I keep its fire lit."
16. **"Who are the Black Network / Zhentarim / Brotherhood?"** → E31.
