# Part 01 · Chapter 1: Targos (notes)

The owner's complaints all show up in the code. Every spoken line from a Herald in Chapter 1 comes from someone who isn't on the map, or out of the wrong person's speech bubble. The party has no sprites on the pier, and from the end of the tavern scene onward they have no sprites anywhere. The first meeting runs 38 boxes before you get to do anything. The spine sends you to the Town Hall for a man who is at the Luskan Arms. The side quests make you chase people: the dog who starts the biggest one runs about as fast as you do, and three townsfolk wander off before you can press A. The writing itself is mostly good and in voice. Almost everything below is staging, order and signposting. Severity counts: **High 8 · Medium 19 · Low 15** (42 editor findings: 32 E, 10 side-quest S), plus the sweep's silent and unreachable NPCs traced to their cause, 20 first-time-player questions and 57 dialogue rows (34 of them over-long boxes).

## 1. Editor

### Staging and animation

**E1 · High · 1.01 · The pier scene is four voices with nobody on screen.** The log flags all four spoken boxes `[offstage] … absent`: ALERION ×2, SHIMMER, ALBERICA. *Where they physically are:* the five Heralds exist only as NPCs on map 2002, sitting at their tavern tables (Alberica 9,8; Reptin 10,10; Alerion 13,5; Shade 13,8; Shimmer 15,10; `build_act1.rb:179-184`). Map 2001 has no Herald event at any time. So when Alberica is "passing with a sea-bag", the game has her sitting in the tavern 30 tiles away. The camera holds on you alone for the whole scene. Alerion "You fell out of the air", the first person to touch you (design note, act1_script SC 1.01), is a disembodied box. **Fix:** in `scene_targos_arrival` (007_Story.rb:152-157), `Stage.herald(:alerion, 0, -1, 2)` one tile in front of you with `emote(:exclaim)` before his first line. `Stage.actor(:shimmer, …)` at the pier's foot, `face` you, then `leave` west toward the tavern after her line. Alberica walks across the bottom of the frame with `walk`, says her line mid-walk and keeps going. `Stage.cleanup` once the last of them is off screen. Alerion then walks off after Shimmer, so the player has someone to follow (§9a "Follow me!").

**E2 · High · 1.03, every scene after it · The party vanishes in one frame and stays invisible for the rest of the chapter.** `set_switch(SW_PARTY_FORMED, true)` (007_Story.rb:240) flips all five tavern NPCs to a blank page during the tip box. No one stands or walks out; they're simply gone from their stools. *Where they physically are after that:* nowhere. No Targos map has a Herald event (2001, 2003-2009, 2124), and they exist only as `state[:companions]`. Every later scene is talk from people the player can't see. **Fix:** don't blank them at the meeting. Keep page 1 until you leave the tavern (switch on at the map transfer), so the five stay seated and can be talked to (see S1). From then on, call a `Stage.party(formation)` helper at the start of each spine scene (part 02 E1 proposes the same helper) that places the travelling Heralds round you, and `cleanup` behind a fade.

**E3 · High · 1.04, 1.05, 1.06 · Heralds' lines come out of Epheris's and Naerth's bubbles.** `say()` falls back to the event you talked to whenever a tag isn't found (007_Story.rb:26-30). In the log these scenes show no `[offstage]` notes at all, because the fallback hides the problem. On screen, Epheris appears to say "(setting the medallion down very gently) Of course. So sorry. Is the floor also cursed?" and "Bait for what?". The worst case is the Luskan Arms: the bubble puts "SHIMMER: Sephek." on Naerth, and then Naerth answers "...You know his name. Interesting." That reads as Naerth talking to himself. Five Herald boxes are affected in 1.04, three in 1.05, and three in 1.06. **Fix:** in `say()`, fall back only when the tag matches the event's own name (part 02 E9). With E2's party helper the Heralds will be found by name anyway.

**E4 · Medium · 1.02 · Ma Yarbroul is on the map but the game can't find her.** Her event is named "Barkeep" (`build_act1.rb:176`), so `Stage.speaker("MA YARBROUL")` matches nothing, and her line plays with no bubble (log: `[offstage] MA YARBROUL absent`). **Fix:** name the event "Ma Yarbroul" (or "Barkeep Ma Yarbroul"). The speaker lookup matches every word of the tag against the event name.

**E5 · Medium · 1.03 · Shimmer is out of frame.** She sits at (15,10) and you stand at the door (5,11), ten tiles away. All three of her lines here get no bubble (log: `off_camera`). Her "Don't." is the button on the scene's best exchange. **Fix:** walk the player in to (9,10) before Shade speaks (`Stage.walk($game_player, …)`, which also gives the room some movement), or seat Shimmer at (12,10).

### Structure and pacing

**E15 · High · 1.02-1.03 · 38 boxes before the player does anything.** From the door to "What do you do?" the cutscene plays 38 boxes, and 47 in all, with one choice and no movement (WRITING_GUIDE §2.3 puts the limit at about 25). This is the owner's "pacing" complaint in one place: the chapter's first scene is a play you watch. **Fix:** split it. 1.02 ends on Ma Yarbroul's line. Then give the player control, with Shade waving you over (`emote(:exclaim)`, `approach`): "SHADE: You. The one by the door. Sit, before you fall over." Each Herald then introduces themself and puts their relic down when you talk to them at their table. That turns 1.03's 17 intro boxes into five short conversations the player chooses, and makes the unreachable tavern lines reachable (S1). When you've talked to all five (or sat at the empty stool), the hum, Reptin and Alerion's "ghost story" beat, and the choice play as a short closing scene (about 10 boxes).

**E16 · High · 1.04-1.05 · The spine points to the wrong building for Naerth.** After Epheris, the goal says "First, Naerth's seal to get out of Targos" and doesn't say where Naerth is. Every pointer you can find then says the Town Hall: Ma Yarbroul ("Speaker's seal? Town Hall, up the main street", 006_Dialogue.rb:59), the gate guards ("Want the road? Win his seal at the Town Hall", 014_Chapter2.rb:104), and the hall sign ("Challengers welcome"). Naerth isn't in the hall until you've met him at the Luskan Arms. The hall's guide says "Try the Luskan Arms", but only once you've walked to the far end of town, and Ilsa, Corrin and Mara will fight you there anyway. The design meant the guard to send you to the Arms (act1_script SC 1.05, and the original lines at 006_Dialogue.rb:20-22), but 014_Chapter2.rb:97-104 overrides those lines. **Fix:** restore the Arms pointer in 014's guard and Ma Yarbroul's line. Make the goal "Get Speaker Naerth's seal to leave Targos. He does business at the Luskan Arms." (rewrites below). Block the hall's first gap with the guide until `naerth_terms`.

**E17 · Medium · 1.05 · If you meet Naerth before Epheris, the goal still says "Ask Epheris".** The Town Hall goal is set only `if flag?(:relics_are_lures)` (007_Story.rb:349). Epheris then sets "First, Naerth's seal…", which is right. But between the two visits, the Goal menu hides that the hall is now open. **Fix:** set a two-step goal in either order, e.g. "Ask Epheris what the relics are, then win Naerth's seal at the Town Hall."

**E18 · Medium · 1.06 · 16 boxes after the gym battle with no input, then a poison message.** The badge, the TM and its pocket line, Reptin's aside, the lady in the tower, Alerion, the Splicers' three boxes and their pocket line all come at once. **Fix:** Naerth gives the seal and TM and says the lady-in-the-tower line (8 boxes). **E19 · Medium · 1.07 · The chapter's character scene is optional and nobody points to it.** The carved cat ("Saltmarsh was one of them. Yours, not mine.") sets up Glimmer in Ch6 (`heard_of_glimmer`, read at 029_Glimmer.rb:118). It only plays if you go back to the tavern and talk to the barkeep after the gym. The goal at that point says "The south gate is open". **Fix:** goal "Rest at Three Flags Sailing tonight. The south-west gate opens in the morning." Or play 1.07 automatically on the first step toward the gate after the seal, with a fade to the tavern.

**E20 · Medium · Side: the trapper · The run button is hidden in the Pokémon Center.** Snowshoes are running shoes. Pokémon games hand them over at the start, before the first long walk. Here they wait on an unnamed man in the Center, and nothing points to him. ### Sweep findings: who says nothing, and why

**Boy, "NPC targos_cold_talk", "NPC targos_sacrifice_talk", "NPC targos_child_talk" (map 2001) say nothing.** Their lines exist and play when you press A on them: `targos_cold_talk`, `targos_sacrifice_talk` and `targos_child_talk` at 006_Dialogue.rb:25-34, and Boy's `boy_sled_dog` at 045_MountainClimb.rb:148-159. The cause is movement. The three townsfolk are built with `move: 1` (random walk, `build_act1.rb:147, 148, 150`; default frequency 3, so a step every second or two). Boy is random at `move_speed: 4, move_frequency: 5` (`build_act1.rb:138-142`). The sweep (tools/headless/story_run.rb:248-256) reads an NPC's tile once, walks there over several steps (driver.rb:334-356), then faces the old tile and presses A. By then the NPC has moved on, so A hits empty snow. That's why the reports change from sweep to sweep: at the start of the game the sweep missed sacrifice and child, after two badges it missed all three. For a player it's the same problem, milder: the townsfolk drift off as you approach, and Boy is close to uncatchable on the two-tile lower street. **Fix (map):** make the three stationary with a "turn random" custom route (`move_type: 3`, route Turn Random + Wait 60), or `move_frequency: 1`. Make Boy a trigger, not a chase (S2). **Fix (tool):** in `talk_to`, re-read `ev.x, ev.y` each step and re-path when the target moved.

**"Rat" at (8,13) on map 2004 can't be reached.** The Rat is built `through: true` with random movement (`move_type: 1, move_frequency: 6, move_speed: 5`, `build_act1.rb:205-206`). `through` ignores passability, so it walks through the walls. The Arms' floor is x 3-16, y 2-11, with the exits at (9-10,12). The sweep found the rat at (8,13), in the void under the doorway, where no one can stand next to it. It can also walk onto the exit tiles and over Naerth, Skath and the trainers. ("Rat said nothing" after two badges is the wanderer problem again.) **Fix:** drop `through: true` (it then stays on the floor, and walks into people instead of through them). Better still, keep the rat as decoration on a fixed route along the skirting, and put its two lines on a Sign at the wall (e.g. (3,9)), so the text about rotten floorboards has a fixed place to be read.

## 2. First-time player

*Questions from someone who has never played a Pokémon game and doesn't know D&D or the campaign, in the order they'd hit them.*

1. **"Where am I? When am I? I was at a festival a second ago."** (1.01) The first box is "You land on frozen planks. Snow, everywhere. The sky has no sun in it at all." It never says Targos, Icewind Dale, or that you've gone back four hundred years. The Prologue's notes (00 E1e, Top 5 #3) hand this to Targos's first box. → Add a second box: *"Targos, in the Icewind Dale of Hellen's stories. Four hundred years before you were born."* Show the map name banner on the fade-in as well.
2. **"Who's talking? I'm alone on a pier."** (1.01) → E1: put Alerion, Shimmer and Alberica on the pier.
3. **"Who is Alerion? Who's 'pretty boy'? Who are these people?"** Their name tags are the first time you see their names. Nobody gives a name or role on first sight (§9a rule 1). → One narration line before Alerion speaks: *"A pale elf catches your arm. The ring on his hand is white with frost."* Then in the tavern, everyone's own introduction (E15).
4. **"Which way is the tavern? What hill?"** → Shimmer's rewrite ("west along the docks") and Alerion walking that way (E1).
5. **"Who was the woman in furs, and why does everyone go quiet?"** Leaving her unnamed is deliberate (named in Ch2), but nobody even asks. → One Herald line after "The door swings shut on nothing": "SHADE: Anyone want to say what that was?" / "SHIMMER: No."
6. **"What's a relic? Why do they hum? Why is there a stone under someone's ribs?"** The mystery is earned, but the narration says "Four relics on the table" as if the word were known. → Before the hum: *"Old things, cold things. Relics, the Dale calls them."*
7. **"What are 'the Heralds'? The tip says to open 'Heralds'."** Nobody in Chapter 1 calls the five that. → The tip's rewrite below says "your companions" and puts the word in the menu only.
8. **"Who's Ma Yarbroul?"** → E24.
