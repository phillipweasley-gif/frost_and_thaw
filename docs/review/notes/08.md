# Part 08 · Chapter 8: The Rootsun Forge — notes

Scene numbers refer to `docs/review/script/08.md`. File references are to `game/Data/Scripts/998_FrostThaw/` unless a path is given. Counts come from the log (`parts/08.txt`): 649 message boxes, 181 of them over ~90 characters, 9 sentences split across two boxes, and about 200 Herald or Glimmer lines with nobody on screen.

## 1. Editor

### Staging and animation

**E1 · High · The party is never on the map (every scene).** No Herald and not Glimmer has an event on any of the 15 maps (`tools/build_ch8.rb`, `tools/build_ch8_deep.rb`), there are no followers, and the chapter calls `Stage.herald` once (Meat Meat, 8.21). Reptin "kneeling beside her", Alberica holding out her arm, Wyn handing out a blanket and Alerion "already running" all happen to nobody on screen. This breaks WRITING_GUIDE §9a ("everyone who speaks is on screen") in every scene of the chapter. **Fix:** a helper, e.g. `stage_party(ex, ey)`, that calls `Stage.herald(k, dx, dy)` for each companion in a fan behind you at the start of every `scene_*` and examinable with party lines, and `Stage.cleanup` at the end. Do the three set-pieces first: Autumn falls (8.13), Glimmer's sacrifice (8.21), and the Heart Chamber (8.19-8.24).

**E2 · High · Glimmer's death is narration with no Glimmer (8.21).** "Glimmer steps past her." / "He lifts the Rimefang." / "Glimmer is kneeling where it was. Light is coming out of him" / "The light goes up". The only effects are one gold flash and Saint7. This is the death of a main character, and it runs 30 boxes with no input. Then a boss fight starts six boxes after "Glimmer is gone." **Fix:** put a Glimmer stand-in beside you before the Avarice fight. After the win, walk him to the engine (`Stage.walk`), pose him, play an effect on the engine tiles, flash, then fade his sprite out over the "light goes up" boxes (opacity steps). Give the moment a panel ("He drives the Rimefang into the heart of the engine" is a frozen moment). Fade to black after "Glimmer is gone." before the Shard rises, so grief has a beat to itself.

**E3 · High · Autumn falls is told, not staged (8.13).** Darrus stands still at (11,14) and vanishes when the switch flips. There's no Meat Meat, no cocoon, no blade effect and no fall, and Journey mode gives the player no input across 16 boxes. This is the act's moral turn and the charge at the Ch10 trial. **Fix:** walk a Meat Meat stand-in onto the bridge, play a psychic-blade effect, move Darrus back a tile with `Stage.walk` and jump him off the rail (or fade him out) with a sap splash effect. Give Journey mode one beat of input, for example a "!" on Meat Meat and a choice of what *you* do while she does it ("Grab the cocoon" / "Shout her name"), recorded for the trial.

**E4 · Medium · Avarice is never on screen as Avarice (8.19-8.23).** The autorun fires at the west door (4,12). Var's sprite is at (16,18), off camera (logged `VAR off_camera`), facing away. No event is named Avarice, so all 20 AVARICE boxes have no bubble. "He turns" and "The face cracks like a mirror" play on a sprite that doesn't turn or crack; FT_var_cracked only appears later, at the Breaking Circle. When the engine dies, switch 3979 blanks Var's event, so "Var's body cracks all over" (8.23) plays on an empty floor. **Fix:** fire the scene a few tiles in, or `Stage.pan_to(16,18)` for the reveal. Turn the sprite on "He turns". Swap to FT_var_cracked with E-06 on "The face cracks". Name the event "Var Rime-Breaker (Avarice)" so `Stage.speaker` finds both tags. Keep Var's page visible until `scene_coat_falls` ends.

**E5 · Medium · Showpieces happen off screen.** The Dragonforge reveal (8.14) fires on the bridge at (8,14). The dragon at (26,15) is 18 tiles away, and the E-14 heartbeat effect loops on it where you can't see it. The Barricade illusion (8.01) plays 16 tiles from its sign. The dragon's launch (8.24) is 5 boxes about another map. **Fix:** `Stage.pan_to(26,15)` before "Chained among them", then `pan_back`. Play the illusion when you examine the sign (keep one establishing box on arrival). For the launch, cut with `Stage.visit` to the Dragonforge (or a panel of the dragon breaking the roof), then back.

**E6 · Medium · Bubbles on the wrong character, or none, because event names don't match tags.** VEXMORTIS's after-battle lines get no bubble (the event is "Devil-priest"). Lovely smile." gets its bubble on the devil-priest, because `say()` falls back to the event you're talking to (`007_Story.rb:27-30`). **Fix:** name the events "Vexmortis the devil-priest" and "Echo of Sephek". Put a duergar sprite at the Barricade gate. In `say()`, don't fall back to the current event for a tag that is a party member's name.

**E7 · Medium · People who leave, don't; things that fade, stay.** Each of these is narrated and not staged:

### Structure and pacing

**E10 · High · Three wipes and three six-map walks (8.08, 8.12).** Between the Barricade rest and the Heart Chamber there is no heal. The run holds about 30 trainers at L45-50 and 15 set-piece fights at L44-52. The test lost to Turl, then Morn, then Sharl. Each loss sends you to the Barricade (5,14), and each time the test walked the same six maps back (and ground to L64, then L67). The sap-lift shortcut exists (Root ↔ Forge once `forge_upper_reached`), but its Barricade stop is an invisible tile at (28,15). The only hints are Ord's after-line ("The lift goes where the levers send it.") and "Somewhere, a sap-lift wakes up". **Fix:** add a rest at the Root-Throne, e.g. the freed Rootwarden from the pens sets up a camp, healing like the woken digger. Make the lift visible (a sap-pillar graphic on page 1). When it wakes, say where it is: "With the Rootbreaker down, the levers are free. The sap-lift by the Barricade gate hums awake." Give the woken digger a line after a loss: "DIGGER: The lift's running again. By the gate. Root to Forge, quick as you like."

**E11 · High · The Heart Chamber does too much in one unbroken run (8.19-8.24).** In order:
1. Two battles (the opening, then the mechs).
2. The six-spire circle.
3. Avarice.
4. Glimmer's death.
5. A boss (the Shard).
6. Three nested choices.
7. The faked death.
8. The launch.

There is one heal in all of it, and Avarice stands idle through steps 4 to 6 without a line. The design order is 8.07, a better fire, then 8.08, the coat falls off; the code puts `scene_heart_choice` between them (`034_Chapter8.rb:675-676`). **Fix:** call `scene_coat_falls` straight after Glimmer, so Avarice reacts to the engine dying while she can. Then fade to black and play the Shard and the heart choice as their own beat in the empty, collapsing chamber.

**E12 · Medium · No urgency after the launch (8.25-8.26).** Reptin shouts "GO!", the Veins say "Run.", and a dragon is flying at Ten-Towns. Next comes a treasure room with five pickups, a mimic battle, a guardian battle, a prophecy and five visions, then "You climb for a long time." **Fix:** move the Echo Vault's loot and the Root-mimic before the Heart Chamber (the Antechapel has room). Keep the Vault to the attendant, the tree's prophecy and the visions, and put the stair right there.

**E13 · Medium · The gate route and the lever route play differently (8.01a, 8.04).** The only payoff for Hew's missing diggers sits in `barricade_gate`: "the diggers stop hauling... start to cry", Wyn's blanket, and Meat Meat's Hew line. The traitor's lever, which he tells you to use and the test used, skips all of it. The diggers then silently switch to their woken line. The "Woken digger" rest NPC is awake from your first step in. **Fix:** play the digger-waking beat the next time you enter 2072 after `traitor_lever`. Show the rest digger only after `barricade_passed`, and before that give the Barricade a different rest (or none).

**E14 · Medium · The captain's advice doesn't match the fight (8.16, 8.21).** "Her engine vents when it gets too hot. When it vents, she gets up again. Don't let it vent twice." There is no heat timer (the design's "four-turn timer" isn't built). The engine "vents" only when you lose, and the second loss is a whiteout. **Fix:** build the timer, or rewrite: "CAPTAIN: Knock her down and the engine vents and stands her back up. It can do that once."

**E15 · Medium · The heart choice hides its rules (8.22).** "Sanctify it" leads to two more choices, and each has one right answer ("Warm it slowly", then "She burned you to keep you..."). Any other pick costs the blessing and starts a battle, with no hint why. The meaning of all three Shard choices is explained only by the optional Ghost of the Shard (8.18), so spine information lives in an optional NPC (guide §7). **Fix:** one reaction box per wrong pick ("The frost bites back. The echo curls tighter.") and one retry. Add a one-box summary in the scene from Alerion: "ALERION: Clean it and he's only a man again. Leave it and she waits in it. Break it and pieces get away."

**E16 · Medium · The chapter's choices are mostly never read back (guide §6).** Grep finds no reader outside this chapter for:
- `mm_turned` / `mm_reached`: the design says "The court will hear which you chose".
- `avarice_doubt`: the careful player's catch.
- `glimmer_has_rose`: Glimmer pockets Shimmer's rose minutes before he dies, and it's never mentioned again.
- `rootbeasts_freed`, `prisoners_freed`, `captain_died`, `razzy_scale`, `corsair_arms_deal`, `knows_great_unchaining`.
- `broke_hag_mirror`, `chapel_broken`, `shard_sanctified`, `traitor_lever`, `personal_visions`, `attendant_paid`, `attendant_promise_broken`.

**Fix:** read the first group at least:
- Ch10's Assassin's Reckoning names which you chose.
- Avarice's return has a line for `avarice_doubt` ("You saw her arms. You didn't say.").
- At the Shrine (9.02), Meat Meat gives Frostina the rose with the dagger.
- The freed Rootwarden and fisherwoman turn up in Rootspire.

**E17 · Low · Optional set-pieces cluster right before the boss (8.18).** The shrine, the golem workshop and the Ghost of the Shard all sit in the last room before the Heart Chamber (guide §7: optional content never right before a boss). The shrine's choice is a real moral choice (`chapel_auril` is read in Ch13). **Fix:** acceptable as a dungeon's last room. Consider moving the golem workshop earlier, to the sanctum, so the final room is the Antechapel's three voices.

**E18 · Low · Blessings spent oddly, and HP lost silently.** The Wennen scout's blessing is used up on the minor effigy trap (8.03), and the fight happens anyway. In the Breaking Circle every Pokémon loses 1/12 HP with no box (8.20, `034_Chapter8.rb:596`). **Fix:** say it ("The whispers find a grip. Your Pokémon flinch."). Let the effigy trap skip its fight when the blessing works.

## 2. First-time player

**F1 · "Who is talking? There's nobody here."** Every party line in the chapter comes from an empty screen (E1). **Fix:** E1.

**F2 · "What is a duergar, and who is Xardorok?"** The chapter's first spoken line is "DUERGAR: Xardorok said you'd come." Neither word is explained there. The explanation comes in an optional room (the traitor, 8.04) or at the very end (Avarice, 8.19). **Fix:** make the first line the design's: "DUERGAR: The Forge-Mistress said you'd come. The failures always return." Introduce the duergar in the narration box before it: "Grey dwarves, duergar, wearing Avarice's sigils..."

**F3 · "Why is a compass talking, and who's 'Father'?"** The beat assumes you remember Hew's gift and his construct praying "Father". **Fix:** add "(Hew's compass, built from his construct's spare parts.)" to the first box: "In your pack, Hew's compass, the one built from his construct's parts, starts ticking faster."

**F4 · "Everyone keeps warning me about a heart. Mine?"** These all arrive before the game says what the heart is:
- "The heart in her engine isn't hers" (8.05).
- "Don't let her take the heart..." (8.06).
- "She will take your heart." (8.16).

The answer, Sephek's heart, comes only at 8.14. **Fix:** Glimmer's "Everybody down here keeps warning us about a heart." (8.06) gets an answer: "ALERION: The engine runs on one. Somebody's." Rewrite the mirror-hall line to "She will take the heart."

**F5 · "Who are Sephek, Brightmane, Frostina, Hyphora and Kelyn?"** The two biggest scenes stack names:
- 8.14: Hyphora, Sephek, Brightmane and the Rimefang in three boxes.
- 8.21: Saltmarsh, Brightmane, the Rimefang, Shimmer, the Lumen Shard and Frostina.

The guide asks for at most one new proper noun per beat. These aren't new to the game, but a player who forgot them gets no reminder. **Fix:** plain-word tags on first use in the chapter:
- "And that's his heart. Sephek's. Auril's drowned saint."
- "This blade's been killing me since the night I picked it up."
- "Tell Frostina, my sister's dragon, it was a better fire."

**F6 · "What are an umber hulk, a quaggoth, a myconid, a mind flayer, a barbed devil? Why are they Pokémon?"** These are D&D monsters named only by their D&D names. Each turns into a battle against a fusion Pokémon called "Umber hulk", which looks nothing like the description. **Fix:** describe each by what you see and keep the name as flavour, e.g. "a pale, blind, burrowing brute (the duergar call them umber hulks)".

**F7 · "I lost. Why am I back at the start of the dungeon, and why does it say Pokémon Center?"** **Fix:** E10 and E26.

**F8 · "What do the spires do? Scour or break?"** "Scour her glyphs and the frost shows through. Break both and nothing does." says what happens to the runes, not what it does for you. **Fix:** one plain box after it: "(Scour a spire: Avarice draws less, but Auril hears you. Break one: quieter, but the backlash burns your lead. Six, then she fights.)"

**F9 · "Why did Glimmer have to die? Did I do something wrong?"** "This blade's been killing me slowly since Saltmarsh" leans on a curse the chapter never restates, and the player does nothing in the moment. **Fix:** set the curse up at the forge rest ("GLIMMER: The Rimefang's colder every day. Brightmane says a sacred fire will break it."). Give the player one input at the moment: a choice to reach for him, which doesn't change the outcome.

**F10 · "Avarice said she'll see us again. Why does everyone say she's dead?"** **Fix:** E19.

**F11 · "What's the difference between Sanctify, Leave it whole and Destroy?"** **Fix:** E15.

**F12 · "A dragon is flying at Ten-Towns. Why am I picking seeds out of a wall?"** **Fix:** E12.

**F13 · "What's the White Womb? Vessel IX? The Dissectory? The whispering attendant?"** Hooks are fine, but four parenthetical memory boxes in 8.22-8.23 assume you saw optional Labyrinth rooms. **Fix:** keep one per beat, written so it reads without the backstory: "(The note you found in the labyrinth: Vessel IX. A spare body, kept warm.)"

**F14 · "Who is Mother Thruuna?"** Thredra names herself by an alias the player has never heard, in a 123-character box. **Fix:** see the Dialogue table; cut the alias or tie it to Rootspire's stories.

**F15 · "Where do I go after I pull the lever?"** The goal "Down, into the hag's old lair." doesn't say the way is back through the Barricade gate, east. **Fix:** "Back to the Barricade gate. It's open now: east, and down into the hag's old lair."
