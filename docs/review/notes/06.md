# 06 · Chapter 6: The Festival of Returning Light — review notes

Scene numbers refer to `docs/review/script/06.md`. Severity: **High** confuses or blocks the player or breaks the story; **Medium** weakens it; **Low** is polish.

## 1. Editor

### Staging and animation

**E1 · High · Every Herald speaks from nowhere, all chapter (6.01–6.26).** No scene in Chapter 6 puts a Herald on the map. `Stage.herald` exists (043_Stage.rb) and `stage_act` is defined in 024 for this purpose, but no Ch6 scene calls it. The log marks every Herald line `absent`: the reunion with the dragons, Shade stepping into the mirror, the Council oath, the coffee scene. The player watches one sprite (Hellen, Kelyn, Eline) answer five invisible people. *Fix:* at the start of each story scene, `Stage.herald(key, dx, dy, dir)` for every companion who has lines, in a fixed formation round you (two left, two right, one behind), and `Stage.cleanup` at the end. Do it first in 6.01, 6.02, 6.03, 6.04, 6.07, 6.08, 6.14, 6.22 and 6.23.

**E2 · High · Speech bubbles point at the wrong people (6.02, 6.04, 6.05, 6.14).** `say()` (007_Story.rb:26-30) falls back to the event you talked to when it can't find a speaker. So Shade's and Alberica's lines in the Hearth, and Shade's, Alberica's and Meat Meat's in the cellar, get **Hellen's** bubble, and Glimmer's "That's him. Sephek." gets **Eline's**. In the Council Hall `Stage.speaker` matches the stone statues by name, so Reptin's "Let me do the talking" and Meat Meat's "Mirrors remember" bubble out of their statues. *Fix:* only fall back to `event_id` when the tag matches that event's name; skip events whose name starts with "Statue" in `Stage.speaker` (043_Stage.rb:758). Both fixes become visible the moment E1 is done, so do them together.

**E3 · High · The dragons land in an empty market (6.03).** "Two dragons come down out of the gold sky… He shoves his enormous head into Meat Meat's chest" plays over a still market: no shadow, no sprite, no shake for "the cobbles crack", no cry, no Lumin. The sprites `FT_razzilith` and `FT_frostina` already exist (on the Canopy map). *Fix:* `Stage.actor(:razzilith, …)` and `:frostina` above the top edge, `Stage.walk` them down to you, `$game_screen.start_shake` on "lands so hard", `Stage.cry`, Lumin as an actor at the crowd's edge with an "!" emote; walk the dragons off north for "lift off together".

**E4 · High · Festival night is the ordinary daytime market (6.22).** The night that the whole week waits for plays over Tamsin, Pip and the stalls in their daytime places, with the camera on the Hearth door. The Circle is said to be at the Council Hall's steps (20,28); the Summer Throne, the lanterns, the effigies, the crowd, Kelyn, Eirwen and Wyn are not on the map; Rheven, Lirielle and Hew stand 18 tiles away, off camera. The market theme keeps playing ("The music falters" over unchanged music). *Fix:* transfer to (20,30) facing the Hall; give the market a night page under SW_FESTIVAL (dim tone, lantern events, the throne, two effigy events, a ring of dancers, townsfolk moved in); `Stage.actor` Kelyn at the steps and Eirwen by the throne; `Stage.pan_to` Rheven for his lines; Wyn walks in from the Root-Gate with Sableye; `Stage.theme` a festival tune and `theme(nil)` on the shudder.

**E5 · High · The Council summons never takes you to the Council (6.25).** "The runner takes you straight to the Council Hall of Seasons. All four crystals are lit, and nobody is sitting down." Then Kelyn, Selrae and Darrus speak while you're still in the Library; you walk out onto the Canopy. *Fix:* `transfer(2054, 14, 15, 8)` between the runner's line and Kelyn's (048_F:716-719 → 024:508), or end the audience on the runner and play `council_summons` when you talk to Kelyn, with the goal "The Council Hall of Seasons. Now."

**E6 · Medium · Long talk with nothing moving.** The script's ACTION lines record the moments the text describes and the map doesn't show: Selrae "steps down from her seat" (6.04), Hellen "dropping a tray" (6.02), Eline "turning" and "slips out through a back door" (both disciples vanish at once, 6.14), the cultists "run for the root-lift" (vanish, 6.07), Wynsnow measuring you and "the air over his desk splits" (6.09, 6.11), Frostina "drops down past the Rift" and "pacing the crown" (her sprite stays put, 6.08, 6.09a), Eirwen "closing the Library's doors" (6.25). *Fix:* `Stage.walk` / `turn_to` / `leave` for each; a flash plus an E- effect at Wynsnow's desk; walk Frostina's shrine sprite to the Rift for 6.08.

**E7 · Medium · The Rift (6.08) has one effect for 56 boxes.** The aurora overlay and one flash carry Shade's whole transformation. Shade, Brightmane and Glimmer are never seen, and the player can't tell that the party is standing at the Rift. *Fix:* Shade as an actor who walks into the light (Stage.leave), a two-beat panel (the plain of stars; Glimmer stepping out), Glimmer's actor sprite walking back to you, `Stage.theme(:heralds)` when he names himself (the design asks for *Thaw*, then *Heralds*).

**E8 · Medium · Visions and vision-cuts with nothing to look at.** The Dreaming of the Bloom Guard (6.07) uses no O-03 overlay or panel, though WRITING_GUIDE §9 makes O-03 the vision signifier and the Codex (6.16) uses it. ### Structure and pacing

**E10 · High · No-input runs far past the 25-box limit (WRITING_GUIDE §2.3).** The Council (6.04) is 62 boxes with one yes/no. The orb (6.07) is about 60, 44 of them unbroken narration. The Rift (6.08) is 56 with one choice that changes nothing. Night 2 (6.16) is 50 boxes from Hellen's greeting to Reptin's last line. The coffee banter (6.23) is 34. Festival night (6.22) runs about 100 boxes with three choices. *Fix:* Council: end the first scene on the oath; play the motto, Kethan/Rheven, the terrace argument and the rite in a second beat when you next talk to Kelyn. Play Selrae's court as she catches you at the door: she walks to you, and it's its own scene. Orb: show one Herald's vision per touch of the orb (or when you talk to each Herald's dragon), each under O-03 with a panel. Night 2: the Codex is one night, Alberica's rumours the next, the Glasswater view a third. Festival: cut the opening narration from 9 boxes to 3 (the scenery should be on the map, E4).

**E11 · Medium · The week is six identical rests.** You must talk to Hellen six times between the rite and the festival. Each opens with the same three boxes ("Back again! Sit, sit…", the countdown, "Spend the days…"). That's 18 repeated boxes, and nights 4 to 6 add little the player acts on. *Fix:* after the first rest, one varied greeting line per night and no "Spend the days" box; offer "Rest until the festival's last night?" once the week's beats you can see are done; give each night one player action (nights 4-6 only offer "Go with her?").

**E12 · Medium · The clock contradicts itself.** Selrae says the rite is "tonight", Kelyn "if the Halo moves tonight", Eline "Tonight we cool the tree", and a festival-goer says "The Festival of Returning Light is tonight!" (024:123). Then the goal says the festival runs seven days and its last night is six rests away. Every heal at Hellen is a night (048_E:162-176), so a player who heals before the rite burns nights while it stays "tonight". *Fix:* don't advance `rs_day` until `rite_stopped`; change the festival-goer to "The festival's on all week! The seventh night's the big one."; Selrae: "There's a rite at the Still Sky Shrine tonight, the first night of the festival."

**E13 · Medium · Beats run in the wrong order inside a night.** In 6.23 "Morning. Hellen sets a black iron pot on the bar" comes first, then "Dawn over Rootspire", then "Past midnight, someone hammers on the Hearth's door". In night 6 the dawn walk comes before "You wake in the dark, sweating". In night 2 Alberica spends "the next day" carousing and learns the rumours "by night", then the Glasswater view is "at dawn". Spring's name "has bloomed overnight" on the same festival night. *Fix:* in `hearth_night`, play the dawn line after night-time beats, not before; move `hearth_coffee` to the morning after the Rootwardens; gate `:spring_name` on `rs_day` past the festival.

**E14 · Medium · Coffee banter lands on the heels of "something is waking below" (6.22 → 6.23).** The comic set piece plays at the first rest after the ominous festival. *Fix:* it's the design's rest banter (SC 6.02a): move it to the morning after the scorch marks and Eirwen, or before the festival (it needs Wyn, so: the morning after the summons, before the descent).

## 2. First-time player

**F1. "Who's talking? I can see one person and five voices."** Every Herald is invisible (E1). *Fix:* E1.

**F2. "Where am I, and why is it four hundred years later?"** The arrival is six boxes of description. The reason is two lines from Hellen ("You broke the hag's mirrors, and a seed took root." / "The tree grew in one night that lasted four hundred years."), and the Wall is never said to be a ring. *Fix:* E18's line; and Tamsin's "You were gone an HOUR" already gives the hook: let Hellen answer it: "HELLEN: An hour for her. Four hundred years for the city."

**F3. "Heralds of Dawn and Dusk? The hag? Avarice? Auril? Sephek? The Rimefang? The White Womb?"** The Council and Selrae's court bring in eight proper nouns in one sitting (§8: one per beat). *Fix:* cut Selrae's "Avarice has a vault in the ice to the north. The White Womb." to "SELRAE: There's a vault in the ice to the north. She'd make you something there." Gloss Sephek at the altar: "GLIMMER: (very quiet) ...That's him. Sephek. The Frost Saint." Give Hellen "the Heralds of Dawn and Dusk. The ones who broke the winter."

**F4. "Who's Anduin?"** "REPTIN: (very quietly) Anduin is out there." *Fix:* D7.

**F5. "Summer is a man or a woman?"** Darrus: "Our Summer Marshal, Var Rime-Breaker… He hasn't come back." Razzilith: "Summer is a woman. A tiefling." Eline: "We gave it to Summer." With no frame, it reads as a mistake, not a clue. *Fix:* keep the clue but mark it as one: "RAZZILITH: Summer wears the Marshal like a coat. Inside: a woman. Horns." (49), and Meat Meat: "Razzy says the coat is lying. Meat Meat believes Razzy."

**F6. "What's a Wennen? A tabaxi? A myconid? A Feycorn?"** "Wennen" is used (Lumin, Lirielle) and never explained; Glimmer is "a tall cat-man"; Wyn's "myconids" come with no gloss. *Fix:* one gloss each, first time: "LUMIN: (a rabbit-eared Wennen in priest's robes, trembling)", "WYN: …Through the myconids. The mushroom folk. I can take you."

**F7. "Is the cellar a gym? Where's the fourth badge?"** E15.

**F8. "What do I do for six nights?"** The goal names people you haven't met (E17), and the week is rest, rest, rest (E11).

**F9. "What was the orb telling me? Do I choose one of the two roads?"** 44 boxes of prophecy show two fates per Herald and no choice, and nothing says these are futures to steer between. *Fix:* end on Lumin: "LUMIN: Two roads each. The wyrms will follow whichever you walk. Choose well, guides." Or make the per-Herald visions (E10) each end on a small choice that sets a flag.

**F10. "What is 'Something only Alberica sees'? Is the screen glitching?"** A blue tint, an Ice8 sound and a parenthesis, with no set-up. *Fix:* E30, and give the first omen a reaction the player sees: Alberica's "I'm fine, darlings" on the first one, not the third.

**F11. "Grafting? I can already buy DNA Splicers in this shop."** Wynsnow explains Grafting's rules, but not how it differs from the Splicers sold two doors down. *Fix:* "WYNSNOW: Splicers fuse two for a while. A graft is for keeps." (41)

**F12. "Why didn't the Council show up when they summoned me?"** E5.

**F13. "Warm scorch marks — in a city that's always warm?"** D1.

**F14. "Who are the Halo, and why are two of them pouring something into an orb?"** The Fractured Halo is never introduced as a name before the orb; Selrae's "If the Halo moves tonight" is the first mention. *Fix:* "KELYN: The Fractured Halo. Auril's last faithful. They pray for the cold to come back." when Selrae first says it.

**F15. "Bough circle? Claw circle?"** Rheven, the initiates and the Rootwardens at the door use circle names that nobody explains. *Fix:* drop them on the spine ("Two Rootwardens, soot on their sleeves.") or give one line: "RHEVEN: Rootwardens go in circles. Bough walks the Wall. Claw guards the gates."
