# 04 · Chapter 4: Dougan's Hole — review notes

`game/Data/Scripts/998_FrostThaw/` unless a path is given. Box lengths count `NAME: `.

## 1. Editor

### High

**E1 · Whole chapter · No Herald is ever on screen.** Ch4 has no `Stage.herald` or `Stage.actor` call (022_Chapter4.rb, 045_HoledUp.rb); the only staging is the mirror crack. About 100 Herald boxes (Alberica, Reptin, Alerion, Shade, Meat Meat) come from nobody visible. That includes every scene's banter, Reptin's "Behind me. Now." to children who also aren't there, and the bite, the moment Reptin's whole arc grows from. Part 05 opens by staging Reptin with `Stage.actor`, so the tool works; Ch4 just never uses it. WRITING_GUIDE §9a: "A line from someone the player can't see is a bug."
*Fix:* in every scene, call `Stage.herald(:key, dx, dy)` for each Herald who speaks, around the player, then `Stage.cleanup` at the end. Do the big ones first: 4.05 arrival, 4.10 the pack (Reptin kneels, pose + a B- balloon on the bite), 4.19 homecoming, 4.22 the hag.

**E2 · 4.03, 4.06, 4.07, 4.20, 4.22, smokehouse · Herald lines get the NPC's speech bubble.** When `say()` can't find a speaker it falls back to the event that started the scene (007_Story.rb:28-31). So the Brewmistress seems to say "A mead hall with no mead. It's like a bed with no company." Durmoot seems to say Meat Meat's "Meat Meat would still bite her." The hag seems to say Alerion's "...Boy." and Alberica's "you dried-up old...". Hilda seems to say Alberica's "Keep the bowls out, love". The Smoker seems to say Shade's line about Shimmer. These lines also never reach the off-stage log, so the sweep under-counts the problem (no `[offstage]` lines for the 5 Heralds in the mead hall, log 42-46).
*Fix:* fall back to the event only when the tag matches that event's name; never for a tag in `COMPANION_NAMES`. With E1 fixed, the Herald's own stand-in is found first.

**E3 · 4.19 · The homecoming plays to an empty street.** It's an autorun on map 2035, so it fires wherever you arrive. In the log that's the west gate (1,20), 20 tiles from Hilda's door (21,11). Silja, Finn, Hilda and Durmoot are all absent, and nothing moves for 19 boxes. Silja "off down the street, braids flying", Hilda "runs out barefoot" and Durmoot "from her door" are all narration over a still frame, at the emotional payoff of the chapter's main quest.
*Fix:* use `Stage.actor` for Silja and Finn beside you; `Stage.walk_to` them to Hilda's door; put Hilda on screen at (21,11) with `Stage.hop`; give her an `approach` with the boots; pan to Durmoot at her door (14,10) for her line; then `Stage.leave` the children into the house. Move the trigger to the street, or start it with an escort to Hilda's door.

**E4 · 4.17-4.19 · The children vanish at the cage and aren't seen again until Hilda's house.** `free_kids` sets switch 3919 and the cage page goes blank, so nobody steps out of the gate. On the tundra Shade says "Stay behind us. All the way home", Reptin says "Behind me. Now." and Silja asks "...Can we go home now?", all with no children on the map (log 296).
*Fix:* Silja and Finn walk out of the cage (`Stage.walk`), then follow one step behind you (a follower or escort). In the ambush, place them behind you and `turn_to` them for Reptin's line.

**E5 · 4.20-4.21, 4.25 · Night starts at the gym door, and morning comes without sleep.** Durmoot says "...Stay in tonight." and the goal says "Rest for the night." But `scene_dh_night` is an autorun on switch 3921, and the night tint is applied as the map loads, so it's night the moment you step out. You can't rest. Then the morning starts as soon as the hag's thaw ends: a 40-frame tint lift and "Morning." with no fade, sleep or time skip (022_Chapter4.rb:622-626). The design's trigger is "try to rest" (act1_script SC 4.09) and SC 4.10 is "wake".
*Fix:* when you rest at the smokehouse with the seal, fade to black, show "That night...", and play `scene_dh_night` in the street. After the thaw, `pbFadeOutIn` with a "..." box, and wake at the Stones or the smokehouse for SC 4.10.

**E6 · 4.17, 4.22, 4.23 · Three spine steps have the wrong goal.** (a) After the children are freed, the goal still reads "Follow the wolves' tracks west across the tundra, to Garagai's lodge." (b) After the hag's parley, it still reads "The Twenty Stones. Find who's calling.", although you've found her and her task is to break the mirrors. (c) After the third mirror nothing tells you to go back to her; the last box is "the crone stops laughing". WRITING_GUIDE §9a rule 3 requires a goal for every step.
*Fix:* `free_kids`: "Take Silja and Finn home to Dougan's Hole, east across the tundra." · after the parley: "Break Thredra's three ice mirrors among the Twenty Stones." · after mirror 3: "The mirrors are broken. Face Thredra at the centre stone."

**E7 · 4.24-4.25 · The chapter's climax isn't staged.** Thredra's event goes blank on switch 3922. Her last two lines are said without her event id, and the event is called "The Hag", so they find no speaker and show no bubble (log 463-466). "She splits into shards and blows away south" plays no effect. "Out on the lake, the frozen figures breathe" happens 9-30 tiles away with no pan. The FISHERMAN who "looks up at you" is absent: his shape at (30,31) is 20 tiles away and isn't named FISHERMAN. Durmoot "comes down the hill" while she's still inside her lodge (4 boxes absent).
*Fix:* say her lines with `event_id`, or rename the event "Thredra". Play E-06 on her, then `Stage.hide`. Use `Stage.pan_to(28,34)` for the thaw, holding as the shapes change page, then `Stage.pan_to(30,31)` for the husband and `turn_to` the player. Put Durmoot on screen with `Stage.actor` at her door, have her walk down to you (`walk_to`), and give her a lantern pose.

### Medium

**E8 · 4.09 · Kanan is named before he's introduced, and the wolves are said to walk beside you while they're 37 tiles away.** On the default "Show us." path the second wolf was only "something else white... just out of sight". The tundra then says "Koran and Kanan fall in on the windward side". Kanan's name first reaches the player there, in narration, and the wolves are actually sitting outside the lodge. §9a rule 1: introduce before you use.
*Fix:* narrate the second wolf without a name (dialogue table, row 11), and have Kanan name himself at the lodge (row 13). Better still, stage both wolves walking with you for a few tiles, then `Stage.leave` them west.

**E9 · 4.08 · Koran doesn't limp, doesn't leave, and the second wolf is never shown.** Koran is a standing sprite, but the text says he "limps out of the snow on one back leg". The "Which leg was it, again?" option invites you to watch for something the sprite never shows. After the scene he blinks out (self switch A).
*Fix:* a limp pose or a short hop-step move route on the left leg; for the "Which leg" branch, switch legs on screen. `Stage.leave(koran, 0, 22)`, and a brief Kanan actor crossing behind him at the screen edge.

**E10 · 4.18 · Two "Two white shapes" boxes in a row that disagree.** `lodge_east_exit` says "Two white shapes are waiting at the openings, exactly where they said they'd be." Then `wolves_ambush` opens with "Two white shapes rise out of the blizzard ahead of you, side by side." (log 284-285). They're waiting, and then they rise out of the blizzard.
*Fix:* give `wolves_ambush` a `from_door:` flag that skips its first box (045_HoledUp.rb:488, 495).

**E11 · 4.02 · Olivessa speaks from inside the cooperage, then repeats herself when you meet her.** Her arrival line plays on map 2031 while she's indoors on 2033 (log 24). Her first line inside the cooperage is the same "Headed to the Hole? Eat first..." The design says she's the person you pull from the burning hall in Ch9, so the player needs her face now.
*Fix:* add Olivessa to Good Mead as a `Stage.actor` who walks up with a honey cake (`approach`) and then `leave`s to the cooperage door (7,16). Give her cooperage line a new opener (dialogue table, row 4).

**E12 · 4.05 · The arrival describes panes of ice that aren't on the map, and the shapes can't be examined.** "Between them, panes of ice stand upright, like mirrors." But the mirror events exist only at the Stones, and only after the badge. The shapes' first page has no script, so pressing A on them does nothing; the design asks for an examine line (act1_script SC 4.02 Flow). There's also no pan to the lake, so at (41,1) the player sees almost none of what's described.
*Fix:* add 3-4 non-interactive ice-pane props among the shapes. Give the shapes the design's examine line: "A man with a fishing line, mid-cast. His breath is frozen in the air in front of him." Then `Stage.pan_to(30,30)`, the four boxes, `pan_back`.

**E13 · 4.19 · The boots promise a mechanic that doesn't exist.** Hilda: "They'll not slip on ice, not ever." The item text says the same, but `FT_WINTERBOOTS` is only given (045_HoledUp.rb:31-32, 73), and the Dougan's Hole lake ice still slides. A player who tries them on Redwaters will think the game is broken.
*Fix:* skip ice sliding while the boots are owned (the obvious reading), or cut the claim from the line and the item text.

**E14 · 4.19 · A joke straight after grief, at the town's expense.** Hilda's dead husband, then "He was my older brother too", then SHADE "(very quietly) ...Her husband was her brother." / ALERION "Small town." It's RotF canon, but WRITING_GUIDE §3 says no joke right after grief, except one line from Alberica. With the chorus's "small, misshapen ears" and "little pointed teeth" it also reads as mocking the town. Voices.md: Alberica never "mocks anyone's pain", and this is the scene she cries in.
*Fix:* cut "He was my older brother too" and the Shade and Alerion lines (rows 22-23). Keep Alberica's and Meat Meat's not-crying pair.

**E15 · 4.22 · The player isn't told why to break the mirrors, and Thredra asks you to break her own power.** World rule 1 (mirrors hold the frozen people) is never said. "Break my mirrors, dearies. If you can bear to look in them." reads as the villain asking you to undo her, and the thaw afterwards comes as a surprise instead of a goal.
*Fix:* make it a dare with the stakes in it (row 36). Add one Herald line, e.g. ALERION: "She keeps them in the glass. Break it, they go free." (65).

**E16 · 4.22 · Timeline: "The moon's been so cruel to him".** Reptin was bitten that same afternoon. Nobody, Reptin included, knows yet that it's a curse; it first shows in SC 5.01 under a full moon. Yet nobody reacts to "cursed", and Reptin answers as if he already knew there was a wolf in him.
*Fix:* make the line about the bite's future (row 34). Then Reptin's "...She can do that? Take it out of me?" works as fear of something new.

**E17 · 4.22 · Shade says nothing when Thredra names Shimmer.** "And the one who'd been worn before needed no gift from me. She already carried a door." Shimmer died a chapter ago and Shade is her sibling, but Alerion and Alberica react to their epithets and Shade doesn't. WRITING_GUIDE §3: "In a major scene, every Herald who can be present gets a beat."
*Fix:* add `cl(:shade, ...)` after 022_Chapter4.rb:554 (row 29).

**E18 · 4.23 · Three identical mirror scenes, in a chapter about laughing that never plays a laugh.** Each mirror plays the same four boxes and the same narration, and "the crone stops laughing" appears four times. No laughter sound plays anywhere: not at the arrival, the night, the mirrors or her exit. The breathing and coughing in 4.12 are silent too.
*Fix:* give each mirror its own two lines and outcome (rows 37-38). Play a cackle SE on the night's first box and on each mirror's crack, and breathing and cough SEs in the lodge.

**E19 · 4.24 · The husband's message doesn't match what the widow repeats, and needs a branch.** In the scene he says "Tell her not to give the Spheal away". The widow later says "He said to keep the lamp lit" (017_TargosExtras.rb:34), a line you only hear if you go back and talk to his shape at (30,31). If you never took the widow's Spheal, his line refers to nothing. In this run the Spheal has just evolved into Sealeo.
*Fix:* rewrite his line (row 39) and add a `widow_spheal` branch. If the Spheal has evolved: "(She's a Sealeo now. He knows her anyway.)"

**E20 · 4.10 · The bite, the root of Reptin's arc, has no staging.** The pack doesn't move, nobody steps forward, there's no shake or cry, and all four wolves vanish at once afterwards.
*Fix:* stage Reptin kneeling. The she-wolf (31,18) `walk_to`s him and bites (a flash plus a Mightyena cry), then `Stage.leave` the pack west. Add a B- balloon on Reptin for "I'm fine."

**E21 · 4.14 · Norsu is off camera for all six of his lines and never turns.** The text says "It turns, and it stares down at you with hate in its eyes." The log has `NORSU off_camera` throughout.
*Fix:* `Stage.pan_to(19,13)`, `Stage.turn_to(norsu)`, and an angry emote before "You are to blame". Add a Mamoswine cry.

**E22 · 4.07 · Hilda is never on the spine before the rescue.** Her before-scene ("I keep their bowls on the table" / Alberica: "Keep the bowls out, love. We'll fill them.") is the best setup in the chapter, and nothing sends you to her. The test never went in, so the homecoming's payoff lands on someone you've never met.
*Fix:* after Durmoot, set the goal to "Hilda Dejarr's house, then west." Or have Hilda stop you at the west gate (`approach`).

**E23 · 4.19, 4.22, 4.24-4.25 · Long runs with no input.** The homecoming is 19 boxes. The parley is 18 boxes before its one choice. From the hag's defeat to the TM is about 20 boxes, across a night-to-morning jump.
*Fix:* staging covers most of this (E3, E7). Cut the Shade/Alerion pair (E14). Split the hag scene at the choice, and the morning off with a fade (E5).
