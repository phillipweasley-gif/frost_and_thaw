# 00 · Prologue: review notes

The prologue is now staged cleanly: every speaker is on screen, Tamsin walks you everywhere after the starter, and the starter table plays like a Pokémon game's. The problem is what it doesn't explain. It never says who the Heralds are, never ties the mural or the vision to them, and never shows the fall itself, so a newcomer reaches Targos without knowing where or when they are. 22 editor findings: 3 High, 9 Medium, 10 Low.

Files: `005_NewGame.rb` (intro, menus, journey_prologue), `030_Prologue.rb` (scenes), `tools/build_prologue.rb` (maps 2090-2092). Line numbers are as of 2026-11-03.

## 1. Editor

### High

**E1. The opening never says who you are, who the Heralds are, or what happened 400 years ago.** (0.01-0.02)

Before the morning, the player gets one box of history that trails off into a menu: "Four hundred years ago, a winter that would not end gripped Icewind Dale..." Three boxes later comes "The year 400 of the Verdant Reversal" and "Every lantern in the city is lit for the Heralds of Dawn and Dusk." Nothing links the winter to the Reversal or the Heralds, says that the winter ended, or says who you are. Your role ("You've run his errands for years") arrives at the College, about 25 boxes later. Three new proper nouns land in three boxes, against WRITING_GUIDE §8 (one per beat, explained on first use).

*Fix:* a six-box card, split by the menus (005_NewGame.rb:74-75 and 222-224):

- Before the menus: "Four hundred years ago, a winter that would not end gripped Icewind Dale." / "Five strangers stood against it. Rootspire calls them the Heralds of Dawn and Dusk." / "Where the ice broke, a great tree grew in a single night."
- After the menus, over the Rootspire backdrop: "The city in its roots is Rootspire. It counts its years from that night." / "Year 400. Today is the Festival of Returning Light, and every lantern is for the Heralds." / "You run errands for the College. Today you get your first Pokémon."

**E2. The fall isn't set up, isn't shown, and doesn't land.** (0.12-0.13)

The only clues that the lantern is a door to the Heralds' time are the seller's "Real Reversal glass! ... where the old hag's mirrors broke!" and "A dock in a blizzard. Five figures coming down a gangplank." Nobody says Reversal glass holds old moments; "the old hag" is never explained; the five figures aren't tied to the five on the mural. Then Tamsin says "What are you..." and the screen goes white. Nothing happens to your sprite and no line says you fall in. Chapter 1 opens with "You land on frozen planks," so the player has to guess that they went through the glass, that it's the past, and that those five are the Heralds. The Story Bible's beat is "The glass cracks... You fall in."

*Fix:* (a) Give the seller a box after Tamsin's "It's not real": "LANTERN SELLER: Laugh all you like. That glass saw the Heralds' winter. It remembers." (b) Under S-01: "Inside the crack, it's night." / "A dock in a blizzard. Five figures, like the five on Hellen's wall." (c) After Tamsin's line, cut the music and add a pull: a flash on your sprite (or Stage.pose) and "The crack pulls at you like a door in a gale. You fall into the glass." (d) Hold the white until the map changes (see E8). (e) Hand-off note for part 01: Targos's first box should say where and when ("The Dale of Hellen's stories. Four hundred years before you were born.").

**E3. The mural, the one place the Heralds are shown, never says it's them.** (0.11)

"Five heroes, carved in the living wood..." is the prologue's best scene and its only picture of the Heralds. It never uses the word "Heralds", so nothing connects the carving to the lanterns, the stories or Hellen's "Heralds didn't save the Dale..." The Story Bible calls this beat "the Herald carvings". This is what makes the five on the dock, and Chapter 1's strangers, mean anything. It also sets up the Act II payoff ("This one has a mural of me").

*Fix:* "TAMSIN: Look. The Heralds. Every festival I check if the little one moved." and "The Heralds, carved in living wood. One in snow, one in flame, one with a lute." (030_Prologue.rb:410-411).

### Medium

**E4. The mode, version and difficulty menus don't explain themselves.** (0.01)

- Mode: "On the festival night, you fall back..." is wrong: the fall happens in the daytime. Nothing says which mode to pick first. "the Heralds" assumes you know who they are.
- Version: "Auril", "her faithful", "Avarice", "Frostina" and "Razzilith" are five unexplained names in two boxes.
- Difficulty: there's no explanation, and no "What's the difference?" option, unlike the other two menus.

*Fix:* see the Dialogue table for the mode and version lines. Add "What's the difference?" to the difficulty menu, saying what each level changes in this engine.

**E5. Day and night contradict each other.** (0.02, 0.04, 0.08, 0.12)

The Rootspire backdrop is a night scene with lit lanterns under "This morning is the Festival..." The card says "Every lantern in the city is lit". Wynsnow says "The lanterns go up at dusk", the seller says "Lanterns tonight", Hellen says "Lanterns are lit", and the mode menu says "festival night". Meanwhile the scene is morning-to-midday, and S-01 surrounds the globe with lit lanterns.

*Fix:* pick one rule. The lanterns are hung in the morning and lit at dusk, and the fall happens by day. Then: "Every lantern in the city is hung for the Heralds"; seller "Lanterns at dusk, little one!"; Hellen "The lanterns are up". Use a daytime Rootspire backdrop, or move this card's text over the morning instead.

**E6. Poké Balls and Potions come from nobody, before you have a Pokémon.** (0.02)

"You obtained 5 Poké Balls!" shows over the city backdrop with no giver.

*Fix:* Wynsnow gives them after the heal: "WYNSNOW: And take these. Five Poké Balls, two Potions. I want notes on everything." In Herald mode, keep the silent gift or give it in the ship text.

**E7. After the battle the prologue is on rails, and nobody reacts to your first Pokémon.** (0.10-0.12)

From the College door to the fall, four autoruns chain together (market lead → mural → lantern → fall), and there is no free moment until Targos. As a result, these lines can never play: Hellen's "There it is. Your first. Here, it can have a crust...", the crowd's post-battle and post-mural lines, the seller's post-mural line, Tamsin's "Hang on, I'm haggling" and both "Look at it!" lines, and the mural's "Tamsin wants to show you this..." Hellen's warmest lines, the only reaction to your new partner, are dead.

*Fix:* fold one into the mural scene after Hellen's line: "HELLEN: (to your Pokémon) And you. Here's a crust. Everyone eats in my house." Cut or repurpose the rest.

**E8. The fall has no music cut, and the white may flash back to the market.** (0.13)

Azalea Town keeps playing through the shudder, the vision and the fall. The design asks for "Hearth, then silence", and §9a says to cut the music for shocks. In `fall_through` (030:483-492) the tone goes to white, then `start_tone_change(Tone(0,0,0))` starts before `transfer`, so the market can reappear for a moment before Targos (code).

*Fix:* `pbBGMFade(1.0)` at the shudder. Keep the screen white through the transfer and reset the tone on the Targos side.

**E9. The mural is three boxes of narration with no picture.** (0.11)

It's a showpiece (the sixth figure pays off in Act II and the epilogue), but the player reads "Five heroes... One with a wolf..." over plain wall tiles. §9 says showpieces get a panel, not stacked narration. The design's *Heralds* music cue isn't played either.

*Fix:* a mural panel (five carved Heralds left to right, dragons above, the faceless sixth figure low on the right under a root). Play the Heralds theme for the scene.

**E10. Nobody leads you on the first trip to the lift.** (0.04-0.05)

Tamsin leads you everywhere else, but on the one trip where you don't know the market, they run off. The lift is about 15 tiles west and 16 north of the Hearth door, through a crowd. The goal says only "from the market". The only directions ("Root-lift's by the big stair, north of here") come from an optional crowd NPC at the edge of the map. §9a.3 says a character leads you if the way isn't obvious.

*Fix:* Tamsin waits outside the Hearth and escorts you to the lift ("TAMSIN: Lift's this way! Keep up!"), or the goal names the landmark: "Go up to the College for your first Pokémon. The root-lift's by the big stair, north."

**E11. The goal never says why you're going to the College.** (0.03)

The goal reads "Get to the College at the top of the tree. Take the root-lift from the market." The reason (your first Pokémon) is in Tamsin's "The point is POKÉMON" and the optional kid. §7 says every spine fact appears in a line, the goal, and one revisitable source.

*Fix:* goal "Go up to the College for your first Pokémon. The root-lift is in the market."

**E12. The first rival battle plays the gym-leader theme.** (0.09)

The log shows `[music] gymleader`. FT_TAMSIN_I copies LEADER_Brock (026_Rootspire.rb:90). The rival's encounter theme plays before and after, then the battle switches to a gym fight.

*Fix:* give FT_TAMSIN_I a rival battle BGM.

## 2. First-time player

*Questions someone who has never played Pokémon, and doesn't know D&D or the campaign, would ask, in the order the prologue raises them.*

1. **"Which version? What's Auril, Avarice, Frostina, Razzilith?"** I'm asked to choose before I know anything, and the help names four things I've never heard of. → E4: say "the goddess of winter", "a mage with her own plans", "her ice dragon".
2. **"Which mode should I pick? Is Herald Mode harder?"** → Mark New Journey "Best for a first game." Say that Herald Mode starts somewhere else, as someone else.
3. **"What do Easy, Normal and Hard change?"** → E4.
4. **"I'm choosing a look and a name. Who am I?"** Nothing frames the character menu. → One box before it: "Tell us about yourself. You're a kid in Rootspire, about to get your first Pokémon."
5. **"Four hundred years ago a winter... and then? Is it still winter? Who stopped it?"** The sentence ends in "..." and a menu. → E1.
6. **"What is the Verdant Reversal? The World Tree?"** → E1: the tree grew in a night; the city counts its years from it.
7. **"Who are the Heralds of Dawn and Dusk, and why should I care?"** By the fall, I know only that they "saved the Dale" on "pine-mead and spite". I never learn a name, a deed, or why lanterns are lit for them. → E1, E3. One more beat would help: the bard's song names one ("the dwarf sang"). Let Tamsin add, at the mural, "TAMSIN: Five strangers who walked into the winter and came back out with spring."
8. **"Where did these Poké Balls come from?"** → E6.
9. **"Why is it always first light?"** Tamsin answers an objection nobody made. It's a nice seed for the endless summer, but as written it reads like a missed line. → Dialogue table.
10. **"What's a Rootwarden?"** Said three times, never explained. → "TAMSIN: I'm the future Rootwarden. Tree guard. [Name]'s the errand-runner. For now."
11. **"Is Hellen Tamsin's mum? Why does Tamsin call her Hellen?"** → Fine as a character note, but say it once: "Hellen Brightrun, Tamsin's mum, keeps the Rooted Hearth. Nobody has ever left it hungry."
12. **"Where's the market? Where's the root-lift? What's the Canopy-Tier?"** → E10. Lift-keeper: "LIFT-KEEPER: Up to the College? Right at the top of the tree. Hold on to the rail."
13. **"What is a Pokémon? What do I do with it?"** For someone who has never played, nothing says what a Pokémon or a Poké Ball is, or that I'm about to battle. → One Wynsnow box before the table: "WYNSNOW: A Pokémon in each ball. Raise it well and it'll fight beside you. For you, if you're lucky."
14. **"Why did I lose my first fight? Was I meant to?"** Tamsin always takes the type that beats yours, and a newcomer picking Switle faces a 2x Vine Whip. The loss is allowed and Tamsin gloats, but nothing says it was fine. → After a loss, Wynsnow: "WYNSNOW: Lost? Good. Now you know what it hits like."
15. **"What does 'the old hag's mirrors' mean? What's Reversal glass?"** → E2(a).
16. **"Why did the ground shake?"** It's the forge's first heat-pulse, which pays off in Chapter 6. A mystery is fine here, but Tamsin should feel it too, so it's real: "TAMSIN: Did you feel that?" in place of "Nobody else notices".
17. **"What just happened? Where am I? Is this a dream?"** → E2. As it stands, the prologue ends on a white screen and the next chapter starts on a pier.
18. **"Who are the five people in the glass?"** → E2(b), E3.

## 4. Top 5

1. **Rewrite the opening card** (E1) so a newcomer knows who, where, when and why before the morning: the winter, the five strangers, the tree grown in a night, Rootspire's year 400, and you, the College's errand-runner getting your first Pokémon today. Fix "festival night" and explain the menus in plain words (E4).
2. **Name the mural as the Heralds** (E3) and give it a panel and the Heralds theme (E9). It's the bridge between the festival and the whole game.
3. **Land the fall** (E2, E8): the seller says the glass remembers the Heralds' winter, the vision ties the five on the dock to the five on the wall, the music cuts, a line says you fall in, and the white holds through to Targos. Part 01's first box should then say where and when.
4. **Fix the day/night story** (E5): lanterns hung by day, lit at dusk, a daytime backdrop. Bring the spine facts into the goals (E11, E14), and have Tamsin lead you to the lift on the first trip (E10).
5. **Make your first Pokémon matter to someone** (E6, E7): Wynsnow hands over the Poké Balls and Potions, and Hellen gives your partner a crust at the mural. Swap the gym-leader battle music for a rival theme (E12).
