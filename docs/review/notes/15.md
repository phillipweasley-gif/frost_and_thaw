# 15 · Side: Rootspire's quests and townsfolk (Act II) — review notes

Scene numbers refer to `docs/review/script/15.md` (15.01-15.40). Severity: **High** confuses or blocks the player or breaks the story; **Medium** weakens it; **Low** is polish. The owner's complaint for this part is engagement with the NPCs for side quests, so section 1 starts there.

What the run reached: the downtime week's mornings and nights, Hellen's errands (half), the Memory Codex, the Lorn Scribe's four relic quests (the Key and Canticle, the Mantle, the Blade, the Staff), the Still Ones, the Cycle Day rumour, 15 omens, 6 vignettes and the favour track's first boon. It never entered any of the twenty 048 maps (the Verdant Halls, the Promenade, the shops, the Frozen Cellar, the Frozen Run) except the Smuggler's Run, which it crossed for part 10 without the heist. So most of the part is code-only, and most of these findings are about how a player would or wouldn't find it.

## 1. Editor

### How side quests find you (engagement)

**E1 · High · No quest-giver ever comes to you, and nobody signals that they have something.** Not one scene in 048_A-H calls `Stage.approach`, `Stage.emote` or `Stage.walk` (15, header). About forty people hold a quest or a secret. Almost all of them stand still, facing down, and say an introduction that ends on a full stop. The quest is on the **second** talk, with no hint that there is one: Serelle's vision (15.25), Shivven's woman in the ice and Viessa's wax map (15.26), Pimmlet's scroll (15.29), Krolf's boy's voice (15.28), Selene's verses. Mira Thynelight's needs a rest at the Hearth and then a second visit (15.25), and nothing says so. A player who talks to each person once, as Pokémon games train you to, sees none of them. *Fix:* (a) end every first meeting that has a second beat on a hook line the player can act on (see the dialogue table: Shivven "Ask me why. No. Don't." is already that; Viessa, Serelle, Pimmlet and Krolf need one). (b) When the second beat is ready, put a "!" over the NPC (`Stage.emote(ev, :exclaim)` on map entry) and, for the big ones (Mira Thynelight, Kelyn at the orb, Halden, Ivran), have them walk two tiles to you (`Stage.approach`) and speak first.

**E2 · High · Side quests don't reach the Goal, so the pause menu never helps you resume one.** The few quests that write a goal write it with `state[:goal] ||=`: Darrus's heist (048_D:812), Kethan's infiltrator (15.20), the Still Beast (15.21). During Act II a spine goal is always set, so none of them ever shows. The Lorn Scribe's four riddles (15.24) are said once and never written anywhere, though they are the biggest side chain the run played; the Staff's "Bond the Heartwarden's Staff" goal is the only one that does show, and it is never cleared after the bond (15.24). The Frozen Run, the Frostfell portal, the Canticle's "note filed under A", Kelyn's orb and every watcher set nothing. *Fix:* add a side-quest list under the Goal (one line each: who, where, what), written when the ask is made and struck when done; never use `||=` for a side ask; clear the Staff line on `staff_attuned`.

**E3 · High · The new districts are unsigned.** The Verdant Halls are an unmarked door tile at the top of the Bazaar's north lane (15.18). The Lightfall Promenade is a leaf-bridge off the Canopy whose sign lists the Shrine, the Aerie Markets, the Rift, the Library and the College, and not the Promenade (15.25). Both together hold 13 shops, 14 townsfolk, 6 watchers and 9 quests. The run spent seven in-game days in Rootspire and never set foot in either. *Fix:* add "North stair: the Verdant Halls, the civic ring." to the Bazaar's sign and "West, over the leaf-bridge: the Lightfall Promenade." to the Canopy's (048_G TEXT `canopy_sign`); give Hellen's first countdown line a pointer ("HELLEN: The Halls up the north stair, the Promenade over the leaf-bridge. Go and be looked at."); put a townsperson at each entrance with a line that names the place.

**E4 · High · The watchers are a ten-part system the player is never shown how to use (15.38).** Each is a faint, animated Pokémon sprite (opacity 60) that a player sees from day one: a Gengar three tiles from Hellen's bar all week, a Sudowoodo and a Haunter on the Bazaar. Touching one before its hidden condition says only *Something was there. Out of the corner of your eye. Now it isn't.* The condition is "have met its master", and nobody says who the master is. Jast's Lantern of Revealing explains them (15.26), but Jast is in a Root-Tier side shop nobody points to. After a catch, *They'll know you know*, but only four masters ever react (Bryn, Ysera, Reva for the wrong watcher, and the Suncleft door). *Fix:* make the first faint watcher you touch play a two-box explanation (Alerion: "Something's watching us. It's waiting for whoever sent it to be near."); have Kelyn or Hellen mention Jast's lantern; give every master a confession line like Bryn's and Ysera's (Lumea, Gorran, Jast, Eira, Kethan; Avarice's Lurker gets an omen-style line from Alberica).

**E5 · High · Finished quests change nothing and nobody mentions them.** Fifty-two flags set in 048 are never read anywhere (checked by grep across all scripts). Among them the endings of whole quests: `council_support` and `rootspire_haven` (15.36: Kelyn's "Whatever you need" and "Rootspire as your home"), `naerth_ledger_published/_sealed/_council` (15.35), `rootwardens_favour` (Rheven's "The Rootwardens owe you one. I'll remember.", 15.37), `glimmer_hope/_judgment` (the envoy, 15.12), `kelyn_warned`, `yeti_child_home`, `harget_paid/_reported`, `wynsnow_confessed`, `chronal_glimpse`, `helion_known`, `icy_avatar_fought`. Kelyn, after the Council supports you, says nothing new; Eirwen in the trial week asks whether to show the ledger "to the court" whatever you decided (15.35, 036:776). The heist is the model to copy: exposing Darrus makes Maera take his place at the Forge, and holding him to his word pays for the voyage in Chapter 11 on screen (15.30). *Fix:* give each ending one reader. Cheapest: a rotating "news" line in Hellen's rest greeting keyed to the last finished quest ("HELLEN: The whole Bazaar's saying you pulled a sliver of Auril out of Elitha's ice. Is that true?"), and one party-chat line per ending; the trial (036) should read `naerth_ledger_*`; the Council's first session (047d) should read `council_support`.

**E6 · Medium · Asks that aren't asked.** Several quests start with the game saying you did something you didn't choose to: *You mention Summer. Maera freezes.* (15.25), *You point at the black grit* (Jorik, 15.26), *You show her Pellar's dropped note* (Kelyn, 15.33), *You put a lump of the black, warm ore… on his anvil* when you never picked any up (Gorran, 15.28). Eirwen's Canticle research opens "The Canticle. Yes." though nobody has asked her about it (15.24). *Fix:* turn each into a choice the player makes ("Mention Summer?" / "Show Jorik the grit?"), and give the ore crate an item (a lump of Warm Ore) that Gorran takes.

**E7 · Medium · Choices that are the same choice.** Halden's "No." and "Break the engine." play the same branch, and neither breaks the engine (15.29). Wynsnow's "Pay the Chronal Debt?" has no "yes" (15.29). The petitions: showing any finding wins and appealing always loses, so it's not a choice (15.36). "Meat Meat's way, or yours?" is the prompt for four choices none of which is labelled as either (15.30). The heist wards' right answer is always the first option. *Fix:* make "Break the engine" a short scene (the rings shatter, Halden sits down in the wreck); change Wynsnow's prompt to "Wynsnow waits." with a third option, "Anchor us. Show us everything." (with a real cost); give each Council seat a preference (Winter wants the dagger, Autumn the reliquary) so the order matters; rotate the right answer among the wards and change the prompt to "How do you get past?".

**E8 · Medium · Cross-wired quest logic.** (a) Catching **Maera's** Garden-Whisper Weasel makes **Reva** confess to spying for **Lumea** (048_C:148, 15.28). (b) Krolf's boy's voice and the frozen hunter set the same flag (`talion_foreshadowed`), so whichever plays first locks out the other; the run saw the hunter, so Krolf's scene can never play (15.28). (c) Selrae's confession needs `eline_named_selrae` and not `rite_stopped`, which only happens if you lose to Eline (15.33). (d) Mira's denial at her stall can never play: her own text table sets `mirror_twin_known` first (15.28). (e) Lumea asks you to "bring home" a fox that sits in her shop (15.25). (f) Halden's engine stays on the map after you talk him round (15.29). *Fix:* (a) gate Reva on `watcher_moth_swarm` or the Rune Beetle (Lumea's), or rewrite her line to name Maera; (b) give Krolf his own flag and let the hunter's Alerion line follow up ("Krolf heard him too."); (c) move Selrae's confession to her Hearth back-door visit after the Rite; (d) drop the stall line or move the flag set after it; (e) move the fox to the Promenade (15.16's vignette fox already walks there); (f) clear SW_HALDEN on `halden_done`.

**E9 · Medium · Retries replay the whole scene.** Knocking out the Rime Stalker (the run did) replays all twelve boxes of the frozen hunter, Alberica's apology and the TALION reveal included (15.24). Saying "No" to Felka, Darrus hiding, Ivran or the Guild assessor replays their whole pitch next time (15.27, 15.28, 15.30). Kelyn at the orb replays six boxes each "Not tonight". *Fix:* after the first time, open on one line ("ALERION: It's back. Gently, this time.") and go straight to the battle or the choice.

### Staging and animation

**E10 · High · People speak where they aren't.** Eirwen is "waiting, barefoot" at the Council pillar and speaks seven boxes there with no sprite (played, 15.24). Kelyn and Selrae speak in the Library for the ledger (15.35); Ivran calls "from the rail" of a sledge-ship that isn't on the Frozen Run (15.27); Mira "unlocks it herself" from aboard the Requiem (15.26); Kethan speaks from nowhere at Ash's arrest (15.20); the garment-double "walking toward you" never appears (15.25); Darrus "runs" and stays at his seat (15.30); Jast speaks "behind you, very close" from his counter (15.26); Elitha speaks "behind you" from beside the sculpture (15.25). *Fix:* `Stage.actor` Eirwen at the pillar for the Canticle; Kelyn and Selrae walk in for the Council branch of the ledger, or move the scene to the Council Hall; place a sledge-ship event (a boat sprite moving east with `Stage.walk` each time a revenant falls) and Ivran on it; Mira walks down the gangplank to the strongbox; the double as a `Stage.actor` copy of the player that walks two tiles and lunges; Darrus `Stage.leave`s south; Jast `Stage.approach`es.

**E11 · Medium · Big moments told over a still map.** The Verdant Surge ("Roots tear up through the platform, thrashing, blooming, burning", 15.33), the reliquary smashed ("a sound like a thousand mirrors breaking", 15.30), the Festival of Balance ("That night the whole city comes", 15.36), the bonding night at the Wall (four seasons in four boxes, no tone change, 15.24), Eirwen's flashback (an overlay, nobody placed, 15.29), the Canticle's choir ("very faint, the crystals show other singers", 15.24). *Fix:* one tone per season at the Wall (`$game_screen.start_tone_change`); a green flash and shake for the surge; SE "Glass" + flash for the reliquary; place two or three actors (Duvessa, Alberica) in the Bryn Shander flashback; a fade-to-night and three townsfolk moved into the Hall for the Festival of Balance.

**E12 · Low · Four sprite pairs share a face.** Solvaran and Filgen are both "BW (109)"; Halden and Darrus "BW (55)"; Selene and Elitha "BW (107)"; Eirwen and Kelyn "BW (30)" (story bible table). Darrus is on the Council map and Halden in the Library, two floors apart. *Fix:* give each a distinct sprite, starting with Darrus/Halden and Eirwen/Kelyn, who share scenes with the player in the same act.

### Structure and pacing

**E13 · Medium · The relic quests are good and the Lorn Scribe hands them out in one breath (15.03, 15.24).** Four riddles in four boxes, eighteen boxes with no input, then never repeated. They map to four of the Heralds (Alberica, Alerion, Meat Meat, Reptin) and each pays off with a strong personal scene. *Fix:* the Scribe gives one riddle per visit ("Come back when the key is found."); each riddle goes into the side-quest list (E2); the relevant Herald says one line on hearing theirs ("REPTIN: First Wardens. In sap. I know where that is.").

**E14 · Medium · Long runs of narration with no input.** The Codex morning (38 boxes, 15.03), Meat Meat's undercity walk (21-23, 15.06), the Lorn Scribe (18), the Canticle (about 32 boxes with three choices), the Frozen Run's ending (7 boxes after the last revenant then a hard cut). *Fix:* split the Codex morning across two mornings; in the Frozen Run, stop on Ivran's "Look. Under the ice." and let the player walk to the edge and press A on the ice to see the shape.

**E15 · Medium · The clock contradicts itself.** The Canticle "has to finish before dawn" and plays at midday (15.24); "By dawn the staff hums" at midday; "That night… Rootspire's dawn shifts" with no night (Tree Communion, 15.32); "Night on the Canopy" for Kelyn by day (15.33); Eirwen wants to publish "at the festival" after the festival (15.35); "By the next morning…" with no morning (15.35). The summer's age: thirty years (Tree Communion, the petition bench, Kelyn 047d), "a year" (the Suncleft window vignette, 048_E:530), "since the Reversal" (the Suncleft sign, 048_B:55), and Alberica's "Thirty years shut" for the Suncleft (048_B:612), which is the brewery's number. *Fix:* use "thirty years" everywhere for the summer and the Suncleft; for night scenes, fade to a night tone or say "Come back after dark" and gate on the Hearth's rest.

**E16 · Medium · Hidden gates on party, chapter and day.** The key needs Alberica, the blade Meat Meat, the staff and Tree Communion Reptin, the mantle Alerion, the heist Meat Meat as Wardens; Felka, Halden, Ivran's run and the blank scrolls wait for Chapter 7; Kelyn's orb for Day 3; Thynelight's double for "a rest after you met her". Without the right person the text says only "Whatever it's waiting for, it isn't you." *Fix:* when a gate is a Herald, let another Herald say who it's waiting for ("ALBERICA: That lock wants Meat Meat's fingers, darling."); when it's a chapter, give the NPC a line that says "not yet".

### Continuity

**E17 · Medium · Who knows what.** Ivran's Frozen Run quotes Shivven ("Shivven saw her") whether you met Shivven or not (15.27). Alberica names "Lumea's moths, Maera's weasels" before you've seen either (15.25). Filgen's scrolls whisper "a name you don't know: Helion" after you've met Helion's mural (15.25). Wyn's "I said. I said it was wrong" refers to a party-chat line at the Guild dig that may not have been heard and may not have happened yet (15.28). Darrus is called "a tiefling" in Chapter 11 (047d:850), the only time his race is given; Gorran is the tiefling. Eirwen in the trial week has "kept it twelve years" (036:777), a ledger of a man four centuries dead, and "never knew who to show it to" after deciding with you who to show it to (15.35). *Fix:* see the dialogue table.

**E18 · Medium · New Journey breaks.** Several lines assume the player is a Herald: Halden sends you "back to your origin. Targos, four hundred years ago" (048_C:580); Eirwen's flashback says "Faces you remember" and "You remember it from the inside" (048_D:606-612); Eira Voss says "Buy something, Herald" (048_B:275). The New Journey player is a Rootspire child. *Fix:* branch on `herald_mode?` or reword (the dialogue table).

**E19 · Low · Duplicate rewards and dead items.** Three Never-Melt Ices in this part alone (Elitha, the yeti mother, the Frostfell angel), plus the Winter boon; the Shard of Stillness (Mira's $500 mirror that "pulses near anything interesting") does nothing; Viessa's map ("Take it. The map.") is never given; Tirena's compass "points at the Celestial Rift every time" and doesn't. *Fix:* make the Shard pulse (a one-line message on maps with an un-found watcher) and Viessa's map a key item that marks the six places; vary the ice rewards.

## 2. First-time player

**F1 · "Why does everyone call me 'Herald'? I'm the kid from the College."** Eira Voss, Lord Aster, the petitions and most of the Promenade say "Herald" to the New Journey player. *Fix:* "Herald's friend", or their name, where the speaker is talking to you and not the party.

**F2 · "What's that faint Gengar doing in the tavern? Is my game broken?"** (E4). A translucent sprite that says "Something was there" looks like a bug. *Fix:* E4's first-touch explanation.

**F3 · "Where do I go now? I've finished the spine bit and there's a whole city."** No list of what the city offers (E2, E3). *Fix:* the side-quest list; signs; a Hellen line that names three things to do.

**F4 · "What's an eladrin? An aasimar? A goliath? A drow? A dragonborn? A tiefling?"** First-meeting lines describe nine people by D&D race (Elitha, Lumea, Ysera, Kethan, Dathra, Shivven, Gorran, Felka, Mira). A Pokémon player has no picture for these words, and the sprites are human. *Fix:* describe what the player sees ("A woman with frost-white hair that drifts like falling snow…") and keep the race word, if at all, for a second line.

**F5 · "What does Filgen mean, Fireball and Misty Step? He sells TMs."** (15.25). *Fix:* "Fire, haste, frost, the lot. Grown in sap, picked fresh." and keep the TM shop.

**F6 · "Who is 'she'? 'The hag's girl'? 'The Mirror Mother'? 'The lady at the forge'?"** Avarice is referred to by six names in this part (she, her, the lady, the hag's girl, A., Mirror Mother) before the player is told who she is. *Fix:* one Herald gloss the first time each name appears ("MEAT MEAT: Mirror Mother. The lady. Avarice. Same lady.").

**F7 · "What's the Frostfell? What's Ithryn Athis? What's the White Womb? Are those the same place?"** (15.29, 15.34). Three names for two places. *Fix:* Wynsnow: "The White Vault, under the glacier. Selrae calls it the White Womb. Same door."; Alerion at the portal: "Auril's own country. The cold behind the cold."

**F8 · "Why did the frozen man have Alerion's face? Who's Talion?"** The run met the hunter before any Talion scene in this chapter. *Fix:* Alerion answers Alberica's joke: "ALERION: My brother. He went out on the ice. I didn't bring him back." (one box).

**F9 · "I beat the Rime Stalker. Why didn't I get the cloak?"** "Not killed. Catch it." is said once, before the battle, and the knockout replays everything (E9). *Fix:* the failure line says it: "(It has to be caught, not beaten. Come back and try again.)" (026:1434).

**F10 · "What's the Wheel? Why does the game keep saying +1 to something I can't see?"** Most endings here give "+1 Wheel" silently. *Fix:* a one-time message the first time the Wheel moves, and the omen/Hellen lines to mention it.

**F11 · "What do the factions do? Did I just make Winter angry?"** The ledger choice, the petitions and the emissary move four hidden numbers; the player is never told (15.14). *Fix:* a short "Winter will remember that." line after each change, and the favour board shown once by Hellen.

**F12 · "Why did Darrus just ask me to rob a vault in front of the High Speaker?"** (15.30). *Fix:* stage it after Kelyn walks off (E10), or play it at the Hearth's back door.

**F13 · "Is this shop open? It says closed, and the window glowed."** The Suncleft opens only after a vignette or a watcher catch, and its door pushes you back a step even after "the lock gives" (15.25). *Fix:* open the door on the same press.

**F14 · "What is a Shard of Stillness for?"** Mira sells one, Eira slips them into purchases, Jast says he buys them and doesn't. *Fix:* E19.
