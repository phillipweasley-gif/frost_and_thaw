# Part 05 · Chapter 5: The Cackling Chasm — review notes

*Read with `docs/review/script/05.md`. Scene numbers (5.01-5.27) are the script's. "Log" means the New Journey playthrough (`parts/05.txt`); "code" means it's read from `023_Chapter5.rb` / `044_ChasmFaithful.rb` / `build_ch5.rb`.*

**Summary.** The chapter is very complete as content: everything from the campaign module is in it (the icefall, the feast hall, the illusions, the bridge, the gate rite, the ghost, the hag in full, a dark ending, loot). It plays like one long read. Across six maps you get 457 boxes and 23 prompts (17 menu picks, 6 yes/no), and the Goal never changes. The five companions say 72 lines without being on the map, and the boss gives her whole speech one row above the top of the screen. Almost nothing on the map moves in the chapter's big moments. Three continuity errors are forced by the code (the swaddling, the lullaby, the troll's fire line), and the chapter's most important character scene (the sanctum) can be skipped entirely.

**Findings:** 5 High · 17 Medium · 11 Low (editor); 16 first-time-player questions; 47 dialogue rows; plus 136 boxes over the 90-character limit (appendix).

## 1. Editor

### High

**E1 · The party is never on the map (staging, whole chapter).** None of the five companions has a sprite on any Chapter 5 map. The log flags 72 Herald boxes `absent`: Alberica 18, Reptin 15, Alerion 15, Shade 13, Meat Meat 11. The moments that are written as physical action all happen to nobody: Alberica's "a hand flat between his shoulder blades" and Alerion "stepping between him and the worst of it" (5.06), "ALERION *(grabbing their arm)*" (5.10), "ALBERICA *(an arm around Shade's shoulders)*" (5.11), the honey cake handed over while "Everyone's watching" (5.15), Reptin frozen to the knees and freed (5.20), Meat Meat "arriving at a dead run" and Reptin "between her and the cub" (5.22). Reptin appears only three times as a scene stand-in, and Alberica and Meat Meat only in the last two minutes. Chapter 2 already does this right: Bryn Shander's square puts every Herald on the map with `Stage.actor` and walks them. *Why it matters:* §9a says "A line from someone the player can't see is a bug"; to a player it reads as voices in an empty cave, which in a chapter about illusions and voices in the dark also blurs which voices are real. *Fix:* at each scene start, add the party as stand-ins round the player (`Stage.herald` for each companion, offsets ±1). Do it at least for 5.01, 5.05, 5.06-5.08, 5.10, 5.13-5.15, 5.18 and 5.20-5.27, and add `Stage.cleanup` at the end. Walk the ones whose lines say they move (Alerion to Shade at the fork, Meat Meat to the cub). Better still, add a party follower for the chapter: the design for SC 5.01 already asks for "Reptin's overworld follower swaps to a wolf for the rest of the chapter".

**E2 · The hag is off camera for her own confrontation (5.20-5.23).** The confrontation row is at y 13 (`build_ch5.rb:261`); Thredra stands at (11,6), one row above the top of the screen. All 18 of her boxes from "Ssooo, my dearies" to "Let's dance upon the glass" are flagged `off_camera`, so they get no speech bubble. Before them come seven narration boxes describing her ("A tall, gaunt crone... A long hooked nose... A cloak of patchwork skins...") while the camera shows you, Kanan, the troll and a mirror. Her pose (P-THR-02, "leaps backward into the largest mirror") also plays off screen. At the cave mouth (5.02) she has no sprite at all: "a hunched silhouette... She holds a lantern. She beckons", then "She flickers and is gone". *Why it matters:* this is the act's villain reveal, and the player never sees her face. *Fix:* `Stage.pan_to(11,8)` as the parley starts and `pan_back` before the first battle. Or move the confrontation row to y 9-10 and step the player up to it with `Stage.walk`. Cut the description to two boxes and let the sprite carry it: "THREDRA steps out from behind the Heart: tall, bruise-coloured, white hair in a wind you can't feel." and "A black staff hung with frozen things. At her heel, Kanan. At her other side, an ice troll." Give her `Stage.emote(:exclaim)` on "Ssooo". At the cave mouth, put a translucent crone actor (FT_thredra at opacity 120) eight tiles up the tunnel with a lantern, and hide her with an effect on "She flickers and is gone".

**E3 · The sanctum, the ghost and Reptin's choice can be skipped (structure, 5.16-5.19).** The door to the Heart (`build_ch5.rb:201`, at 31,1) sits straight through the Guardian's Gate. The hidden sanctum is off to one side behind the "Hollow wall" at (34,8), and nothing gates the Heart on it. A player who opens the gate and walks north misses all of the following: the ghost with Alberica's face (Chapter 5 beat 3 in the bible), Reptin's "I'll keep it. The wolf. The watch." (his arc beat), the locket's blessing (it is what defeats her evasion and her illusions in battle), the "only flame" and "destroy her staff" tips, the mid-boss heal, and "Rest, warden". The cost then lands only as harder fights, and the ending's "Rest, warden" and the "new Order" glimpse silently drop out. *Fix:* add a page to the Heart door that needs `SW_GHOST_REST`, with a person as the gate. For example, before the ghost: "REPTIN: (stopping dead) Wait. That seam by the gate. Something in there knows my name." / "REPTIN: We look first." and the player steps back. Or have the gate itself open into the sanctum, with the Heart passage behind the bier.

**E4 · The yeti looks at a swaddling nobody has found (continuity, 5.07).** In the sing branch: "The mother stops, shaking. She looks at the swaddling." You only find the swaddling at the dead yeti, and the code only allows that after the mother has been faced (`dead_yeti`: `if flag?(:yeti_faced) && !flag?(:found_swaddling)`, `023_Chapter5.rb:305`). So this line always plays before the object exists for the player. *Why it matters:* it's the beat that tells the player the cub exists, and the clue arrives backwards. *Fix:* either move the swaddling into the yeti scene ("Her dead mate's hand is clenched round something small. She keeps looking at it." / "She looks at his hand. Then she points, deeper in, to where her cub was taken.") and then let the dead yeti reveal what it is, or give the dead yeti its swaddling before the trigger row (move the row to y 21, north of the body).

**E5 · Glimmer's journal contradicts the canon (continuity, 5.08).** The backpack lies "Beside a half-chewed skeleton, too long in the arm to be human, with claws on its fingers", and the journal is "signed: Glimmer", "desperate to save Alberica". The bible makes Glimmer what Shade becomes in Chapter 6 (a tabaxi, so the claws), and his death is at the Forge ("His death at the Forge is final"). A Glimmer skeleton in the Chasm in Act I either contradicts that or is a time-loop clue that nothing ever pays off. The ghost's "You'll see. Not yet." and the epilogue's bard song "Glimmer in the Chasm" make it look deliberate. The design says the opposite: "the journal's owner is never named. Don't reuse 'Glimmer' here" (act1_script SC 5.04 notes). *Why it matters:* returning players who learn Shade is Glimmer will read this as an error. *Fix (owner's decision):* either follow the design (unnamed owner; "Granny Thredra says she can bring my sister back." / last page: "SHE LIES."), or keep it and commit to the loop: log it in the bible and give Glimmer a line in Chapter 6 or 8 that recognises the journal. Severity is High because, either way, the current state breaks the story for anyone who follows it.
