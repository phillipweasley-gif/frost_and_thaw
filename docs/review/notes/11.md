# Part 11 · Chapter 11: The Frost Saint Walks — review notes

The chapter's words are mostly strong (Auril's whisper, Sephek's "I'm sorry. I'm taking her home", Alerion on the
steps). What makes it feel disjointed is the staging and the flow. Nearly every speaker is off the map. The Shrine scene
plays at the root-lift, 12 tiles from the Shrine. Two runs of 66 and 42 boxes have no input. And the chapter ends
pointing three ways at once, with the relics, the one optional task it set up, dropped from the goal.
Findings: 4 High, 10 Medium, 7 Low.

Script: `docs/review/script/11.md`. Line references are to `game/Data/Scripts/998_FrostThaw/`.
