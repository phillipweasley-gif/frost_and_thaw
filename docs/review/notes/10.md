# Part 10 notes · Chapter 10: Trials of the Roots

The trial week has the richest content in the game so far (43 scenes played, 12 more reachable), but it plays as a list of errands told by invisible people, on a clock that never runs, and it ends without paying off most of what the player gathered. The fixes are mostly staging and structure; the writing underneath is often good.

Scene numbers refer to `docs/review/script/10.md`. Line references are `file:line` in `game/Data/Scripts/998_FrostThaw/`.

## 1. Editor

### High

**E1 · The Heralds are never on screen (every scene).** In New Journey mode the companions have no sprites, so every Herald line in the chapter comes from nobody: the log flags 112 absent or off-camera speakers in 555 boxes (Wyn 22, Alberica 13, Reptin 10, Meat Meat 5, Alerion 4). The worst cases: the Underdark crawl, where "Two Rootwardens fall in either side of you" and Teague and Oda don't exist either (10.19); the Heartroot's Test, where Anduin "comes" and holds Reptin's hand but neither is drawn (10.12); Summer's quarters, where four people sneak past a construct and only you are visible (10.28). This breaks WRITING_GUIDE §9a. **Fix:** at the start of every scene that gives a Herald a line, call `Stage.herald(key, dx, dy)` for each speaking companion (it already makes a stand-in beside you) and `Stage.cleanup` at the end. Do the same for the named NPCs who "come with you" (Anduin, Teague, Oda, Kelyn and Selrae on the escort). Longer term, a follower train for companions.

**E2 · The week has no clock, so the "seven days" mean nothing (10.07, 10.33, 10.41, 10.42).** A day passes only when you talk to Hellen. The run rested once, did the whole week on "Day 2", and Kelyn let it call the verdict there ("Day 2 of seven"), after which the narration says "The seventh day." The lake ghost's "Nights pass at the shore" moves no counter. Nothing tells the player that days matter (the wall's `neglected` count punishes days you rest without working, which the player can't see). **Fix:** advance the day after each personal trial and each "you spend the day" job, show "(Day 3 of 7. Four trials left.)" on waking, and at Kelyn before day 7 ask "It's only day {n}. Call the crystals early?" with the verdict opening on "The crystals sit early. The hall fills anyway." when it's before day 7.

**E3 · Meat Meat is in a cell and with you at the same time (10.09, 10.07, 10.15, 10.20, 10.27, 10.29).** She "sits in a root-cell" (10.15), but she fought the wolves the day before, dreamed beside you, and after the cell scene, where the player chose "Stay, and answer for it", she walks into the Underdark, picks the tower lock and leads the Smuggler's Run. Nobody mentions her being let out. **Fix:** either take her out of `companions` from the Tribunal until the cells scene, then release her into your custody at the end of it ("KELYN: (at the bars) She goes with you. On your word. Bring her back on the seventh day."), or make the cells a nightly rule from the start (Kelyn in 10.01: "Meat Meat sleeps in the root-cells. By day, she's yours to watch.").

**E4 · The goal line is unreadable, partly wrong, and then goes stale (10.01, 10.15, 10.17, 10.22, 10.24, 10.25, 10.38).** The first goal is 254 characters: "Seven days. Anduin by the Bazaar fountain; Meat Meat in the root-cells; Wyn's summons at the Echo Gate; Talion in the Blooming Fields; the councillors in the Undercanopy; Selrae's bid in this Hall. And the city: the wall, the camp, the debates." "The councillors in the Undercanopy" is wrong (the hearing comes first, in the Hall; the Undercanopy NPC sends you back), and "this Hall" is only true while you stand in it. The first trial replaces it, and the overview never comes back. Later goals outlive their quests: "Wyn's hearing, in the Council Hall." after Wyn is cleared; "Kelyn and Selrae wait at the Sap Gate..." after the Fane; "The chronal leaf..." after it's restored. "The Sap Gate" is never shown or explained on the way. **Fix:** a short hub goal, restored after every trial: "Trial week, day {n}: {k} trials left. The Heralds menu lists them." Put the six trials in a checklist page of the Heralds menu (who, where, done). Clear each trial's goal when it ends.

**E5 · The escort to the Fane happens at the Undercanopy's front door (10.25).** You press A on one sprite one tile from the entrance; the descent, two battles, a cave-in, the arrival at the Fane, Luminara's speech and the memory bubble all play there. Luminara's own sprite is about 35 tiles away and off camera for all six of her boxes; Kelyn sits "on the floor of the Fane" while standing at the entrance. This is the climax of Alberica's trial. **Fix:** `Stage.escort` Kelyn and Selrae (two actors, not one "Kelyn and Selrae" sprite) through the beds to the Fane, with the skirmishes on the way and a screen shake for the cave-in; or fade to black at "The councillors follow you down" and cut to the Fane with Luminara on screen.

**E6 · Every skill check puts the right answer first (whole chapter).** Of the 46 challenges in this part, 45 have the correct option at the top (the one exception, Eirwen's, accepts the first two). The test picked the first option every time and never failed a check. A player learns this in ten minutes and the week's choices stop meaning anything. **Fix:** shuffle the options in `challenge()` (`036_Chapter10.rb:257`) and map the right indices through the shuffle; keep "Not today"/"Leave it" options last.

### Medium

**E7 · The verdict's witness box (10.42).** One box of 539 characters (about six boxes) lists 26 names, including "Phildendrin, Phildendrin, again", "Talion, through the lake" (he testified through the Bazaar's mirrors, in the Fields), objects ("A Leaf of Hyphora", "The ritual runes"), and both presiding judges ("Selrae Vorn", "Kelyn of Spring"). Nobody actually testifies. **Fix:** a montage of the three strongest witnesses per charge, each one box in their own voice, then "(Twenty-six witnesses in all.)". Drop the judges from WITNESSES (`036_Chapter10.rb:147-148`), merge the two Phildendrin rows, rename Talion's to "Talion, through the mirrors".

**E8 · The verdict skips three of the five charges, and the chosen speaker never speaks (10.42).** The Tribunal charged Wyn, Alberica and asked about Reptin; the verdict rules only on Summer and Meat Meat. "Who speaks for the Heralds?" changes a hidden score and the chosen Herald says nothing. **Fix:** one Kelyn line per charge settled during the week ("KELYN: The Underdark door: the Rootwarden Wyn is cleared.", "KELYN: The spores: the Council has a Fungal Liaison now. That will do.", "KELYN: Reptin: the Heartroot says he stands beside her, not over her."), and one line from the chosen speaker before the reading.

**E9 · The vote ignores what you did and explains nothing (10.43).** The player endorsed Rheven and Mira; Rheven and Eirwen won, with no line saying why (Eirwen gathered support points from Anduin, the Shrine and the colony). The design (act2_script SC 10.09) says four or more witnesses win both picks; the run had 26. Phildendrin, who "becomes a candidate" (`candidate_phildendrin` is set at `036_Chapter10.rb:925` and never read) is silently missing because her petition needed ten names. The scene says "Out in the Blooming Fields" but never leaves the Hall. **Fix:** cut to the Fields stage with the candidates as actors; after the count, a line for a lost pick ("Mira comes third. MIRA: (shrugging) Next year, Heralds."); a line at the vote if a petition fell short ("PHILDENDRIN: (from the crowd) Six names short. Next year."). Decide whether witnesses or support points rule, and make the design and the code agree.

**E10 · The Undercity's intro never plays (10.29, 10.30).** `scene_smugglers_run_enter` (`048_Rootspire_D_Quests.rb:820`, the Guild-heist intro) and the Undercity's own intro (`047b_TrialWeek.rb:348`) both use `run_entered`, so arriving in the Undercity shows nothing and Wyn's "...Spores. Old ones. Hungry ones. This way." is lost. **Fix:** give the Undercity intro its own flag (`undercity_entered`).

**E11 · `say()` puts unmatched speakers' bubbles on whoever you're talking to (10.15, 10.17, 10.39).** When a tag isn't found and an event id was passed, `say()` (`007_Story.rb:27-30`) bubbles the talked-to event. So Wyn's "...Sorrel." comes out of Sorrel, Meat Meat's cell lines come out of the hooded woman, and the druid's "Balance. Always balance." comes out of Selrae. **Fix:** only fall back to the event when its name matches the tag or the event has no name of its own; otherwise note it as absent.

**E12 · The root-cells show the wrong person, in the wrong place (10.15).** The cells event's sprite is `FT_phildendrin_hooded` from the first day of the week, so the hooded woman is already at the bars before she "pushes through the crowd in the gallery" "during the first hearing" (no hearing, no gallery, no crowd here). "She pulls her hood back" and the sprite keeps it. 34 boxes play with two inputs and nothing moving. **Fix:** the cell event shows Meat Meat behind bars; Phildendrin enters as an actor from the stair, walks to the bars ("!" bubble on Meat Meat), and swaps to an unhooded sprite on "She pulls her hood back". Cut "During the first hearing" (see the dialogue table).

**E13 · Gym 8: you "wake in the Council Hall" eight boxes before you do (10.40).** `seasons_outcome` runs on the dream map, then `transfer` (`036_Chapter10.rb:1707-1714`). The dream's Selrae sprite is named "Winter", so all her lines are logged absent. Each season is one narration box over the same snowfield, with no weather, tint or effect. **Fix:** transfer first, then the outcome; rename the event "Selrae (Winter)"; give each season a tone and weather (green tint and grass for Spring, heat shimmer for Summer, leaves for Autumn, snow for Winter).

**E14 · The omens run backwards and cut into conversations (10.01, 10.03, 10.04, 10.37).** The Reptin omen's strongest tier ("she sees him dead in the snow... Stop them, and all is mine") plays on day one, and its milder tier ("Life and death march only so long as they move") plays days later; those are the two halves of Auril's line in Chapter 11, heard in reverse. One omen fires between pressing A on the foreman and his reply. **Fix:** in `omen_check` (`048_Rootspire_E_Nights.rb:122`), play tier t only after tier t−1; never fire an omen inside a talk event (queue it for the next step).

**E15 · Two Spring baskets in two days, and two different rivals (10.02, 10.07).** Spring's boon arrives twice (Revival Herbs on the walk, Sitrus Berries at the Hearth door) from two systems. The jeer comes from an Autumn courtier, then the next morning says "Winter's Order of Balance has noticed." `FACTION_RIVAL` is defined twice with different values (`047d_FixRound.rb:338`, spring → autumn; `048_Rootspire_H_Faithful.rb:510`, spring → winter); the second wins. **Fix:** one rival table and one boon per faction per week.

**E16 · The night at the Hearth is out of order (10.07).** Hellen greets you; then dawn; then a basket at the door; then "(Day 2 of the trial week.)"; then "That night you all dream the same dream." **Fix:** dream first (with a fade to black and a dream tint), then "You wake.", then dawn, the basket and the day count.

**E17 · Talion's choice gives the answer away and his testimony goes nowhere (10.23).** "Decide what to do" is offered before any investigation and its first option is "Accept he's a reflection, and let him fade", so the player is told what he is. "On his way out, through every mirror in the Bazaar, Talion gives the court his testimony" while you're in the Fields and no court is sitting. The stand-in Alerion appears only to kneel and vanishes on cleanup. **Fix:** offer the decision only after at least one look (pulse, listening or the hold); word the first option without the answer ("Let him go"); move the testimony to the verdict montage ("TALION: (in every mirror of the hall) The tall gold man had no reflection."); keep Alerion on screen for the whole scene and walk him off.

**E18 · One sprite plays many people (10.04, 10.14, 10.17, 10.21, 10.24, 10.35).** The wall foreman and Rheven are both BW (72), four tiles apart. Sorrel, Kethan, Ivo and the frozen Brisa are all BW029 (Kethan and Ivo two tiles apart). Eirwen, the "Spring and Winter" stand-in, the "Kelyn and Selrae" stand-in, both debate stages and a gallery member are all BW (30). One "Spring and Winter" sprite stands for two councillors while the real Kelyn and Selrae stand across the room. **Fix:** a distinct graphic per named character; delete the stand-ins and have Kelyn and Selrae themselves walk to the spot.

**E19 · Rescued and dropped (10.35).** Warden Brisa, a missing patrol the city was searching for, is thawed and never seen again: no walk home, no reunion, no line in Rootspire, no witness entry. **Fix:** she walks back with you and appears at the Rootwardens' hall or the Root-Tier ("WARDEN BRISA: Ten weeks, they tell me. It felt like a minute. Thank you."), and joins the witness list for the Underdark charge.

**E20 · Alberica's own quest ends without her (10.31, 10.38).** Eirwen says a sliver of reality, "Her sliver", will unravel without the leaf, which is never explained; when it's restored there's no handover box and Alberica, whose life is on it, says nothing. **Fix:** say what's at stake in plain words, and give her the last line (see the dialogue table).

**E21 · Evidence arrives after the hearing it was for (10.25, 10.31).** The run found the Leaf of Old Alliances after the spores hearing, so it was never used, and nothing before the hearing hints that the Library, Luminara or Yarro have evidence. WRITING_GUIDE §7 asks for order independence in a hub. **Fix:** Kelyn's line in 10.24 names what would convince Selrae ("Bring me something older than my fear. The Library keeps the long histories."); let any evidence gathered later count at the verdict for the spores charge.

**E22 · The Heartroot's Test has no answers to the player's answers (10.12).** Anduin's third choice ("Tell him about the Forge") gets no reaction box; both answers to Hyphora get no reply; the vision chamber doesn't exist on the map. **Fix:** one reaction box for each (see the dialogue table), and end the passage at a chamber the map actually has, or fade to the vision.

Hyphora's "Shadow answered my call." plays with no light or sound from the crystal. **Fix:** an Eira line after the choice, a flash of green on the crystal, and a short Wyn reply (see the dialogue table).

**E24 · Long runs without input (10.15, 10.25).** The root-cells run 34 boxes with one choice and one yes/no; the escort and Fane run 38 boxes with three battles but no movement. WRITING_GUIDE §2.3 caps it at about 25. **Fix:** split the cells scene at the apology (Phildendrin's mission becomes her repeat line on the next visit); stage the escort as a walk (E5).

Talion stands in the Fields although the bible places him at Glasswater Lake; Gym 8 moved from the Still Sky Shrine to the Hall. **Fix:** the Kelyn repeat line (dialogue table); log the moves in the Story Bible as §10 asks.

## 2. First-time player

1. **"Am I on trial? I'm not one of the Heralds."** In New Journey you're the newcomer, but the charges, the defence and the vote never say what you are to the court. **Fix:** one Kelyn line in 10.01: "KELYN: And you. You travel with them. You'll speak for them, or against them. Choose carefully."
2. **"What do witnesses do, and how many do I need?"** The menu counts them but nothing says what they win. **Fix:** Kelyn in 10.01: "KELYN: Every witness you bring speaks at the verdict. Bring enough, and the crystals will see what Summer was."
3. **"Who is Avarice? Who's Var? Who's the Marshal? Who's 'the tall gold man'?"** The same person goes by four names in this chapter. **Fix:** use "the Marshal" for the body and "Avarice" for the woman inside, and gloss once: "(The tall gold man: the Summer Marshal, the body Avarice wore.)"
4. **"Where do I go first, and what's left?"** The goal is one long sentence, then disappears. **Fix:** E4's hub goal and checklist.
5. **"Why is Meat Meat in jail and also fighting wolves with me?"** **Fix:** E3.
6. **"What's the Sap Gate?"** Named in the goal and by Kelyn, never pointed to. **Fix:** goal "Kelyn and Selrae wait at the Undercanopy's entrance, below the Root-Tier."
7. **"What's a Rift? What are ice-angels? What's the Frostfell?"** All arrive in Selrae's first box of the gym. **Fix:** "SELRAE: There's a tear in the sky over the Canopy. Things come through it. Ice-angels." (and the option reads "Counter it: you closed it" only if you did).
8. **"Who's Erato?" / "What's a god-spark?"** Both appear once in passing. **Fix:** drop Erato from Anduin's line (dialogue table); in the hearing, "SELRAE: A god-spark (a sliver of a god, carried in a hand), and a sapling that sings. Convenient." or cut the word.
9. **"Who's the hooded pilgrim, and who's Sephek?"** The dream ends on a name nobody explains. **Fix:** keep the mystery but anchor it: "You wake. Alerion says "Sephek" out loud in the dark, and won't say who that is."
10. **"What did choosing a speaker do?"** Nothing visible. **Fix:** E8.
11. **"Why did my second pick lose? Where's Phildendrin?"** **Fix:** E9.
12. **"What's the Draconic Vault? Is that the White Vault? Fragment of what?"** **Fix:** one name (dialogue table) and "(The third piece of the map.)"
13. **"What's a chronal leaf, and what happens if it's lost?"** **Fix:** E20.
14. **"Why does Phildendrin's mirror quest exist?"** The Council's offer arrives secondhand. **Fix:** "PHILDENDRIN: Kelyn told me: one good act, done in front of witnesses, and the court weighs it."
15. **"Who's Kethan? Who's Ivo? Why do they look the same as Sorrel?"** **Fix:** E18, and a name-and-role line on first meeting: "Captain Kethan Vale, the Rootwardens' captain of the Underdark watch, reads the charge."
16. **"What's 'Wardens Emeritus'?"** The basket's label is the only use. **Fix:** "From the High Speaker. For the Heralds."
17. **"What's 'V-7' and 'Prototype Verdant'?"** Two lab names for Meat Meat with no gloss here. **Fix:** "HOODED WOMAN: They told me V-7 died. My baby. That's the number they gave her." and keep "Prototype Verdant" (the pilgrim's menace works if V-7 is understood).
18. **"If I call the verdict now, what do I lose?"** "(The trial week ends.)" doesn't say the trials still open are gone. **Fix:** "Call for the verdict? Trials you haven't finished won't be heard."
