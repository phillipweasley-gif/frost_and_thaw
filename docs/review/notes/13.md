# Part 13 notes · Grimskalle, the endings and the epilogue

The writing in this part is some of the best in the game: the four tests each turn on one refusal, Sephek's turn lands, and the Third Way pays off Shimmer's song. The problem is everything around the words. **No Herald, Eline, Tamsin or Old Alberica stands on any map in this part. Auril speaks her whole throne scene from off screen. The tests show an empty room under a frost frame. The ending, epilogue and coda run 55 boxes with no input, in silence, on the throne-room map.** A player sees a trainer standing alone in ice rooms while a crowd talks. Losing a test, which the test run did, gets no reaction and no second try.

Script: `docs/review/script/13.md`. Line refs: `038` = 038_Chapter12.rb, `039` = 039_Chapter13.rb, `047c` = 047c_WhiteVault.rb, `build` = tools/build_act3.rb.

## 1. Editor

### Staging and animation

**E1 · High · The endings, epilogue and coda: 55 boxes with no input, nothing on screen, no music (13.19-13.23).**

From "Alberica picks the diamond up" to "THE END" the player presses A 55 times in the empty throne room. The music fades out at the start of the epilogue (`pbBGMFade(2.0)`, 039:620) and nothing replaces it. The "year later" vignettes in Rootspire, the Shrine, the Chasm and Lonelywood all play over Auril's throne room. The coda ("Many years later. The Cackling Chasm.") puts Old Alberica on your tile in that same throne room (039:680-681). The design (act3_script 14.01, 14.02) places the epilogue in Rootspire [2050] with *Heralds* then *Hearth*, and the coda in the Cackling Chasm [2042] with *Thaw*.

*Fix:* fade to black after "Frostina and Razzilith turn the wheel" and `transfer` to Rootspire before "A year later." Give each vignette a short `Stage.visit` (or one S- panel) of its place and stage the people in it: Tamsin at the Hearth mural, Razzilith landing in the market. Play *Heralds*. Then cut to 2042 for the coda, with the ghost actor in the hag's hall and the camera on the cave mouth for "She looks up at the cave mouth." That also breaks the run of boxes into scenes the player can feel move.

**E2 · High · No Herald stands on any Grimskalle map. Every Herald line in the part is spoken by nobody (13.01-13.22).**

`build_act3.rb` places no Herald on 2080-2089, and no scene calls `Stage.herald` (the one exception is the unplayed wolf branch of Endurance, 039:179). The log flags MEAT MEAT, ALERION, ALBERICA, REPTIN and WYN absent on every map. It only flags the first line per map, so the real count is every Herald line: about 70. On the Shimmer statue the fallback in `say()` puts Alerion's and Alberica's bubbles on Shimmer's statue (13.02), so the statue appears to say "...Shimmer." This breaks WRITING_GUIDE §9a rule 1 through the whole finale.

*Fix:* at the start of each scene, `Stage.herald(:key, dx, dy)` for every Herald with a line, placed round you, and `Stage.cleanup` at the end. In each test, stage that test's Herald one tile beside you. In 13.02, walk Alerion's stand-in up to the statue for "(touching the cold cheek)".

**E3 · High · Auril is off screen for her whole throne scene, and the four-form final fight never changes the map (13.16-13.17).**

Auril's sprite is at (9,4) and you arrive at (9,12) (build: throne). All of her lines are logged `off_camera`. There is no pan. "(standing)", "a green dragon lands on the ice", "The crone's furs fall away", "The maiden shatters", "Alberica is lifted off the floor" and "a woman made of all three" are words only. The furs sprite sits on the throne until the whole fight is over.

*Fix:* `Stage.pan_to(9,5)` before "The woman in frost-furs is sitting on it.", then walk her down two tiles on "(standing)". Bring Razzilith and Frostina in as actors on "Through the roof..." (a shake, then the two land beside you). Between forms, a flash plus `Stage.fx` on her tile, and swap her graphic per form. On "Alberica is lifted", pose Alberica's stand-in (P-ALB-04 already exists for the rune). Put the diamond down as a small actor or effect for the last scene and the choice.

**E4 · High · Eline dies at the Crown without ever being seen or mentioned there (13.15).**

Eline isn't on the Crown map (build: crown), and nothing before her death says she is present. The player's last sight of her was at the Shrine in Chapter 11. Then: "The cold comes off Auril's throne behind you in one last wave", "Eline steps into it", "she doesn't get up". No sprite, no flash, no balloon. Only Wyn reacts, then the goal changes to "The Throne of Frost." The throne is ahead through the door at (13,2), not "behind you".

*Fix:* place an Eline event beside Sephek when `eline_spared` or `eline_unbeaten`, and give her one silent beat on arrival: *Eline stands at his shoulder. She doesn't raise her hands.* Stage the wave as a white flash from the throne door, walk Eline one tile into it, and grey her sprite (opacity), with B-07 over Sephek. Give one Herald a beat after Wyn's (for example Alberica, plain: "ALBERICA: (quietly) ...Somebody should sit with her. After."). Correct the direction: "Cold rolls out under the throne-room door in one last wave, at all of you, at him."

**E5 · Medium · The four tests show an empty ice chamber under the frost frame (13.09-13.12).**

Each test map is the same bare 20×14 room with four pillars and one translucent "Keeper" sprite (build: tests). The words describe a hide-tent camp, a march into a blizzard, a snowed-in village with a warm house, a boy laughing in the snow and a frost-Alberica. O-03 is a frost *frame*, so the player sees the empty room through it. These are the four showpiece scenes of the act, written as stacks of narration (WRITING_GUIDE §9: showpieces get a panel, pose or effect).

*Fix:* one S- panel per test under its first box, showing the setup only (the camp and the elders; the march and the fallen child; the house and its open door; the boy and the frost-Alberica), then drop the panel for the Herald's refusal on the map with the Herald staged.

**E6 · Medium · The shades' events are named "Keeper", so their lines never get a bubble (13.09-13.12).**

`say()` finds speakers by event name. "GUNVALD'S SHADE", "JARUND'S SHADE", "THE TIGER QUEEN'S SHADE" and "STILL KEEPER" don't match "Keeper" (build: tests), so all are logged absent although a sprite stands six rows up. This is a one-line fix.

*Fix:* name the four events "Gunvald's Shade", "Jarund's Shade", "The Tiger Queen's Shade" and "Still Keeper" (the match needs every word of the speaker tag, "The" included).

**E7 · Medium · The coda's speaker has no bubble (13.23).**

The stand-in is `Stage.actor(:green_ghost, ...)` (039:681), so its event is "stage_green_ghost". "OLD ALBERICA" never matches it and is logged absent while standing on screen.

*Fix:* `Stage.actor(:old_alberica, px, py, 2, graphic: "FT_green_ghost")`.

**E8 · Medium · The Garden and the Gate describe action nobody performs (13.03-13.06).**

"An ice troll is gnawing on one of the blocks" (the troll is 20 rows away, standing still). "It splits top to bottom with a sound like a bell" (Nass's block doesn't change and no sound plays). "A giant walrus slides round and round an ice rink" (Ukuma steps in place), and the race is two lines of text.

*Fix:* pan to the troll for its line, and add a gnawing move route plus a crunch SE. Play `Ice8` or a bell SE and an E- shatter on Nass's block, and fade her opacity on "(fading)". Give Ukuma a looping move route round the rink; for the race, a `Stage.walk` lap for Ukuma and a stand-in of your fastest Pokémon.

### Structure and pacing

**E9 · High · A lost test is final, gets no reaction, and contradicts the scene (13.10).**

The test lost to Jarund. The engine's whiteout box says "You scurry back to a Pokémon Center" (there is none on Solstice). You wake in the Hall, and "The vision fades. The tablet ENDURANCE stays whole." follows. Reptin had just carried the children out, which the scene presents as the refusal that breaks the tenet. The game then says he failed, and nobody speaks. `finish_test` marks a lost test as faced (039:129), so there is no retry. The same applies to all four tests.

*Fix:* fight the shades with `canLose` (`pbTrainerBattle(type, name, nil, false, 0, true)`, as Eline's fight does, 047c:174), so there's no whiteout. Then write the loss as a beat:

*The vision fades. The tablet ENDURANCE stays whole.* / "REPTIN: (setting the last child down) They're out. That's the part that counted." / *(The Brittle Maiden will fight at full strength.)*.

Better still, let a lost test be retried: set `_faced` only on a win, and make the door say *The tablet is still whole. Try again?*.

**E10 · High · The loop throws the story away with no warning, and replays Act III in full (13.21).**

"Let Alberica hold her." wipes every story flag and companion (039:592-614) and sends you to the prologue, with no confirm. The design wants the choice to be a trap, but its loop costs "a couple of hours" because "at every chapter gate a 'You remember this' prompt lets you skip to the next boss" (act3_script 13.10). The code has two skips: Act I and Act II (040_Loop.rb). Chapters 11-13 (the Shrine, the voyage, Solstice, the tests and the four-form boss) replay in full every loop, and in Journey mode the prologue replays before the first skip.

*Fix:* add the act3 skip the design promises: offer "You remember this. Skip to the skull's jaw?" at `scene_four_chairs`, using an Act III snapshot. Before the loop starts, keep the trap but make the cost legible: one line after "It just stops. And then it starts again." such as *(The story starts over. You'll keep your team and your bag, and you can skip what you remember.)*. Ask the owner whether an autosave before SC 13.10 should be kept.

**E11 · Medium · The biggest choice in the game has no confirm, and B picks the Third Way (13.18).**

`pbMessage(..., -1)` returns -1 on cancel, and `case` sends anything but 0 and 2 to `ending_third_way` (039:517-523). A player who presses B to reread gets an ending. Shatter has no confirm either.

*Fix:* cancel 0 (no cancel), and confirm the chosen line with one ask: "Give winter back its place?" / "End her?" / "Let Alberica hold her?".

**E12 · Medium · The way into Grimskalle is one way, and the prompt doesn't say so (13.07).**

The Hall has no warp back to the Gate (build: hall). Past "Go into Grimskalle?" there's no camp, no shop and no heal but whiting out. WRITING_GUIDE §6: irreversible steps are hinted before.

*Fix:* "Go into Grimskalle? (No way back out until it's done.)", or a rope from the Hall back down to the Gate.

**E13 · Medium · The three shortcuts do nothing (13.05-13.07).**

Eirwen's servants' stair, Nass's safe way up and Ukuma's back door are listed at the jaw. Every one of them lands on the same tile of the Hall (`grimskalle_jaw` → `HALL_ARRIVAL`, 038:363). "None of them skips a Test" tells the player nothing about what they *do*. Three optional beats (a council vote, a Fire/Fighting check, a Speed check) pay off in one sentence. Skant's "I'll tell you the thought later. It's a good one." is never paid off either (`skant_freed` is read only for the list).

*Fix:* give each one a small, visible reward in the Hall: Eirwen's stair puts a healing spring in the Hall, Nass shows a cache of two Full Restores, Ukuma's back door skips the wild encounters. Rewrite the line: "(Your shortcuts: ... Each one makes the climb easier. None skips a Test.)". Pay Skant off at the Crown or in the epilogue.

**E14 · Medium · The throne's opening lines assume you won all four tests (13.16).**

"AURIL: You broke my tests." / "ALBERICA: We passed them, darling. Our way." play even though the test lost Endurance and its tablet "stays whole" (039:413-414). Read the flags.

*Fix:* all four broken, keep it. Some broken: "AURIL: You broke three of my tests." / "ALBERICA: Three's a lot, darling. Our way." None: "AURIL: You failed my tests. And still you climbed." / "ALBERICA: We're stubborn, darling. Our way."

**E15 · Medium · The final boss plays the wild-battle theme (13.16-13.17).**

The Cold Crone, the Brittle Maiden, Winter's Womb and the Rewritten Auril all use *Wild Pokémon Battle (Kanto)*, the same track as the troll and the Garden's wild Glalie. The tests use the Gym Leader theme. The design asks for *Frostmaiden, final arrangement* for the forms and a final-boss track, and for silence then a held note at the choice.

*Fix:* set a final-boss BGM (`setBattleRule("battleBGM", ...)` or `$PokemonGlobal.nextBattleBGM`) for the four forms. Cut the BGM to silence on "The Heralds stand round the tiny light on the floor."

**E16 · Medium · Hum Shimmer's notes silently becomes "approach slowly" (13.14).**

Without `hummed_song` (Chapter 9), picking "Hum Shimmer's four notes" prints "You go forward one step at a time." (039:275-283). The player's choice is ignored. Right before it, the Crown scene ends "Iskra lowers her antlers." and the choice box repeats the same line.

*Fix:* without the flag: *You hum four notes you only half know. Inside the crystal, Frostina doesn't stir.* then the approach lines. Change the choice prompt to "Iskra won't move from her nest."

**E17 · Medium · The world doesn't change after the end (13.24).**

Nothing reads `game_complete`, the ending flags, `auril_down` or `sephek_turned` outside 039. Rootspire is as it was. Hellen's mural still says the sixth figure "has no face" (030 `pro_mural`), though the epilogue just carved your name under it. The goal "The end. (Rootspire is yours to wander.)" points at nothing to do. The silver egg ("Something to come back for, one day") has no follow-up anywhere.

*Fix:* a post-ending mural line ("Under the small sixth figure, your name, freshly cut."), post-ending lines for Hellen, Tamsin and the market NPCs, Sephek's soup stall in Lonelywood, and the Heralds' party chat. Either build the egg payoff or cut "Something to come back for".

**E18 · Medium · Story key items survive the loop (13.21).**

`start_loop` keeps the bag. The Act I relics, Dawnfang, Frostshard Sigils and the scale core stay in it while the story replays. Scenes that hand items out with `give` (for example the sigil at the Shrine, 047c:199) add a second copy. Scenes that use `give_key` skip their "You obtained" jingle.

*Fix:* list the story key items and take them out in `start_loop` (keep relics only if the loop lines refer to them).

**E19 · Low · Shatter is a short ending without its last image (13.20).**

Shatter is four boxes, then the shared epilogue minus two vignettes. The design's last shot (a white owl on a branch at midwinter, watching) isn't built.

*Fix:* add the owl as a panel or an actor on a winter map after "Next solstice."

**E20 · Low · The twins' Twin Breath is invisible (13.16).**

"They'll breathe for you, once each, every time she changes." The code only takes 15% off each form's HP (039:402); nothing happens in battle.

*Fix:* at each form's first turn, a battle message "Razzilith breathes spring fire!" / "Frostina breathes winter!" (a battle-start event hook), or reword: "Before each of her forms, they breathe on her once. It will hurt."

**E21 · Low · Trainer names print doubled (13.11, 13.12).**

The intro reads "Shade of a King The Tiger Queen" and "The Still Keeper Still Keeper" (type name + trainer name, 039:39-50).

*Fix:* type "King's Shade" with names "Gunvald", "Jarund" and "the Tiger Queen"; type "Reflection" with name "Still Keeper".

**E22 · Low · Herald mode: epilogue lines about the Herald you are stay in third person (13.22).**

"In the Wind-Glass Library, Alberica finds..." (039:669), "Reptin and Anduin kneel together" (039:654), "Nothing feeds on the Heart while he's there." (039:651), "Every Herald and Tamsin" (039:673). These break WRITING_GUIDE §5 for Alberica and Reptin players.

*Fix:* branch them like their neighbours: "You find a crystal page in your own handwriting", "You and Anduin kneel together", "while you're there", "All of you, and Tamsin".

### Continuity

**E23 · Low · Small repeats and slips.**

- "The diamond cracks." is followed at once by "The diamond cracks, and it pulls." (039:440, 447).
- "Through the roof, a green dragon lands on the ice, and Frostina beside him." (039:420): Frostina was freed at the Crown and came in with you.
- Nass asks "You're not with Vellynne?" (038:314). Vellynne belongs to the unbuilt postgame and is never introduced.
- Wyn has no test and three lines in the chapter's spine (after Eline, during the Rewritten Auril, at the choice). That's fine, but Wyn says nothing in the Hall or at the throne's opening, both "major scenes" by the guide's rule.
