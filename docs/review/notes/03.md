# Part 03 notes · Chapter 3: Shimmer's Requiem

The chapter's best material, the confession, the third way, Meat Meat and the Requiem, plays on an empty screen in a scrambled order. The villain the goal promises is never met in Lonelywood. Shimmer's death happens because the player said yes to a bed. **28 editor findings: 5 High, 13 Medium, 10 Low.**

---

## 1. Editor

### High

**E1 · The spine skips Sephek in Lonelywood, so the night has no cause the player saw.** (3.02 → 3.06)
- 3.02 sets the goal "Lonelywood. Sephek is waiting." 3.04 arrives, but nothing points to Sephek. He is standing at Torg's sleds (37,20), 28 tiles from the arrival tile and off screen. The narration points the player to the green house instead ("Someone in there is baking.").
- Nimsy offers the attic at once, and saying yes is what kills Shimmer. Sephek's Torg's sprite disappears when she's taken, so on the natural route the player never meets him. The goal is never paid off.
- Shade then reads "He said before the next moon, or he picks someone else." Nobody heard him say it. The only place Sephek threatens her (optional, at Torg's) says "Tonight, or the next."
- **Why it matters:** the chapter's spine beat ("reach Sephek before the next moon. Instead, Shimmer goes alone") needs the player to have tried. As it plays, the loss comes from a Yes/No about resting.
- **Fix:**
    1. Make Sephek at Torg's a spine scene that fires as you walk into the clearing. Point to it in 3.04's last box, e.g. "Past the fisher's house, a man in shirtsleeves leans on a sled, watching the road." Set the goal to "Find Sephek at Torg's sleds, in the clearing past the fisher's house."
    2. Nimsy only offers the attic after `met_sephek_torgs`; before that she says "Supper first. Go and see the town."
    3. Change his parting line (044:1087) to set up the note: `SEPHEK: (to Shimmer, softly) Before the next moon. Come to me, or I pick someone else.`
    4. Hook Hlin from the spine: Danae or Scoop says "A dwarf at the Liar's been asking after the man in shirtsleeves."

**E2 · Nobody who speaks is on screen.**
- The Heralds have no sprites anywhere in this chapter: 58 boxes are logged `absent` or `off_camera`. Five more put their speech bubble on the wrong person: Alberica, Shimmer and Shade on Nimsy (3.05); Reptin and Shade on Luna (3.18).
- The worst stretches:
    - 3.02, Shimmer's confession: 11 boxes, nothing on screen but you.
    - 3.06–3.08, the bedroll and the aftermath.
    - 3.11, Reptin, Alerion and Alberica answer Meat Meat from nowhere. Jet Sentry speaks 3 boxes on a map she isn't on.
    - 3.14–3.15, Shade confronts the man who killed their sister while invisible.
    - 3.19, Zenith speaks in Lonelywood though he is in the Warren. Jet Sentry is 24 tiles away.
- This breaks WRITING_GUIDE §9a ("A line from someone the player can't see is a bug").
- **Fix:**
    1. Give each Herald who speaks a `Stage.herald` stand-in in every story scene, placed beside you before the first line.
    2. Put Jet Sentry on the Glade map, walking in from the south path before her lines.
    3. Move 3.19 to the Moon Temple grounds, outside the lodge door, with Zenith and Jet Sentry walking out.
    4. Stop the `say` fallback from bubbling an absent Herald's line on the NPC you talked to (007_Story.rb:28–31): only fall back when the tag matches that NPC's name.

**E3 · The chapter can be played out of order, and the world talks about Shimmer's death before it happens.**
- The north path (Moon Temple, Warren) and the logging road (Lonelywood Forest, Glade) are open as soon as you arrive. Visit them before resting and:
    - 3.13 plays ("SHADE: Talk later. He's here. I can feel it.") with no Sephek. It never plays again.
    - Cosmo Bookman says "I have hers. 'For them.'" Jet Sentry says "I tracked the cold man to the altar where she died." Nova Keeper says "The Circle is grieving." Stellar Fletcher won't forge "with that heart". All of this with Shimmer alive in the party.
    - The rose is already on the Glade altar (event at 15,11 has no condition).
    - Alberica's Glade chat says "My pendant broke when she... I saw all of it."
- **Fix:** hold the north path and the logging road with people-as-gates until `shimmer_taken`. For example, a logger on the logging road: "Not past the stones after dark. Speaker's orders. Come back in daylight." Or flag-gate every line above and the (15,11) rose event on `shimmer_taken`, and make 3.13 need `heart_of_ice`.

**E4 · Torrga's grudge closes three shops for a player who has never heard of Torrga.**
- After Sephek is beaten, `torrga_grudge` is set unconditionally (044:1209). The shops in Lonelywood, Termalaine and Good Mead then refuse you: "She says you killed her man."
- In the played route the test never met Torrga, Hlin or Sephek at Torg's. It passes Termalaine's shop on the way home.
- This is a penalty from optional content the player never saw, right after a boss, when they need supplies.
- **Fix:** only set the grudge if `met_torrga` or `met_sephek_torgs`. Have Torrga deliver the threat in person (3.15 could end with her at the temple foot).

**E5 · Skipping Luna silently breaks later chapters.**
- The Requiem sets the goal "Speak with Luna Carver before you go." But 3.19 is armed by `requiem_done`, so it fires as soon as you reach Lonelywood and overwrites the goal.
- A player who leaves without talking to Luna never gets `knows_three_hearts`, `luna_spark` or `wennen_follow`. As a result:
    - Sephek's statue stays at the Moon Temple.
    - Luna's camp never appears at Dougan's Hole (build_ch4.rb:330).
    - Reptin's callback in Ch11 (037:193) and Shade's in Ch6 (029:82) never play.
- **Fix:** fold Luna into the Requiem: Luna walks over at "It has a heart now..." and says her 3.08 lines there. Or arm 3.19 on `knows_three_hearts`, and keep Luna between the player and the exit.

### Medium

**E6 · The death plays in a lit kitchen to the house theme, 35 boxes without input.** (3.06–3.08)
- After "Rest in Nimsy's attic?" the player reads 35 boxes with no input (42 counting Nimsy's intro), over the §2 limit of ~25.
- The screen never darkens for "One by one, the party falls asleep" or the "..." gap. There is no attic: you stand downstairs beside Nimsy, who doesn't react.
- `mom_theme` (Nimsy's cosy house music) plays under the bedroll and the note. After the vision's flash it comes back under "Only a rose..." and "Sephek falls to his knees. He's weeping."
- **Fix:**
    1. Fade to black on "One by one...", hold for the "..." box, and fade up with a dawn tint.
    2. Move you to a bed tile or an attic corner.
    3. Cut the music at "Morning", then play a *Requiem* cue.
    4. Let the player walk to the bedroll and press A to read the note (an action, and a break in the run).
    5. After the vision, return to silence or *Requiem*, not `mom_theme`.

### Side-quest engagement

- **The Cold-Hearted Killer** (Hlin, Torg's): the strongest writing in Lonelywood. Hlin's ask is clear, the reward is real, and she reacts to what you did (origin known, body found, Torrga beaten). But nothing on the spine leads to her, and the Sephek half disappears after the night. As played it is missable, and its only visible trace is a shop refusal (E1, E4).
- **The White Moose** (Lonelywood end): a clear ask with a bargaining branch and a good reward (Ramshackle, an alternative rest point). It arrives at the wrong moment, the morning after a death (E14). The goal isn't updated, so the moose job is only in the player's memory. Fix: on accepting, show a one-line side goal in the Heralds menu, or a sign by the logging road.
- **Iriskree**: good texture (the dead she remembers, Shade's read on her). No follow-up, and fine as a shop.
- **Zenith's verse**: see E28.
