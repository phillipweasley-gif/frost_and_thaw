# 0.8.7 (2026-11-11): grafting comes up after the licence

Grafting used to unlock with Wynsnow's licence in Chapter 6 and never come up again. Owner's call ("Let's do 1-4"):

1. **Wynsnow's jar hatches.** He holds up a jar ("a Natu and a Sunkern sleep in one cocoon of sap"). After the
   rules and the Joining beat, it ticks, he tips it onto the boards, the cocoon rocks and splits, and the graft is
   shown and given to you (Natu + Sunkern, Lv 34; it evolves at its next level). He tells you how the Cocoon Chamber
   works: "Pick the one who stays first. Then the one who gives itself." (`051_Grafting first_graft_gift`, called
   from `026 wynsnow`.)
2. **Trainers who graft show it.** Each brings the graft out in a Poké Ball window before the battle and says
   what it is:
   - Quill at the College (Xatu + Noctowl)
   - Hew (Golem + Dugtrio)
   - Luminara (Vileplume + Parasect)
   - Mira (Gyarados + Kingdra; possessed Mira: "We know all about keeping.")
   - Selrae's dream (Piloswine + Jynx)
   - Tamsin's send-off (Furret + her Hoothoot)
3. **The mod's random NPC grafting is off in a Frost & Thaw save.** Checked headless: after the licence, every
   trainer's team was regrafted at random (Eren's Noctowl became B164H39, Luminara's lead B71H182 became B2H364 at six
   badges). That broke hand-built teams and the Pokémon their lines name. Only the trainers above graft now, the
   same way every time. Infinite Fusion saves keep the mod's behaviour.
4. **The party reacts when you graft.** On the first graft Meat Meat is with you for, she stares at where the
   cocoon was and asks "...Did it hurt? In there?". The first companion with you answers. Then she walks round it:
   "Both still in there. Meat Meat checked." Later grafts get one line from whoever's with you, in turn. The new
   Pokémon is on screen for the line. Sets `first_graft`, `mm_saw_graft` and counter `grafts`.
5. **Hyphora knows a graft (SC 7A.07).** With a grafted Pokémon in the party, her roots find it ("Two in one. My
   old way.") and she gives you a Destiny Knot. If either Pokémon holds it during a graft, five stats
   take the better parent's IV instead of three. It's
   optional and never a gate.

Also:
- The licence scene said "a jar on the desk", and the lecture said "the air over his desk", but there's no desk
  where Wynsnow stands. Now "He holds up a jar" and "The air in front of him".
