# Part 03 · Chapter 3: Shimmer's Requiem (as played)

**Route as played:** Termalaine [2120] → Lonelywood Road [2020] → Lonelywood [2021] → Speaker Huddle's House [2027] (the night, the vision) → Lonelywood → Lonelywood Forest [2129] → Glade of the Unmade [2026] → Lonelywood Forest → Lonelywood → Moon Temple [2023] (Sephek) → Wennen's Warren [2022] (the Requiem, Luna) → Moon Temple → Lonelywood (the hag named) → Lonelywood Road → Termalaine → Termalaine Path [2121] → Bryn Shander [2012] → The Eastway [2030].

**Who is physically on the map.** The Heralds (Alberica, Reptin, Alerion, Shade, Shimmer, later Meat Meat) have no sprites on any map in this chapter. Every line they speak is spoken by nobody you can see (the log marks 58 boxes `absent` or `off_camera`). The only times a party member is on screen: a stand-in Shimmer in the vision and as a ghost at the Glade, Meat Meat's own NPC at the Glade, and a stand-in Shade who pops in for two boxes at the Requiem. Where a Herald talks during someone else's conversation, the speech bubble lands on that NPC instead (Nimsy, Luna Carver). Each scene says so.

**Played:** 163 message boxes, 20 scenes (3.01–3.20), 4 battles (Nettle, Wyst, Sephek, Tamsa). Side content, other branches and townsfolk follow the spine, written from the code.

**Not in this part:** Termalaine's town, its gem-mine quest and the Lonelywood Forest / elven tomb half of "The White Moose" are Ten-Towns side content (Part 14). The Lucky Liar's rumour-teller uses the shared Ten-Towns rumour list (Part 14).
