This part runs from the Council's summons (the last box of Part 06) to the step onto the Rootward Barricade, where Chapter 8 begins. The test played New Journey mode as "[Name]", a newcomer and not a Herald. It took the optional Echo Gate and the Mirror Maze first, then Road A, then Road B. The log is `parts/07.txt` (1,952 lines). It has **866 message boxes, 96 battles (20 of them story or trainer fights) and 55 map changes**, plus four lost battles that sent the player back to a rest point.

**Read this first: where the Heralds are.** Your party here is Alberica, Reptin, Alerion, Meat Meat, Glimmer and Wyn. None of them is drawn on any map in this chapter. They travel as companions, not followers, so only your own sprite walks around. Of their 170 lines, 145 are flagged `[offstage] … absent` in the log. The other 25 get their speech bubble on whatever event started the scene: the Blight, the lake-wyrm, Myr, Hew, Selrae, the attendant, the Shadow Mycolith. That's worse, because the bubble points at the wrong person (see `say()` in 007_Story.rb:21-40). A Herald appears only when a scene calls `stage_act`: a stand-in pops up next to you, plays one pose, and is removed by `Stage.cleanup` a few boxes later. Every scene below says who is really on the map.

Conventions: `> CAMERA`, `> ACTION`, `> CHOICE`, `> BATTLE`, `> GOAL`, `> FLAGS`. Staging I inferred from the code, not from the log, is marked "(code)". Wild battles on the way are listed in one line. Item boxes read "You put the X away in the Y Pocket." (the log prints these as "awayin the [icon] Pocket", which is a line break plus the pocket icon). Content the test didn't reach is written from the code under headings marked *(from the code, not played)*.
