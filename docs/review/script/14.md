# Part 14 · Side: the Ten-Towns quests and Act I's optional townsfolk

**Scope.** The *Rime of the Frostmaiden* content built as optional quests in Act I (Mountain Climb, Foaming Mugs, A Beautiful Mine, The White Moose, The Mead Must Flow, the Council of Speakers, the Good Mead election), the Ten-Towns rumour table, and the optional people of the Act I towns (Luskan Arms, Triglio, Bryn Shander's shops and inn, the shore road, Termalaine, the Blue Clam, the Eastside, Lonelywood's forest, Ramshackle). "Holed Up" (Dougan's Hole) is Chapter 4's spine and is scripted in Part 04; only its optional corners are here (14.14). Targos's Sparring Ring, Kip's Cups, the widow and the trapper are Part 01's.

**Sources.** The as-played run reached almost none of this: it met Scramsax once (played), crossed Termalaine's square in one cutscene (played, Part 02/03), walked through the Lonelywood Forest map without stopping, and rested in the Good Mead mead hall. Its sweep also logged three silent events: "Boy on map 2001 said nothing", "Jestin Hunrae on map 2007 said nothing" and the Lucky Liar rumour-teller (below). Everything else is written from the code (`045_*.rb`, `017`, `014`, `015`, `022`) and the map builders (`build_act1.rb`, `build_ch2.rb`, `build_ch4.rb`, `build_tentowns.rb`) and is marked *(from the code, not played)*. Staging is (code).

**Box count.** The script below holds about 1,270 message boxes (about 580 spoken, 690 narration) and 70 choice prompts, in 35 scenes and 12 townsfolk tables. Branches are written out in full; a box that appears in several branches is written once.
