# 04 · Chapter 4: Dougan's Hole (as played)

The Eastway, Good Mead, the Redwaters shore, Dougan's Hole, the Western Tundra and Garagai's ice lodge, Gym 3, and the night of the hag. Played in New Journey mode with the full party (Alberica, Reptin, Alerion, Shade, Meat Meat).

**How to read this part.** Every played box is quoted verbatim from the as-played log (`parts/04.txt`, 241 boxes), in play order. The player is written **[Name]**. Branches the test didn't take, and content the log never reached, are written from the code and marked *(from the code, not played)*. Staging that comes from the code and not the log is marked "(code)".

**Staging, once for the whole chapter.** No Herald is ever placed on a Chapter 4 map. There is no `Stage.herald` or `Stage.actor` call anywhere in `022_Chapter4.rb` or `045_HoledUp.rb`; the only staging in the chapter is the crack effect on the three ice mirrors. Every line spoken by Alberica, Reptin, Alerion, Shade or Meat Meat below comes from nobody on screen. Where the line was triggered by talking to an NPC, `say()` falls back to that NPC and the speech bubble points at **them** instead (noted under CAMERA below). Apart from door fades and battles, nothing on screen moves in any scene in this chapter unless a line says so.
