# Part 12 · Chapter 12: The Sea of Moving Ice — as-played script

From the griffon-roost goal on the Canopy-Tier, through Auril's three relics in the Heartroot, the Glasswater, the
Auroral Wall and the White Vault (its door, its three circuits and Avarice's cradle), back to Rootspire, Tamsin's
last battle, the griffon flight over the Sea of Moving Ice, and the dock at Solstice, up to the goal "Solstice. Up
to the crowned skull." and the mouth of the Statue Fields (Part 13 starts there).

**Source:** the headless New Journey playthrough (`parts/12.txt`), with branches the test didn't take written from
the code. **Run state:** Journey mode; all five Heralds travel with you (Alberica, Reptin, Alerion, Meat Meat,
Wyn); the Council elected Rheven, so the crossing is by **griffon**; the three Frostshard Sigils are already merged
(end of Ch 11); you hold all four Herald relics (Heartwarden's Staff, Rime Mantle, Whisper Key, Verdant Blade);
`lake_ghost_rested`, the Still Sky blessing and `corsair_favour` are set.

**Code:** `047c_WhiteVault.rb` (relics, Vault), `047b_TrialWeek.rb` (Glasswater boat, Wall, frozen-time hazards),
`025_Chapter7.rb` (Root-Gate), `026_Rootspire.rb` (root-lift), `038_Chapter12.rb` (Tamsin, voyage, camp, Sopo).
**Maps:** `tools/build_w4.rb` (2057/2058/2067 relics, 2078, 2163, 2168-2170), `tools/build_act3.rb` (2061 roost,
2079 Solstice).

**A note that applies to every scene below.** None of the scenes in this part calls `Stage.*`. No Herald, Tamsin,
Hellen, Rheven, Avarice-as-speaker or Corsair is ever put on the map. Of the 71 named lines in this part, 65 are
logged `[offstage] … absent`; the other six are the lift-keeper (2) and Sopo (2), who are real map events, and
Reptin and Meat Meat at the dock, whose bubbles fall back onto Sopo's sprite (see 12.16). Where a scene says
"nothing moves", that is literal: no actor, walk, emote, pan, panel, flash or shake plays anywhere in Part 12. The only `[stage]` events in the log are
`overlay off O-01` on map changes and after battles.
