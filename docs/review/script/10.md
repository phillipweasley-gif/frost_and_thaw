The trial week, from `trials_open` to `ch10_done`, as the New Journey run played it (player name written [Name]); then the week's content the run did not reach, from the code. Every message box is here, verbatim and in order, one box per line. Code: `036_Chapter10.rb`, `047b_TrialWeek.rb`, `047d_FixRound.rb`, `048_Rootspire_*`; maps `tools/build_ch10.rb`, `build_w4.rb`.

**A staging fact that holds for the whole chapter.** In New Journey mode the Heralds who travel with you (Alberica, Reptin, Alerion, Meat Meat, and Wyn once they join) have **no sprite on any map**: they are "companions" in `state[:companions]`, never events. Every Herald line below is spoken by nobody on screen (the log marks most of them `absent`; the rest are unmarked only because `say()` hands an unmatched speaker's bubble to the event you're talking to). The only exception in the whole week is a stand-in Alerion who appears for one kneel in 10.23. Anduin, Phildendrin, Luminara and Selrae are also absent or off camera in most of their scenes; each scene says where.

**The week's clock.** The run rested at the Hearth once, so the whole week played on "Day 2". The verdict says "The seventh day" while Kelyn's count says "Day 2 of seven" (10.41).
