# Dialogue review · Part 05 · Chapter 5: The Cackling Chasm

Judged against Martell's *Dialogue Secrets* (polish checklist, §2) and Seger's dialogue checklist L, with `docs/story/voices.md` as the yardstick. Scene numbers and titles are from `docs/review/script/05.md`. The mechanical findings in `notes/05.md` §3 (box length, pronouns, positions) are not repeated here except where their rewrite is itself a dialogue problem (section 5).

## 1. In one paragraph

Chapter 5 is mostly narration with the Heralds' lines hung on it like captions. The typical exchange is a round of single reactions to the plot ("They had a child." / "Then we take it back." / "We won't let her do this to anyone else."): people who agree, so nothing bounces (Martell, p. 17). The best lines are small and in voice. Alerion's "Me.", "Noted." and "We could have." work, so do Alberica's "I'm the morale" and "Then nobody tell anyone", Meat Meat's "Touch baby yeti and Meat Meat takes your face", and Shade's "...Hi.". They come in two- and three-box exchanges that end before they can turn into a scene. The chapter's two talky set pieces are monologues with a menu. The ghost (5.18) gives about 70 boxes of lore, and the player's "Ask the ghost..." menu is a Prompter (p. 15). Thredra (5.20) explains her plan to people she has frozen to the floor. The one real fight on the page is the hag's bargain, and the negotiator in the party, Shade, never negotiates; Reptin, the party's "face", speaks in flat statements all chapter. The "doesn't make sense" moments are few but real. Reptin answers a line nobody said (5.01). Reptin calls both forks a trick right after the air clue said one is real (5.10). Meat Meat says the ghost's secret out loud in the ghost's presence (5.18). Shade's lines in two scenes could belong to anyone.

### Lines that don't follow

1. **5.01.** "REPTIN: (standing) I wasn't planning to. I'm fine. Don't look at me." This follows Meat Meat's "Meat Meat will still sit next to him." "I wasn't planning to" answers Alerion's "Then don't listen to her" from two boxes up, or Alberica's Herald-mode "Up you get". Then his next box ("And last night she had more to say") shows he *was* listening. Nobody said "are you all right", so "I'm fine" answers nobody (023_Chapter5.rb:206).
2. **5.07, fight branch.** "ALERION: Something did this to them. Yetis don't die like this." You have just killed her in a battle. "Something did this" either ignores that or blames the player sideways, and nobody answers it (023_Chapter5.rb:372).
3. **5.10.** "REPTIN: So both ways are tricks. Wonderful. My favourite kind of fork." If the player took "Feel the air" first, they've just been told the right is "somewhere big, and open". The line contradicts the clue (023_Chapter5.rb:450). The old rewrite fixes the logic; see section 5 for the voice.
4. **5.06.** "REPTIN: That ring under the gouges. Leaf and bone. I've seen it before. In the dream." The dream he described in 5.01 was a chained wolf and a crone. There was no ring. The plant is good, but it needs a seed in 5.01 (044_ChasmFaithful.rb:226).
5. **5.18.** "MEAT MEAT: Ghost lady is singing lady. Meat Meat says nothing. Meat Meat is very good at secrets." The ghost has just dodged "Who are you?" with "Time is a wheel." Meat Meat then answers it out loud, in front of Alberica and the ghost. Alberica doesn't react, though she's the one person who would (023_Chapter5.rb:777).
6. **5.18.** "GHOST: Child of the wolf, I sense the turmoil in you." Reptin has just resolved his turmoil ("Because somebody has to hold it."). The old notes flag this; it stands (023_Chapter5.rb:752).
7. **5.20.** "THREDRA: Well, my dear wolves... will you honour our deal?" Nobody made a deal. It's fine as manipulation only if someone says so, and nobody does (023_Chapter5.rb:949).
8. **5.20.** "THREDRA: My little seamstress's samples, all in a row." Nothing in the room is "in a row". She means the Ch4 mirrors, and the line points at nothing in this scene (023_Chapter5.rb:927).
9. **5.25.** "REPTIN: Somebody has to watch it. If I could stay, I'd stay." He doesn't yet know he can't stay; time comes loose a scene later. That's a psychic character (p. 18). The old notes flag it too (044_ChasmFaithful.rb:827).

## 7. Top 5

1. **Give Shade the bargain.** The chapter's one negotiation has a negotiator in the party who never speaks to it. Replace "She talks a lot for someone who's about to lose." with a counter-offer, give Shade "We never shook on anything", and let Thredra bounce off them. Voice comes from attitude and background (Martell, pp. 30–32); dialogue is a fight between people who want different things (p. 17).
2. **Halve the ghost and put her lore in conflict.** Cut "Listen, because I only get to say this once", the second "not a curse", "I sense the turmoil", and "Confront the witch". Let Reptin argue with "sacred charge" before he accepts it, so the exposition comes out in the bounce (Seger, pp. 136–142; Martell, pp. 69–70 on speeches). Sing the prophecy instead of reciting it.
3. **Give Reptin his polish back.** Outside the wolf moments he should be the sideways, smiling talker. Then the plain "I'll keep it" and "Not one more step" land, because they're the exceptions (voices.md; Martell, p. 30 on attitude, p. 38: directness has more force when it's rare). Cut two of his three "I'm fine"s, and keep it out of Shade's mouth (p. 35).
4. **Fix the lines that don't follow.** 5.01 "I wasn't planning to", 5.07 "Something did this to them", 5.10 "both ways are tricks", the 5.06 ring missing from the dream, Meat Meat's spoiler in 5.18, and "honour our deal" with no answer. No psychic characters (p. 18).
5. **Turn the round-robins into exchanges.** In 5.08 (the swaddling) and 5.14 (the voices), each Herald reacts to the plot once and nobody reacts to anyone. Cut one agreeing line from each, and add one Herald answering another (house rule 3; Martell, p. 17 on the bounce; p. 59, intersecting monologues done on purpose).
