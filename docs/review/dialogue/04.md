# Dialogue review 04 · Chapter 4: Dougan's Hole

Judged as dialogue against Martell's *Dialogue Secrets* (polish checklist, §2 of the reference) and Seger's dialogue chapter, with `docs/story/voices.md` as the yardstick. Script: `docs/review/script/04.md`. File refs: `022` = `022_Chapter4.rb`, `045H` = `045_HoledUp.rb`, `044` = `044_ChasmFaithful.rb`, all in `game/Data/Scripts/998_FrostThaw/`.

## 1. In one paragraph

Chapter 4 is the best-written dialogue in Act I. Its scenes are fights. Durmoot refuses and Reptin works on her. Koran lies and the party lets him think it's working. Norsu accuses, and you have to answer. Thredra bargains and three Heralds argue Reptin out of it. Several exchanges are textbook bounce: "They're people. Stopped." / "Stopped how, pretty boy?" / "Like I was."; "Think of it as a long, cold walk with excellent company." / "I am. That's the problem."; "Thank you." / "...Don't tell anyone I said that." What goes wrong is smaller and repeated. **Running bits are used past their limit.** Reptin's "she's saying / she means" interpreter gag plays twice here (four times across Ch3–4). Alberica's "I think I'm in love" is the third love-declaration in two chapters. Alberica and Meat Meat both do "I'm not crying" in back-to-back boxes. "Durmoot tells everyone to stay in" is spent three times: in a goal, in Shade's mouth, and in Alerion's line that doesn't follow. **Alerion is sometimes written as a quipping hero or an aphorist** ("Then let's go and find out what's so funny.", "Find the hook before you bite.") instead of flat and literal. **Shimmer's absence goes almost unspoken on the spine.** Thredra names her ("She already carried a door."), and Shade, whose sister it was, says nothing. Shade's one grief line in the menu says the feeling outright. The lines that don't follow are few but real: Alerion's "She told the wolves to stay in too.", Norsu's "And one day, them.", and a party-chat line quoting something Thredra never said.

**Yes / no / hello / goodbye seen in this part**

- Thredra, hello: "Come in, dearies. Mind the cold. Mind your step." Goodbye: "Come and find me in the chasm, dearies. Bring your wolf."
- Reptin, yes: "We'll come. No need for teeth. Teeth are so... last resort." Oath: "I swear it."
- Alerion, no: "No." (Kanan's stand-firm branch).
- Meat Meat, no: "Meat Meat bites back. Meat Meat is very bitey." Yes: "Meat Meat swears too."
- Olivessa, hello: "Headed to the Hole? Eat first. Nobody comes back from there fat."
- Brewmistress, hello: "Come in, come in! Sit by the hive fire."

**For the voice sheets: three hosts share one hello.** Nimsy (3.05): "Come in, come in, mind the little ones." Brewmistress (4.03): "Come in, come in!" Thredra (4.22): "Come in, dearies. Mind the cold." If the hag's false welcome is meant to echo the two real ones, that's an echo line (Martell pp. 48–49), and a good one. Then the Brewmistress copy dilutes it, and she should get her own hello ("In you go. Sit by the hive fire."). If it isn't meant, all three need their own.

**Lines that don't follow** (the owner's "doesn't make sense")
1. **4.20** ALERION: "She told the wolves to stay in too." Durmoot never spoke to the wolves (notes).
2. **4.14** NORSU: "The wolves bring me their food. Their gold. And one day, them." "Them" could be the wolves, or the children, or you. And why do wolves bring a mammoth gold?
3. **4.14** NORSU (to "We'll find who took the axe."): "The ones who killed him took his axe, and left him for me to watch." This repeats his own first speech and doesn't react to the offer.
4. **4.24** ALERION: "She means all of us. She just said your name first." Thredra never said Reptin's name. She said "your wolf".
5. **Dougan's Hole chat (after the hag)** REPTIN: "She called me 'wolf of seasons'. Said balance is a leash." She never says "balance is a leash".
6. **4.08 (branch)** ALERION: "Told you. Liars forget." He only told you in an optional Heralds-menu line ("If the wolf comes, watch which leg it favours.").
7. **4.18** KORAN: "The Dale is very hungry. So is he." "He" has no clear referent (see §5 on the old fix).
8. **Hilda's house (after)** SILJA: "Finn says he could've got out himself. He couldn't." The same joke the player heard at the cage.
9. **4.20 → 4.21** GOAL: "Rest for the night." Night falls the moment you leave the lodge, with no rest (notes).
