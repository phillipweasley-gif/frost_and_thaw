# 02 · Chapter 2: Lottery Night — dialogue review (against Martell and Seger)

Source: `docs/review/script/02.md` (as played), the code (`014_Chapter2.rb`, `045_TenTowns.rb`, `045_FoamingMugs.rb`, `016_TrainerLines.rb`), voices.md, and section 3 of `docs/review/notes/02.md`. Line numbers are the current code's.

## 1. In one paragraph

Line for line, this chapter has the game's sharpest dialogue so far. The road banter bounces on hook words ("humming" / "hum" / "in tune"). The Razzy beat lands on "Kid. Traps don't shiver." The lottery has a real fight in it: Sephek wants Shimmer, Shimmer says "Tell her I said no. Again.", and the threat comes with a clock. Duvessa and the Council make the bargain's cost concrete with detail (Kendrick's town sleeping "in a heap round the hive like bees"). What goes wrong falls into three kinds. **Lines that follow nothing**: Shade's "Too late. You already have." after Reptin's line; Alerion's "You're not fine" to a "fine" nobody said; Shade's "Goblins" about dwarves; Mishann greeting you like a stranger minutes after the acorn. **Plot devices that announce themselves**: the unicorn horn turns up the moment it's needed, with "Never knew why I kept it" (Martell's retroactive plotting, p. 12). **Speeches with passive listeners**: Scramsax's sales pitch and Mishann's acorn run five boxes each (p. 69). Under that, the same jokes recur across the cast: the "That's either X or Y" either/or (Reptin in 1.03, Shade here), the gym leaders' "Fitting for a town…", the Speakers' "keeps its word. Even…", and Alberica opening nearly every line with a pet name.

## 7. The owner's decision: your own goal, leading to Dougan's Hole

In this chapter the goal pulls you away from Dougan's Hole. The relics point south-east, but Duvessa and Sephek send you north to Lonelywood. Once you have a goal of your own that leads to the Hole, the chapter needs one beat to own the detour, or the player will feel the story ignoring them. Lines that would need to change:
- **Goals** "Follow the road south-east to Bryn Shander." (014:387), "Keep the egg warm. South-east to Bryn Shander." (014:477), "Sephek. Lonelywood. Before the next moon…" (014:656) and "Sephek is in Lonelywood: out the north gate, through Termalaine." (after the seal): add your aim, or say why it waits.
- **2.08 after Sephek** (014:651-652): the best place for a Herald to put the detour into words for you. For example, "ALERION: (to you) Your Hole can wait a moon. Shimmer can't." *(suggestion)*. That gives you a want the scene pushes against, which is Martell's "two wants" (p. 17).
- **East gate guards** (Bryn Shander, both versions): "Nobody's been back from the Hole in a month. The gate stays shut." This is now the obstacle to your goal. Keep it, and let it play when you try the gate, so the block is felt.
- **The old hauler** ("You can hear her laughing out on the ice.") and **Wen** ("I hear laughing from the south"): good plants for the hag. Keep them.
- **2.10 Mishann** ("It was always meant to go north"): fine. It's the Heralds' thread, not yours.

The White Vault doesn't appear anywhere in part 02.
