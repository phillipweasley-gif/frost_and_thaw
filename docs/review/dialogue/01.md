# 01 · Chapter 1: Targos — dialogue review (against Martell and Seger)

Source: `docs/review/script/01.md` (as played), the code (`007_Story.rb`, `017_TargosExtras.rb`, `006_Dialogue.rb`, `045_MountainClimb.rb`, `045_TenTowns.rb`), voices.md (Sample 1 is this chapter's tavern), and section 3 of `docs/review/notes/01.md`. Line numbers are the current code's, which has moved a few lines since the notes were written.

## 1. In one paragraph

This is the best-voiced chapter so far, because its centre (1.02–1.03) is the owner's own Sample 1, played almost word for word. Five voices pass the cover-the-names test on their own lines, the relic reveals bounce ("That's all? …" / "No I don't."; "Don't ask." / "Oh, now I have to ask." / "Don't."), and the one echo ("Everybody does, eventually." / "Everybody leaves eventually, too.") is exactly what Martell means by a hook word (p. 45). The rest of the chapter sounds right but is built as information handed over, not as a fight. Epheris explains, Naerth explains, and Keegan explains, while the Heralds agree, ask the leading question, or add an aside (Martell's Prompter, p. 15; the "unalogue", p. 69). Nobody ever asks for what they want: the party never asks Naerth to let them leave, and Naerth answers anyway (no psychic characters, p. 18). The "doesn't make sense" moments are mostly lines that follow the wrong line: Reptin thanks Naerth for a TM with a joke about the seal, Shimmer's "it" points at nothing, and a pronoun turns Shade into a cat.
