# Dialogue review 03 · Chapter 3: Shimmer's Requiem

Judged as dialogue against Martell's *Dialogue Secrets* (polish checklist, §2 of the reference) and Seger's dialogue chapter, with `docs/story/voices.md` as the yardstick. Script: `docs/review/script/03.md`. File refs: `015` = `015_Chapter3.rb`, `044` = `044_ChasmFaithful.rb`, `045T` = `045_Termalaine.rb`, `045W` = `045_WhiteMoose.rb`, all in `game/Data/Scripts/998_FrostThaw/`.

## 1. In one paragraph

Chapter 3 has some of the best single lines in the game. Shade's "...She always does this." over the note, "My house is one smuggler and a carved cat. But thanks.", Sephek's "She killed herself. I only held the knife.", Zenith's "Not her vessel. Her answer." and Alberica's "She'd have hated that. She'd have loved it." all say the true thing sideways, which is the house style. Most of the cast passes the cover-the-names test most of the time. Three habits pull against those lines. First, the chapter's big moment (the vision, 3.07) is written in a different register from the rest of Shimmer: trailer-speech antitheses ("This isn't salvation. It's surrender.", "Damnation, or servitude.") where everywhere else she is clipped and dry. Second, the grief is surrounded by jokes that don't know where they are: Meat Meat's debut at the altar where Shimmer died runs four gags in a row and nobody who lost her reacts. Third, lore arrives as blocks from people nobody argues with. Luna speaks 8 of the 10 boxes in 3.18, and Jet Sentry's three boxes at the Glade send you to the killer while the brother of the dead woman says nothing. Two confessions are "let me tell you what I'm feeling" in its plain form (Martell, pp. 11–12): Shimmer announces hers ("You should know why he wants me.") and Sephek narrates his origin to nobody as he freezes. Each one needs someone in the room to push back, not a better speech.

**Lines that don't follow** (the owner's "doesn't make sense")
1. **3.06** SHADE: (reading) "He said before the next moon, or he picks someone else." No one ever heard Sephek say this. His only threat, at Torg's, is "Tonight, or the next" (optional and missed on the spine). The note answers a conversation the player wasn't at.
2. **3.08** ALERION: "East. The vision was east. The old logging road." The vision showed a stone circle. Nothing has told him where it is (only Jet, if you talk to her outside, names the Glade). He knows more than he can (p. 18).
3. **3.11** JET SENTRY: "The Circle wants that heart." The line before is Shade's "I'm not leaving her here." A stranger claims his sister's heart, and Shade lets it pass. A line that ignores the line before.
4. **3.11** JET SENTRY: "And the man who took her waits at our Moon Temple for it." This sends you to the killer with the thing he wants, and gives no reason.
5. **3.15** SEPHEK: "...I wasn't born at Midwinter." This contradicts a claim made only in the optional Torg's talk. On the spine it answers nothing.
6. **3.17** ALERION: "...Frostina." In every mode but Alerion's, nothing on screen causes him to name the egg. The ring narration that explains it plays only when you are him.
7. **3.17** SHADE: "That's... that's her song." The player has never heard Shimmer hum (notes 3.05).
8. **3.18** LUNA CARVER: "We kept an egg for two years." This follows "follow you. At a distance." with no link.
9. **3.05** SHADE: "She means it." Two boxes after Nimsy spoke, "she" reads as Nimsy.
10. **3.13** (before-the-Glade branch) SHADE: "He's here. I can feel it." He isn't.
