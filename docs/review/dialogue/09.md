# Dialogue review, part 09 · Chapter 9: Destruction's Light

Judged against Martell's *Dialogue Secrets* (the polish checklist, §2 of the reference) and Seger's dialogue chapter, with `docs/story/voices.md` as the yardstick. Source: `docs/review/script/09.md` (Frost version; Thaw branches from the code). Code is `game/Data/Scripts/998_FrostThaw/` (`035_Chapter9.rb` unless named). Mechanical findings in `notes/09.md` §3 are not repeated.

## 1. In one paragraph

Chapter 9 is the best-voiced chapter of Act II. The party trades lines with each other and not just with the plot. "Pretty boy. You're glowing." / "I know." and "Var was a coat! A coat!" / "She's right. He was a coat." are the cast at its best, and the walk south is a model rest-point banter. The trouble is at the joins. The chapter is an emergency (a dragon burning towns), but almost nobody in it *wants* anything from anybody. The Shrine is a 29-box ceremony of agreement while Good Mead burns, and the chase has nothing in the way except the dragon's battles (Martell p. 17: dialogue is a fight). The lines that don't follow cluster at the two hinges. At Bryn Shander, Avarice's face appears and nobody says she's alive, after Ch8 had everyone call her dead. At the arrest, Meat Meat's "Var was a coat!" gets no answer, Kelyn then *tells* us what the crowd is about to shout, and the Tribunal admits Var was Avarice while charging the Heralds for Var's death. That's the owner's "doesn't make sense": the arrest's logic is never argued out loud by anyone who disagrees.

### Lines that don't follow (all found in this part)

1. **9.01** The lift-keeper ("The dragons have circled low since you came.") ignores the black dragon that just tore up through the tree (old notes).
2. **9.03** "Frostina waits at the Shrine, very still." contradicts Ch8's last line, "Far above, two dragons are already diving." (old notes).
3. **9.04** "REPTIN: Lovely to be home, isn't it? Never again!" "Never again" answers nothing Alberica said (old notes; their rewrite is itself a problem, section 5).
4. **9.04** Meat Meat restates Alerion's flight order instead of bouncing off it (old notes).
5. **9.06** "Turn back. She's right." Who is "she"? (old notes; their "Winter's right." holds).
6. **9.07** "ALERION: It's coming here." doesn't answer "Is that thing yours?" (old notes; their "No." fix holds).
7. **9.07** Avarice's face says "Pick me up." In Ch8, Alerion said "...Dead. Nothing in her." and Alberica said "She doesn't get to come back from that." Nobody connects the two. Meat Meat's stamp is the right action, but the party needs one line of *recognition*.
8. **9.08 → 9.10** Nobody ever mentions the shard again: not on the walk, not at the arrest.
9. **9.10** "MEAT MEAT: Var was a coat! A coat!" / "ALERION: She's right. He was a coat." Kelyn's reply, "I can't stop this. The city saw the summer die.", talks past them. Worse, Kelyn heard Avarice's own voice over the sending-stone in 8.19.
10. **9.10** "KELYN: Half of them think you saved them. Half think you killed it." Then the crowd says exactly that ("Murderers!" / "Liberators!"). Showing what was just said is See & Say turned round (p. 27).
11. **9.11** "KELYN: You slew Summer, who was Avarice, who wore our Marshal thirty years." The Council says Var was a coat, one scene after arresting the Heralds "for the deaths of Summer Marshal Var Rime-Breaker". If the Council accepts that, the charge has no case. The line makes the arrest look senseless instead of political.
12. **9.11** "...Some call you liberators. Some call you murderers." comes after the crowd, a child and Kelyn herself have already said it (third time).
13. **9.11** The trial of "the slayers of Summer" never names the one who broke the engine. Glimmer isn't mentioned in the whole of Ch9 after the Shrine.

## 7. Top 5

1. **Make the arrest argue (9.10–9.11).** Kelyn answers "coat" with what she heard on the stone. Cut her "half and half", since the crowd shows it. The Tribunal keeps the ambiguity ("Summer is dead, and our Marshal with her. By your hands.") instead of conceding the defence (Martell pp. 17, 18, 27).
2. **Recognise Avarice's face (9.07).** Keep "No.", then one Alerion callback: "'Nothing in her.' I said that." It mends the hole Ch8's faked death leaves (p. 18).
3. **Show, don't label (9.07–9.08).** The twin iron ring, not "My husband". Cut "What have you become?" so the tree on the horizon answers it (pp. 27, 38, 40, 82).
4. **Name the absent and let the accused answer (9.11).** Alberica says Glimmer did it; Wyn owns leaving the door in one brave line (voices.md; house rule 3).
5. **Put the clock back in (9.03–9.04).** Razzilith's "CHASE" before the jokes run dry; fix Reptin's and Meat Meat's non-bounces in the air (pp. 17, 21).
