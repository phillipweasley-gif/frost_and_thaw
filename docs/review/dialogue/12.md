# Part 12 · Dialogue review: Chapter 12, The Sea of Moving Ice

*Redone against Martell's* Dialogue Secrets *and Seger's dialogue chapter, per `BRIEF.md`. Source: `docs/review/script/12.md` (as played). Line refs are to `game/Data/Scripts/998_FrostThaw/` (`038` = 038_Chapter12.rb, `047b` = 047b_TrialWeek.rb, `047c` = 047c_WhiteVault.rb, `026` = 026_Rootspire.rb).*

## 1. In one paragraph

Part 12 has two kinds of dialogue. The Herald banter (Tamsin's send-off, the flight, the camp, Sopo) is the best-voiced run in Act III. Lines bounce: "They mean they'll miss us." / "I mean I'll WIN." Running bits get twisted rather than repeated ("Meat Meat will bite it. Not fondly."), and fear sits under the jokes ("Meat Meat will die for you. Tomorrow, probably."). The plot dialogue is weaker. The three relic scenes play the same two lines three times. In the White Vault, Avarice gets a ten-box speech in which four Heralds are insulted by name and none of them answers (Martell's "unalogue", p. 69). One insult contradicts canon, and the reply that does come ("Meat Meat is coming") answers the last line, not the cruellest one. A few Herald lines in the Vault tell the player what the narration has just shown. Reptin also picks up Alberica's "darling" again.
