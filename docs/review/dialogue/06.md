# Dialogue review · Part 06 · Chapter 6: The Festival of Returning Light

Judged against Martell's *Dialogue Secrets* (polish checklist, §2) and Seger's checklist L, with `docs/story/voices.md` as the yardstick. Scene numbers and titles come from `docs/review/script/06.md`. Mechanical findings in `notes/06.md` §3 are not repeated here, except where their rewrite is itself a problem (section 5). Per the owner's decision, **"Summer is Avarice" stays a Chapter 8 twist**: every Chapter 6 line that names or describes Avarice as Summer is flagged in section 4b.

## 1. In one paragraph

Chapter 6 has the best dialogue in Act I–II so far. The party relaxes into its voice-sheet selves. The Council scene (6.04) is the model for the whole game: the thirty-year summer, the Wall and the expansion fight all come out of three councillors who want different things ("KELYN: No." / "SELRAE: She always says no. Then something burns, and she says yes."). Reptin gets his polish back, Alerion gets his one-liners, and the coffee scene plays the owner's own sample. The newcomers mostly have voices of their own. Hellen feeds you first. Selrae cuts. Wynsnow measures. Eirwen fusses. Eline is gently terrible. Three things go wrong:

- **Exposition by speech.** Where the 400 years isn't argued about, it's recited. Hellen narrates her grandmother's version to a Prompter ("And outside?"). Lumin explains the dragons over their own reunion. Kelyn's toast and summons recap what the player just did. Razzilith, who should speak in single painted words, delivers a six-box briefing that also gives away the Chapter 8 twist.
- **Two of the newcomers have no voice.** Darrus Fell (meant to be "velvet, clove bark, charm") is curt, and only his wryly chews clove bark. Tamsin never once uses your full name, the one tic voices.md gives them.
- **Tics turn to wallpaper.** "Four hundred years" is spoken or narrated 18 times. Alberica uses about 19 pet names in one chapter, not counting "pretty boy" and three "I love"s. "Diplomatically" comes three times, and "hammers" six times across two scenes.

The "doesn't make sense" lines are mostly people knowing things they couldn't. Alerion names Frostina, whom nobody has named. Kelyn recites Eirwen's private maps. Alberica reads an "A" off a sigil. Shade says they were "somebody's vessel", when that was their sister.

### Lines that don't follow

1. **6.02.** "ALERION: And outside?" Nobody has mentioned an outside except Hellen's "Inside the Auroral Wall, anyway", which a new player can't parse. Alerion is the party member least likely to ask for more lore. It's a Prompter line (024_Chapter6.rb:334).
2. **6.04.** "SELRAE: Sparkbearer." (048_D:416) and "SHADE: I've been somebody's vessel." (048_D:422). Shade hasn't been named Sparkbearer yet. And it was Shimmer whom Auril wore, not Shade (story bible). The line gives Shade their sister's history.
3. **6.04 against 6.17.** "DARRUS: Our Summer Marshal... went down to check the heat in the roots... He hasn't come back." (024:586-587), then "RAZZILITH: Autumn hides it... Says Summer sleeps. Lies." (048_F:484). Autumn told the Council and the Heralds in the open. Either Darrus lies about where the Marshal went, or Razzilith's line is wrong.
4. **6.07.** "ALERION: (very quietly) Into Frostina." (048_D:52) is the first time anyone says her name, and nobody told him. A psychic character (p. 18).
5. **6.09.** "ALERION: No." (026:808), right after Wynsnow measures *you*. Nobody asked Alerion anything, so Reptin's "He means he'd rather not" explains a refusal of nothing.
6. **6.14.** "ELINE: And tell your Winter Matron her chair is beside mine." (048_D:446) comes straight after Glimmer's "I'd rather grieve." The "And" continues a thought nobody had. She changes subject without reacting.
7. **6.12.** "LORN SCRIBE: You wonder who I write for." (048_F:587). Nobody wondered aloud. "Riddles, Herald? One for each of you." (026:360) answers no request.
8. **6.22.** "EIRWEN: Something burns beneath us. The Summer Marshal ventured down to inspect it. / He has not returned." (048_F:691-692). Told to the Heralds, who heard it from Darrus in 6.04: "as you know" (p. 12).
9. **6.24.** "ALERION: Chardalyn. Demon-forged. I haven't seen that since the Dale." (048_E:228). Rootspire *is* in the Dale.
10. **6.24.** "ALBERICA: Those sigils. They're hers. The cracked mirror and the A." (048_E:229). "Hers" points at nobody, and no "A" mark has been shown to the player before.
11. **6.25.** "KELYN: ...now we know where. Eirwen's maps. The scorch at the Echo Gate. Three wardens' cloaks." (024:511). The maps were shown "in a room nobody else is allowed into" a minute ago, and Kelyn wasn't there. It's also a recap of what we saw (p. 11).
12. **6.22.** "MEAT MEAT: Mushroom person smells like coffee. Meat Meat approves." (024:495). This settles the coffee scene's question before it's asked; there, Meat Meat squints at mushroom coffee as if it's new.
