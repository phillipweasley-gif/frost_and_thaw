# 00 · Prologue: The Lantern — dialogue review (against Martell and Seger)

Source: the as-played script `docs/review/script/00.md`, the code (`005_NewGame.rb`, `030_Prologue.rb`, `001_Config.rb`), voices.md, and section 3 of `docs/review/notes/00.md`. Line numbers are the current code's. Cites are to the PDF page markers in the skill's `sources/text/`.

## 1. In one paragraph

The prologue's dialogue is short, quick and mostly in voice. Tamsin is a clear, loud Hop type. Hellen feeds you before she lets you talk. Wynsnow has a real verbal habit (he measures and writes everything down) and one good danger joke per starter. The best exchanges bounce on a hook word: "Obviously." / "Obviously.", "on an empty stomach" / "on pine-mead and spite", "Not even the carver." / "The carver was my great-great-something" (Martell, p. 45). What goes wrong is small but adds up to "doesn't quite make sense". Three lines answer something nobody said or restate what was just said ("Yes, it's always first light", Tamsin repeating Wynsnow's "future Rootwarden", Tamsin's "something on the wall I want to show you" about a wall you've seen every day). Tamsin says your name in seven of her boxes, which wears out her one pet habit (Martell, pp. 35, 134). Nobody in the Rootspire scenes wants anything that someone else resists, apart from Tamsin's rivalry, so most scenes are people agreeing (Martell, p. 17). In Herald Mode, each Herald's best tavern line gets used up early: the select-screen blurb and the ship prologue both say it before the tavern does. The Herald Mode openings themselves (Wyn above all) are the best-voiced writing in the part.

## 7. The owner's decision: your own goal, leading to Dougan's Hole

As played, the New Journey kid has no want of their own in the prologue or in Chapters 1–2: the Heralds' relics point south-east, and you tag along. The prologue already holds the seed: the lantern is "Reversal glass… where the old hag's mirrors broke", and the hag is Thredra of Dougan's Hole. Lines here that would need to change:

- **0.01 mode help** (005:99): if you rewrite "you fall back through time", don't promise a goal the game doesn't give yet.
