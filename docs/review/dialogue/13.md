# Part 13 · Dialogue review: Grimskalle, the Four Tests, the endings and the epilogue

*Redone against Martell's* Dialogue Secrets *and Seger's dialogue chapter, per `BRIEF.md`. Source: `docs/review/script/13.md` (as played). Line refs are to `game/Data/Scripts/998_FrostThaw/` (`038` = 038_Chapter12.rb, `039` = 039_Chapter13.rb, `047c` = 047c_WhiteVault.rb), as the files stood on 2026-11-05 after the owner's White Vault edit. Since the script was logged, the code has added a loss line per Test (039:146-151); those lines are reviewed below too.*

## 1. In one paragraph

This is the best-written dialogue in the game. Each Test turns on one refusal, said in the Herald's own voice ("Meat Meat was made a weapon. Meat Meat chose to be a seed.", "...It would be quiet.", "Yes. And somebody will sing about it."). Sephek's turn closes an echo that began in Chapter 3. The Third Way pays off a "hold" echo that runs from Reptin's circuit line, through his Endurance test, to Alberica's "Reptin taught me that." What goes wrong is small and specific. Three lines in Sephek's turn use "she" and "her" for three different women (Auril, Shimmer, Frostina), so the scene's key beat can't be followed. The old rewrite of his turn line is on the nose and throws away the bible's own wording. The god debate (13.18) is five people each reading out one ending, with nobody pushing back. Wyn's brave line, set up in Chapter 11, never comes. On the length question: the endings themselves are short and earn their boxes. It's the run around them that doesn't: Auril's thesis line, the debate, and about six epilogue boxes that explain instead of show (Martell p. 70; VO rules pp. 137-140). Auril keeps her bible voice throughout ("speaks rarely and never begs"), with one near-miss.

**Lines that don't follow (all found in this part)**

1. 13.02, "ALBERICA: It's her. From the vision. Auril kept her. She kept the moment." (038:272). Which vision? Then "she" switches from Auril to... Auril again. "ALERION: She didn't keep anything. That's not her." makes the reader work out which "she" each time.
2. 13.09, "AURIL: (a whisper) You were made a weapon, V-7. Be one." (039:182). Auril uses Avarice's lab number. Can the goddess know it? Perhaps; but the word belongs to Avarice (vocabulary lever, p. 36).
3. 13.15, "SEPHEK: I've held a lot of knives for her. Four hundred years in the ice." (039:349). The second sentence reads as when he held them, and he was frozen.
4. 13.15, "ALERION: She told you there was a third way. You heard her." (039:365). "She" follows "She speaks to two of us" (Auril), but means Shimmer (old row).
5. 13.15, "ALERION: You've been hearing her for four hundred years." (039:366). In Ch 11 "hear her" meant Auril ("Two of us hear her") and Frostina ("He always hears her first"). Here it means Shimmer's laugh. That's three meanings for one phrase, and this is the turn.
6. 13.15, "SEPHEK: (looking at Frostina) Her silence honours her. Not her return." (039:380). A riddle at the hinge; see §5 for the fix.
7. 13.15, "WYN: (very quietly) She chose. That's what you gave her, when you let her go. A choice." (047c:621). It follows Sephek's line, so "you" reads as Sephek, but it means the player, who spared her at the Shrine (p. 18, misreading).
8. 13.16, "AURIL: You broke my tests." (039:442). It plays when a test was lost (old row; holds up).
9. 13.16, "Through the roof, a green dragon lands on the ice, and Frostina beside him." (039:449). Frostina came in with you (old row).
10. 13.18, "SEPHEK: She'll come back. At the solstice..." (039:537). Sephek was last kneeling beside Eline at the Crown; nothing says he followed you into the throne room.
11. 13.18, "ALBERICA: (very still) I could. I'm the one she wanted." (039:539). Nobody answers. Reptin, who just proposed holding her, says nothing until Ending C, where his "That's not what I..." shows he objected all along.
12. 13.21, "ALBERICA: It just stops. And then it starts again." (039:606). Alberica narrates the game's loop rule from inside the ice: she knows what she can't yet know (p. 18).

That takes it to about 26 boxes with every vignette kept. "Hellen pours. Nobody makes a speech." is the right last word on speeches; keep it.
