# Part 07 · Chapter 7: Two Roads Down — dialogue review (against Martell and Seger)

Source: `docs/review/script/07.md` (as played: Echo Gate, Road A, then Road B; branches from the code where noted). Cites are to Martell, *Dialogue Secrets*, unless marked Seger. File references are `game/Data/Scripts/998_FrostThaw/`.

## 1. In one paragraph

Line by line, this is some of the best party writing in the game. The banter bounces: "Touch nothing that sings. Well. There goes my evening." / "You could not sing." Alberica, Alerion, Meat Meat and Glimmer each get lines no one else could say. The minor cast (Gant, Prototype 3, the Wennen scout, Mira's daughter, the emissary) speak in sharp, specific voices. The chapter goes wrong in three repeated ways, and those three are the owner's "doesn't make sense". First, **lines know or say what they shouldn't**. Eira says "Hyphora" two scenes before the name is a sworn secret. Wyn knows the Saint's heart is burning before anyone has said so. Alberica names Avarice from the word "her". Meat Meat "recognises" a voice she has been carrying in her hands. Second, **big lines get no answer** (p. 17). "Summer has been wearing a man for thirty years," a construct cataloguing the whole party as specimens, Myr's "songbird", a devil in a flower, and the end of the chapter all go by with nobody saying a word. Third, **the scenes with information agree instead of fight**. Selrae, Hew and the lab notes deliver confessions to a listening, sympathetic party (Reptin's "Go on, Matron. We're listening." is a textbook Prompter, p. 15). Alerion has become the man who explains each document after we've read it. Under all of this, Alberica's pet names have turned from a weapon into filler: 18 of her 42 played boxes. Reptin has caught the habit too, with 4 more. Separately, at the owner's request, §4b flags the lines that give away "Summer is Avarice" before Chapter 8.
