# Dialogue review, part 08 · Chapter 8: The Rootsun Forge

Judged against Martell's *Dialogue Secrets* (the polish checklist, §2 of the reference) and Seger's dialogue chapter, with `docs/story/voices.md` as the yardstick. Source: `docs/review/script/08.md`. Scene numbers and titles are the script's. Code is `game/Data/Scripts/998_FrostThaw/` (`034_Chapter8.rb` = Ch8, `046_ForgeRooms.rb` = Forge). Mechanical findings already in `notes/08.md` §3 are not repeated.

**Numbering note.** The owner's "8.06" (the design's *SC 8.06 Summer*, `act2_script.md`) is this script's **8.19 · The Heart Chamber: Summer**. Section 3a lists the lines that spend or contradict that twist before 8.19.

## 1. In one paragraph

When two Heralds trade lines, the chapter's dialogue is often very good. "That is a child." / "It's fuel." is the best bounce in Act II. The pens beat ("Meat Meat is not a method." / "No. You're not.") works through restraint, and Meat Meat, Alerion and Wyn are in voice almost everywhere. The trouble is structural, and there's a lot of it. Most of the dungeon's talk is a **round-robin of reactions**: a journal, mural or altar is read out, then three or four Heralds each comment on a different line of it, to the player rather than to each other (8.03, 8.07, 8.11, 8.16, 8.24). That's Martell's "Look, Mom, research!" (p. 12): experts explaining to each other what both already know, often repeating what the narration just said (See & Say, p. 27). The two big speakers have no bounce at all. The sooty duergar (8.04) and the captain (8.16) each speak six boxes into silence, and Avarice gets the chapter's biggest speech (8.19). She delivers it as an unanswered plan, not an argument (Martell pp. 69–70; WRITING_GUIDE §4, "villains make a case"). And the climax runs out of order. Avarice says "Your move", Glimmer answers someone else, Avarice says nothing while her engine dies, and her "Oh. That was clever." arrives about 35 boxes later, after a whole battle and a three-way choice. That's the owner's "doesn't quite make sense" in its purest form: lines that don't follow the line before.

### Lines that don't follow (all found in this part)

1. **8.01** "MEAT MEAT: Failures. Meat Meat has been called worse. By better." answers the duergar's line from **eight boxes back**. In between come seven narration boxes and Alberica's "That's the Dale." Move it, and Reptin's "That's the spirit.", straight after the duergar line.
2. **8.01** "ALBERICA: Hew didn't know. Look at his face when we tell him..." defends Hew against an accusation nobody made.
3. **8.03** "REPTIN: A frost-born subject... it knew Alberica." talks past Alberica, who has just spoken (old notes; their rewrite holds).
4. **8.03** "ALBERICA: ...Nobody's been up that stair in thirty years." She can't know that, so it's a psychic line (p. 18). It also spends the twist (3a).
5. **8.06** "ALBERICA: Then we keep it, kitten." answers a warning about "the heart" as if she knew whose heart it is.
6. **8.10** "ALERION: ...Like his heart in her engine." Nobody has said "his" or "heart" yet (old notes). The rewrite still knows too much (section 5).
7. **8.11** Grik's "White hair. Red-gold eyes." describes a face nobody has seen (old notes; see 3a for a better fix).
8. **8.14** "ALERION: And that's his heart. Sephek's." Unless you read the optional Dissectory notes, nothing has told him so. Ground it in what he *has* felt: Sephek's cold, in Ch3.
9. **8.17** "REPTIN: ...Var had secrets before she ever wore him." states the 8.19 reveal two rooms early (3a).
10. **8.19** Kelyn's stone hears the reveal, and Kelyn never makes a sound. In 9.10 she acts on it, but never says she heard it.
11. **8.19** "AVARICE: You survived my early prototypes." The Heralds weren't her prototypes; Meat Meat was. "...die in the womb that birthed you" is addressed to all of them and true of one.
12. **8.21** "AVARICE: ...Your move." The next line, Glimmer's "This blade's been killing me slowly since Saltmarsh.", doesn't answer her.
13. **8.21–8.23** Avarice stays silent through Glimmer's sacrifice, the Shard battle and the three-way choice, then says "Oh. That was clever." "That" is now about 35 boxes back. "With the engine dead, Var's body cracks" also recaps something that happened long ago (E11 order).
14. **8.23** "We will speak again in the White Womb..." is followed two boxes later by "...Dead. Nothing in her." (E19).
15. **8.24 → 9.03** "Far above, two dragons are already diving." Then, at the Shrine, "Frostina waits... very still." (notes 09).
16. **8.27** The autumn jokes ignore both Glimmer and the hole the dragon just tore in the tree (E23).
17. **8.09** "SELRAE: I'll be quieter about my opinions from now on." answers a scolding that happened in Ch7, not here, and her voice is "Winter does not ask permission."

### 3b. The two scenes the coordinator asked about

**Avarice (8.19): does she make her case, or monologue?** She monologues. Count it: VAR's line, the crack, then eight AVARICE boxes (three, an optional flashback, then five). Nobody answers until Meat Meat's "You took Razzy's voice." That's an *unalogue* (Martell p. 69). Its content is plot catch-up: the duergar told us the Great Unchaining in 8.04, so she's repeating a scene we've already had (p. 12). It isn't a case either. "Harvested warm", "My patron gets the rest" and "He likes his meals warm" are evil for its own sake, so the player has nothing to argue with. Darrus, two scenes earlier, makes the better argument ("No famine years, no winters, ever."). The voice sheet's lever for her is *everything is arithmetic*, and her best lines elsewhere use it ("Let's see what you've cost me.", "I love a repeatable experiment."). The fix: break the speech into an exchange, let the party's face, Reptin, ask the question the player has, and let her answer it with sums aimed at the listener's own want. Reptin's want is balance. The lottery takes a life a month (canon, 1.05), so the arithmetic writes itself:

> AVARICE: The duergar call me Xardorok. Names are coats too. So is summer. *(keep)*
> REPTIN: (politely) And the Dale? What does your summer cost them?
> AVARICE: Auril takes one a month and calls it mercy. I take them once. Do the sums.
> AVARICE: One harvest, warm, and nobody here ever freezes again. I'm the kind one, Warden.
> AVARICE: The Frostmaiden wants the world still. I want it running. On my schedule. Forever. *(the design's line)*
> MEAT MEAT: You took Razzy's voice. *(keep)*

*(The middle three lines are suggestions; check "nobody freezes again" against what the White Womb plot needs.)* No AVARICE run is longer than three boxes, the patron is gone (the duergar already sent the Dale "down into the Hells"), and she's now chilling *because* she's reasonable. Her other short lines in the chapter hold up and should stay.

**Glimmer's last scene (8.21).** The ending is the best-written death in Act II. "Tell Frostina it was a better fire. She'll know what I mean." pays off a line from Ch6 (`029_Glimmer.rb:147`) and is spoken to someone who isn't there, which is Martell's dancing around the subject (p. 38): he never says goodbye, and that *is* the goodbye. Then 9.03 echoes it again ("Glimmer said to tell you."). The middle is the problem:
- **It doesn't follow.** Avarice offers two ways ("Kill me and it blows. Leave me and it runs forever. Your move."), and Glimmer's next words answer nobody. One word fixes it and sets up his last line: "GLIMMER: (stepping past her) Neither." Then "Shimmer found the third way" is the reply to her offer, not a non sequitur.
- **He explains his plan before doing it.** "Brightmane said the curse breaks in a sacred fire." / "I am a sacred fire. Brightmane made me one." is a syllogism read aloud. It's on the nose (p. 15), and it isn't his voice: voices.md asks for Shade's wry deals, warmed through and sadder than he lets on. Worse, it wastes an echo line the game has built. "Brightmane said I'd know the fire when I saw it" comes three times (`048_Rootspire_B_People.rb:649` "That's not it. That's its chimney."; `048_Rootspire_F_Downtime.rb:453`; `034_Chapter8.rb:390`), and it never turns. Echo lines must change meaning on the last pass (pp. 48–49). This is that pass: "GLIMMER: 'You'll know the fire when you see it,' she said. She never said it'd be me."
- **Avarice goes silent** through the destruction of her own engine. She's the most curious person in the room. One line keeps the pressure on and sets up her later "Oh. That was clever.": "AVARICE: (interested, not moving) Go on. I want to see the sums." *(suggestion)*
- **"It's all right."** is throat-clearing (p. 55), and it's Wyn's comfort phrase. Cut it.
- Keep Alberica's "Glimmer. Sweetheart. Don't you dare." This is pressure, not grief, so the pet name is right. Keep "Meat Meat not crying. That is sap." (voices.md's own sample, saved for this). Keep Alerion's line, but give "it" something to point at (row 31).
