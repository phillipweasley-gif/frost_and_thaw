# Part 14 · Dialogue review: the Ten-Towns quests and Act I's townsfolk

*Redone against Martell's* Dialogue Secrets *and Seger's dialogue chapter, per `BRIEF.md`. Source: `docs/review/script/14.md`. Almost none of it was played, so the lines come from the code. Code paths are under `game/Data/Scripts/998_FrostThaw/`.*

## 1. In one paragraph

The side content has some of the best small dialogue in the game: Copper and Mishann's daily theology fight, Duvessa biting the head off a halfling cookie, Oarus's "(a pause that goes on a little too long) ...They're busy.", Shade's "Fetching your mead back, mostly.", Nimsy's "...Well. It IS white." The townsfolk are drawn from details and nearly all of them want something. It goes wrong in the **briefings**. Keegan, Hruna, Marta, Nimsy, Olivessa and Trex each open with module text read straight out: a self-introduction, a résumé (Seger's "date dialogue", p. 193), the history, then the price list. That runs for four to seven boxes with nothing live in the room. The pressure is usually there, just not used. Boy is scratching at the door. Korux has lost his nose. Storn keeps watching the sky. Clyde is dying in the next bed. The briefings never lean on any of it (Martell, "add a weapon", p. 144). The second problem is the party. Their lines are short and often witty, but they drift toward one shared voice: the scout reading tracks. Shade has six track-reading lines and Alerion has four "my ring's cold" lines. Alberica uses a pet name 24 times in seven files, two of them over the dead. The third problem is the owner's "doesn't make sense". About fifteen lines answer something nobody said, or know what the speaker can't know. The worst: Alberica quotes a charter nobody has read yet, the party swears an oath on a branch where no oath was sworn, and the whole White Moose quest plays the day after Shimmer dies without anyone, Shade included, mentioning her.
