# Part 11 · Dialogue review: Chapter 11, The Frost Saint Walks

*Redone against Martell's* Dialogue Secrets *and Seger's dialogue chapter, per `BRIEF.md`. Source: `docs/review/script/11.md` (as played). Line refs are to `game/Data/Scripts/998_FrostThaw/` (`037` = 037_Chapter11.rb, `047c` = 047c_WhiteVault.rb, `047d` = 047d_FixRound.rb).*

## 1. In one paragraph

Chapter 11 has the act's best single exchanges: Alerion's "You don't get her twice.", Sephek's "I'm sorry. I'm taking her home.", and the Council's packing banter, where each Herald answers the last one in their own voice. Sephek is the most consistent voice in the chapter, and his "Why is it still so cold?" picks up his Chapter 3 death line on purpose. The trouble is in the two scenes that carry the most information. Auril's vision at the Hearth is an eleven-box riddle speech (Martell's "unalogue", p. 69) from a goddess the bible says "speaks rarely". Alberica, who is the only one who can move, never answers her. Three Hearts is a briefing in which nobody wants anything different. Lumin reads a riddle, and then everyone agrees in turn ("Then Meat Meat breaks her." / "Then we go and get Frostina back." / "Then we break them first."). Selrae answers questions nobody asked, tells the party what they just saw, and explains Wennen lore with the Wennen priest standing beside her. Most of the owner's "doesn't make sense" lines in this part are in those two scenes, plus one psychic line from Sephek ("smaller than the notes said").

**Note on the White Vault move.** The owner has moved the Vault to the postgame, and the code was changed on 2026-11-05 while this review was written. In Chapter 11 the three sigils now only hum (047c:110-114), and Eirwen has a new hand-off line (037:239). The 11.10 Herald lines below (Alberica's well, Alerion's "Then we know where it is", Wyn's "counting", 047c:130-133) now play after the ending. They are reviewed here because they are still in this scene's code. Line refs are to the files after that edit.

**Lines that don't follow (all found in this part)**

1. 11.01, "MEAT MEAT: Meat Meat is making a thing of it." (037:76). It answers "Don't make a thing of it", which only the Herald-mode quiet option says. (Old row; the rewrite holds up.)
2. 11.01, Auril: "Keys open, Alberica. They also lock." No one answers, and nobody picks it up until 12.09's "She taught me that", which then reads as pointing at nobody. The setup is fine; the payoff needs to name it (see 12.md).
3. 11.03, "SEPHEK: ...Prototype Verdant. You're smaller than the notes said." (047c:160). Sephek has been ice for 400 years and woke five boxes ago. Whose notes? Avarice's lab notes are not his. A psychic character (p. 18).
4. 11.05, "SEPHEK: Not here. Not in her garden. Here I come back." (047c:210). Rootspire isn't Auril's garden, and her Garden of Death is on Solstice, so the line points the wrong way (old row).
5. 11.07, "SELRAE: Solstice. Her island in the Sea of Moving Ice." (037:185). It follows "And she wants me for the third." Nobody asked where, and Selrae wasn't at the Shrine.
6. 11.07, "SELRAE: The Wennen called it the Rewritten Auril." (037:187). The Wennen priest is the one holding the Wennen text. This is the right line in the wrong mouth.
7. 11.07, "REPTIN: Luna Carver told us that. Four hundred years ago. Last month." (037:194). "That" is eight boxes back (old row; the move holds up).
8. 11.07, "SELRAE: (very low) And you saw him get up again." (037:197). "And" follows nothing, she wasn't there, and it tells the party what they just saw (p. 11).
9. 11.07, "REPTIN: ...We know those roots. Pleasantly, even." (037:201). "Pleasantly" doesn't attach to anything.
10. 11.09, "RHEVEN: The Skytower clan owes Kethan a debt." (037:220). It's backwards; Kethan is Skytower (old row).
11. 11.10, "ALBERICA: That's the vault I saw in the well." (047c:130). In Ch 7 the well showed a map, and in 12.06 she says she *dreamed* the door (old row; see §5 for which line to keep).

## 7. Top 5

1. **Cut Auril's vision to five boxes and let Alberica answer once.** Keep "Hear the wheel...", "Keys open... They also lock.", "You told no one...", "You are my vessel, little songbird." and "Come and be beautiful forever.", and add Alberica's "I don't sing for you." That keeps "speaks rarely" and turns the speech into a fight (Martell pp. 17, 69-70).
2. **Rebuild Three Hearts as a disagreement.** Alerion asks who the third is, Lumin owns the Wennen lore, Selrae owns her guilt ("I let them in"), and the recap lines are cut (pp. 11-12, 17).
3. **Fix the lines that don't follow**: Meat Meat's "making a thing of it", Selrae's unasked "Solstice", Reptin's Luna Carver line, "Pleasantly, even" (p. 18; brief §3).
4. **Take the psychic and greedy notes out of Sephek** ("smaller than the notes said", "So would I") so he stays "quietly sorry" all chapter (pp. 18, 29-31).
5. **Stop Alberica borrowing "mushroom"** and keep Wyn's "Nobody's taking Alberica" as a setup that pays off in 13.17 (pp. 35, 48).
